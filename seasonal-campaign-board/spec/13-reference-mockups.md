## Original direction — "The Countdown Wall" (prose sketch)

The board is a wall, not a spreadsheet. Across the top runs the dual-calendar strip — Gregorian months as the spine, Hijri as a quieter second rail beneath each week, moment windows shaded in soft primary tint with a subtle diagonal pattern (color-blind-safe), the active system labeled once, calmly. Campaigns hang as horizontal lanes between moment markers: each lane starts with a countdown tile — a pill-cornered card whose tabular digits sit in reserved-width boxes, the phase name (التمهيد · الذروة · الانحسار) beneath in small caps, the urgency state expressed as icon + word + color, never color alone. Readiness rides the lane's inline-end as a meter with counts spelled out ("12 of 14 · 2 critical open") — percentages are decoration; counts are the truth. When an item completes, its row settles with the single energetic beat the product allows — a soft pop, ≤8% travel — and the meter patches in place. At the moment's wind-down, the lane's closure prompt arrives as a quiet gold-ribboned card, not an alarm: the season ending is a fact, not a fire. Closed lanes slide to an archive rail where their closure notes read like locker-room posters — what shipped, what slipped, one lesson carried forward — and next season's moment card carries a small "from your last season" link, the loop made visible. The crescent motif appears exactly twice in chrome: the drift badge on Hijri-linked moments (a filled crescent with a ±1 note) and the Ramadan guidance card — never as decoration, always as information.

## Mood anchors

A retail war-room wall in late February, eight days before Ramadan: countdown tiles counting, readiness meters filling, one amber item blinking for attention — festive gold at the edges, deep hilal green holding the structure, warm paper behind everything · a coach's clipboard, not a fire alarm · the crescent as a data point, not a logo.
