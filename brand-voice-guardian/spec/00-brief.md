# 00 — Brief: brand-voice-guardian

Family: content_tools · Tier: 3 · Roster status: researched proposal, pending owner confirmation · Seed: none supplied.

## What it is
A brand-voice governance checker for bilingual brand/comms teams: paste or upload copy, run a governance pass, and receive drift findings — brand-glossary violations, banned terms, register drift against the declared MSA register, and terminology inconsistency — each with an actionable fix and an audit-ready report. It checks; it does not write (the studio writes — adjacency boundary recorded in both 12s).

## Buyer
Brand and communications managers (1–8 seats) at MENA brands and agencies who own voice consistency across writers, agencies, and freelancers — Omar-persona types (see arabic-copy-studio 02) who today act as human spell-checkers for the whole organization.

## Surface
Web application: a check surface (paste/upload), findings review, brand profile manager (glossary, banned terms, register declaration), and reports history. Desktop-first; tablet-usable check surface; no mobile app, no API, no browser extension at v1.

## Unique feature
Bilingual governance linting with register drift detection: a single pass that enforces the brand's Arabic register declaration (فصحى بيضاء/وسطى), terminology glossary, and banned-terms list across ar, en, and mixed text — surfaced as a findings review with per-finding accept/fix and an exportable audit trail. Enterprise governance tools (Acrolinx-class) are English-centric; nothing regional lints Arabic register (01).

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)
- No generative rewriting beyond suggested one-word/phrase fixes (writing belongs to the studio family).
- No grammar-only checking (general writing assistants own that).
- No style-guide authoring CMS (documents-as-product out of scope; the profile is structured data).
- No translation (translation-review-hub family).
- No continuous monitoring of external properties at v1 (checks are user-initiated).

## Success signals
A brand manager can: build a brand profile in under 30 minutes; run a governance pass on a live campaign set; get register/terminology findings with fixes; export an audit report — all against the Mock Adapter, zero provider knowledge in UI.
