## Indirect competitors (3)
- **Human brand review** (the Omar-status-quo): highest judgment, doesn't scale — the displacement target.
- **General assistants + a brand PDF**: free, zero enforcement memory, no audit trail.
- **Docs add-on grammar tools**: catch mechanics, miss governance.

## Demand signals
- Brand-voice consistency is an explicit category concern with published playbooks from enterprise vendors [5][3]; governance tooling is consolidating (Acrolinx→Markup [2]) — the wedge for a regional-first linter is open.
- Arabic content quality is a stated brand-performance factor in MENA marketing practice (arabic-copy-studio 01 [5][6]) — the same demand, governance side.
- Enterprise terminology governance is an established discipline [7]; no regional product applies it to Arabic.

[7] https://www.acrolinx.com/blog/why-terminology-and-content-governance-belong-together

## Positioning gaps
1. Register governance (MSA levels) does not exist as a product capability anywhere in the set.
2. Bilingual glossary enforcement (ar+en terms with do-not-use lists) is spreadsheet discipline today.
3. Audit-ready reporting (who checked what, findings, resolutions) is missing from team-tier tools — only enterprise tiers have it.

## Market size (honest)
Brand/comms teams are a narrower buyer pool than writers: hundreds-to-low-thousands of regional teams. At $15–30/seat for a governance utility, the envelope is modest but strategic: it completes the content family loop (write→plan→govern) and monetizes the Omar persona who approves the family's other purchases.
