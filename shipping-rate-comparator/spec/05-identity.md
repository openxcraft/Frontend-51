## Typography (P16 — Scheherazade New + Libre Caslon Text)

- **Arabic: Scheherazade New** (real weights 400/500/600/700 — rtl-rules §3 honored, no synthetic bold): 400 for Arabic body and destination/coverage strings; 500 for zone labels and card metadata; 700 for Arabic headings. The 4-weight book face reads as a typeset tariff page — generous letterforms that survive dense tables without turning decorative. Arabic body ≥15px in tables; line-height ≥1.6 everywhere (token floor); tracking 0 (token-level, rtl-rules §3).
- **Latin: Libre Caslon Text** (real weights 400/700 — a text serif with Regular + Bold only; the 600 step does not exist in this family and is never synthesized): 400 body; 700 for the single H1 per screen, quote totals, and the derivation rules under figures. The Caslon ledger-serif is the arithmetic voice — a rate card is a printed tariff schedule, and the figure emphasis is scale + family + amber-ink, never a third face.
- **Weight strategy (the P16 composite separation): 4-weight Arabic book / 2-weight Latin ledger** — the Arabic side carries the middle voices (500/600), the Latin side is deliberately two-voice with 700 as the figure-forward emphasis. Figures in price cells render at 700 with fixed decimal places and inline-end alignment; Libre Caslon Text's tabular-figure coverage is NOT assumed — column alignment is by right/inline-end alignment + consistent decimals per currency (the rule binds regardless of font features; `tnum` is a best-effort at implementation, recorded honestly rather than as an assumption).
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; data/finance surface per design-language-map §7): prices, weights, divisors, counts, deltas all 0-9 in both locales; Arabic-Indic glyphs never appear in system chrome (storage canonical grams/millimeters/minor-units — display is formatting, rtl-rules §3).
- One pairing for reading AND chrome (T3 discipline; no third family; no microcopy exception — Scheherazade's book letterforms are legible at table density, unlike display naskhs).
- Mixed-script data (Arabic destination names inside LTR chrome, Latin currency codes inside Arabic lines) renders bidi-isolated per rtl-rules §2 — never force-flipped.

## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Density B (standard, 6–10px)**: price-grid cells 6px, cards and dialogs 8px, the comparison results table row 8px, the derivation sheet 10px (the sheet reads as a tariff page, slightly rounder than the controls that shape it). Chips fully-rounded exception ONLY for removable filter/card-selector chips (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here).
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: one diffuse amber-tinted ambient shadow step distinguishes raised surfaces (cards, the breakdown sheet) from the warm-paper canvas; no stacked occlusion, no glass, no blur. The shadow is decorative — structure is carried by border hairlines and the canvas→raised step; nothing may be "elevated into meaning" (state is text+icon, a11y-baseline §4).
- **Icons: line-1.5** (thin editorial outline set, constructed on the grid; Lucide-class weight converges with the ledger): zone glyph = concentric arcs (a zone is a ring of coverage), weight glyph = beam-scale, box glyph = isometric outline cube (the shipment), divisor glyph = ruler-with-slash, surcharge glyph = stacked-line coins, comparison glyph = two offset columns with a flag, batch glyph = three stacked rows, pin glyph = map-pin line, export glyph = tray-with-out-arrow, calculator glyph = four-function grid. Directional glyphs (tray-out arrow, drill chevrons, pagination) flip in RTL; semantically anchored glyphs (scale, cube, ruler, arcs, pin) never flip (rtl-rules §2). No sparkle, wand, or "smart" glyphs anywhere — declared non-AI product (ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: minimal** (motion-language §1): opacity-only transitions ≤120ms; position changes instant with crossfade; route changes hard-cut or ≤120ms crossfade; modals fade+scale 0.98→1.0 ≤120ms with focus trap and return. Money never moves while being read — figures settle, they never count up. **Signature micro-interaction (the one, T3 allowance): the Chargeable-Weight Settle** — when a weight or dimension keystroke lands, the calculator's three figures (actual / volumetric / chargeable) re-settle in a single ≤120ms opacity crossfade; the winning figure takes the amber-deep underline and its derivation line ("35×25×15 cm ÷ 5000 = 2.63 kg → 3.0 kg at the 0.5 kg step"), and the results table rows crossfade to their new totals. The motion does real work: it marks WHICH weight won the billing decision — the exact fact DIM-surprised merchants missed (01). Reduced-motion: instant swap + identical polite announcement ("الوزن القابل للشحن 3.0 كجم — حجمي" / "Chargeable weight 3.0 kg — volumetric"); perceivability by construction (motion-language §5). Success moments (card activated, comparison ready, export ready) are state chips + polite announcements — no animation-carried meaning (a11y-baseline §4).
