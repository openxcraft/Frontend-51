# 00 — Brief: policy-acknowledgment-portal

Family: business_ops · Tier: 3 · Roster status: researched proposal, pending owner confirmation · Seed: none supplied.

## What it is

A bilingual policy acknowledgment portal for compliance admins at MENA organizations: one authoritative register of the policies a workforce is bound to, each policy carrying immutable, versioned documents in both English and Arabic, and an attestation ledger that records — append-only, with timestamps and version pins — exactly who acknowledged which version of which policy, when, in which locale, and against which attestation text. The product's center of gravity is not policy storage and it is emphatically not policy drafting: the truth begins when a finished policy document exists (uploaded or pasted, stored whole and read-only), passes through a document-control publish step (version label, effective date, change summary, supersedes chain), and lands in the workflow the category already recognizes as its core grammar — who, when, which version (research digest source [1]) — extended with the bilingual dimension no competitor in our research corpus treats as a first-class schema: per-locale document bodies, per-locale read tracking, and a per-policy governing-locale rule that mirrors the legal reality of the region (in Saudi Arabia, workplace policies must be written in Arabic even when bilingual — Labor Law Article 9 per sources [33][34]; in the UAE the handbook language is not prescribed but MoHRE communications run in Arabic [35]).

The unique feature is **versioned bilingual acknowledgments with an attestation audit trail**, as three interlocking commitments. First, *versioned*: policies follow a document-control grammar practitioners already run by hand (draft labels 0.x, published 1.0+, dates and status in the version record — sources [44]–[51]); an acknowledgment is permanently pinned to the exact policy version and attestation text the person saw, so evidence never silently drifts when a policy is superseded. Second, *bilingual*: each published version carries an English body and an Arabic body, each side human-authored (the product never machine-fills a pair — the same no-fabrication stance the portfolio applies to every bilingual surface), with the governing locale declared per policy and unpaired sides surfacing honest warnings; the employee reader surface renders either locale at its own direction and the acknowledgment records which locale(s) the person actually read and attested against. Third, *attestation audit trail*: every consequential event — publish, campaign launch, view, acknowledgment, decline, reminder, export — appends to a ledger that is the product's accountability surface and its export deliverable, shaped for the auditors who demand exactly this evidence (SOC 2 examiners review whether policies are documented, formally reviewed, and accepted by employees — source [27]; Saudi NCA ECC pushes organizations toward evidence-based assurance — sources [40][42]). The AI stance is **declared non-AI** (ai-native-patterns.md §1): a deterministic attestation ledger, zero AI operations on the adapter, no assist affordances anywhere in the UI — the product's honesty is that evidence is arithmetic, not inference.

The buyer's reality (01 evidence): at a multi-site Gulf trading group, retail chain, or logistics operator, the policy program lives in a shared drive plus a spreadsheet someone maintains by memory — the SharePoint "auditing views" hack that practitioners still recommend to prove who opened a document (source [23]) is the incumbent's best effort. The category's purpose-built tools are US-shaped and priced for public safety and enterprise GRC (PowerDMS-class, $4,000–$100,000+/yr — source [9], unverified estimate; NAVEX/ConvergePoint/ComplianceBridge quote-gated); HRIS policy modules (Rippling/Gusto/BambooHR-class) ride per-employee payroll pricing and treat acknowledgment as an HCM checkbox, not an evidence artifact; and none of them, in this research corpus, treats the bilingual Arabic/English policy — the object Gulf employers are legally required to maintain — as a first-class schema with per-locale attestation semantics. This product is the compliance admin's instrument: version first, attestation as first-class data, both languages as first-class fields, the ledger as the default grammar.

## Buyer

Compliance administrators and small governance/risk/compliance teams at MENA businesses with 50–2,000 employees across multiple sites: retail groups, trading and distribution companies, logistics and facilities operators, clinic and school groups, and growing tech companies selling into regulated buyers. The economic buyer is the compliance admin or governance lead who answers when an auditor, a certification body, or a customer's security review asks "prove your staff acknowledged the current information security policy"; daily users are the admin who publishes versions and runs campaigns, HR operations who feed joins and leavers, and — critically — every employee and contractor who must read and attest, including frontline staff without corporate email. Per-seat pricing for admin seats; acknowledger seats free (the evidence is only complete when attesting costs nothing — 12 Q-3). Full personas in 02-user-research.md.

## Surface

Web application with two faces. The **admin console** is desktop-first (≥1280px primary): policies register, policy detail with version timeline and bilingual bodies, version publish flow, campaign composer, acknowledgment coverage console, attestation ledger, people & segments directory, jobs console, settings. The **acknowledger portal** (the employee face) is mobile-first and desktop-capable: a policy inbox of pending acknowledgments and a reader surface with per-locale toggle, read tracking, and the attestation gate; reachable via session link so a frontline worker with a phone and no corporate email can complete an acknowledgment in minutes (delivery channels are abstract needs with manual alternatives — 08). No mobile app; no public API at v1; no browser extension.

## Unique feature

Versioned bilingual acknowledgments with an attestation audit trail. Defensible on three grounds (01): (1) version pinning is the category's own definition of the artifact (who/when/which version — source [1]) but the incumbents bolt it onto mono-locale documents, while bilingual Gulf policy programs need per-locale bodies, per-locale read evidence, and a governing-locale rule that survives an audit in Riyadh and a security review in Dubai; (2) the attestation ledger is a deterministic, append-only evidence structure — the same input state always yields the same coverage arithmetic and the same ledger facts — which is precisely the property audit evidence requires and which HRIS checkbox features and shared-drive hacks do not provide; (3) the reach path for frontline, email-less workers (link-based acknowledgment with per-site chase lists) is designed in rather than bolted on, because a coverage percentage that quietly excludes the warehouse is not evidence, it is a liability.

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)

- **No training/LMS** — no courses, curricula, comprehension quizzes, pass/fail scoring, or completion certificates. Acknowledgment proves receipt of a specific version; training proves they were taught the subject (source [24]) — the two are different products, and the microlearning upsell the category leader runs (source [11]) is deliberately out of scope. A future comprehension feature would be a LOOP-4 declaration, not v1 surface.
- **No policy authoring** — no drafting, redlining, clause libraries, templates, review-and-approval authoring workflow, or AI generation of policy text. Policy content arrives as finished documents (upload or paste) and is stored whole and read-only; a new version is a new document, never an edit of an old one. The portal begins after the policy exists (the same boundary contract-lifecycle-manager draws for contracts).
- **No incident management** — no violation reports, investigations, disciplinary workflows, or case management. A declined acknowledgment is recorded and surfaced as a fact for the admin to handle outside the product; what happens next is HR's and legal's business, not the ledger's.
- No e-signature platform at v1 — the attestation is a typed-name confirmation with a deterministic digest, recorded in the ledger; a wet-ink or dedicated e-signature channel is an abstract execution consideration, not a hosted signing flow (mirrors the house posture set by contract-lifecycle-manager's 00).
- No policy-analytics beyond coverage arithmetic — no reading-behavior profiling, no per-person risk scoring, no engagement metrics. Counts and timestamps only; the ledger reports facts, never judgments.
- No workflow automation beyond reminders and derived zones — no escalations that notify managers-of-managers, no auto-escalation to discipline, no SLA breach workflows (the reminder cadence is a notification schedule, not an enforcement mechanism).

## Success signals (Mock-Adapter Definition-of-Done)

A compliance admin can, in one first session on the Mock Adapter with zero provider-specific knowledge in the UI: create a policy record with bilingual titles and a governing locale; publish a version with both bodies (paste path), a document-control label, effective date, and change summary, superseding the prior version; create a segment and import people (CSV path with validation); launch a campaign on the new version with a due window, reminder cadence, and bilingual attestation text; open the coverage console and read the acknowledged/pending/overdue/declined arithmetic; drill into one person's full acknowledgment history with per-locale read evidence and the attestation digest; walk the employee path — open the acknowledger portal, read the Arabic body, attest with typed name, and watch the ledger entry appear; record a decline and see it surfaced honestly; export an evidence pack for a campaign with per-person attestation records before artifact expiry. The full loop must complete with every abstract capability unbound: pasted bodies, CSV import, manual chase list, direct-download exports (LOOP-5 bar) — and the coverage arithmetic must be identical on every replay (determinism is the product's name).
