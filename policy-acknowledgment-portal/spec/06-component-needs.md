## Layer-3 token additions

`policy.row.state.*` (coverage chips per 05 contracts) · `version.timeline.node.{draft|published|superseded|withdrawn}.*` · `body.panel.locale.*` (per-side direction + governing mark) · `doccontrol.form.label-grammar.*` (validation states) · `campaign.composer.membership-count.*` · `coverage.zone.{acknowledged|pending|overdue|declined}.*` (tint + icon + text triads) · `acknowledgment.card.digest.*` (tnum figures) · `ledger.entry.kind.*` · `reader.surface.heading-nav.*` · `attestation.gate.{confirm|decline|ledger-line}.*` — all Layer 2 re-binds of the 05 palette; no raw values anywhere (token-architecture §1); the Ledger Line's motion binds to `motion.duration.feedback` at the minimal tier ceiling (≤120ms, opacity + width only).

## Icon-flip decisions (RTL — rtl-rules §2)

Mirrored (directional): version-timeline spine direction and node chevrons; the Ledger Line's writing axis (inline-start → inline-end); pagination and outline-panel disclosure arrows; campaign due-window countdown arrow. Never mirrored (semantically anchored): shield (policy category), ledger glyph (three stacked rules — vertical, direction-free), check/clock/minus status marks, upload/download, magnifier, the governing-locale mark (text + icon anchor). Recorded per rtl-rules §2's decision-log obligation.

## Reused-with-twist

- **DiffView**: version-to-version compare at the change-summary + body-provenance level (which locale bodies changed, digest delta) — not character diffs of policy text (the bodies are read-only evidence; the change summary is the authored diff).
- **EmptyState**: S1 first-run offers the seeded sample policy + campaign (interactive: the P22 value walk — publish → assign → attest → read coverage — before real data lands).
- **QuotaMeter**: dispatch batches and evidence-pack exports only; the ledger and coverage surfaces deliberately render NO meter (evidence is never rationed — 01 pricing posture; same reasoning as seo-brief-builder's unmetered deterministic runs).
