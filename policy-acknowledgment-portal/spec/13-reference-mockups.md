## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

**The Denim Ledger.** The register renders as a ruled compliance ledger over cool paper: one continuous Ittibaq Denim-ruled table, hairline borders the only elevation, bilingual titles each sitting at its own direction inside their cells so the mixed-script rows never force the ambient flow. A policy opens into its **version timeline** — a vertical denim spine with border-weight steps, each node carrying label, effective date, and status in tabular figures, superseded links drawn as plain ruled connections because the chain IS the history; withdrawals hang as grey-ruled entries with their reason beside them, never erased. The **coverage console** refuses every dashboard instinct the category runs: arithmetic as text first — "28 assigned · 19 acknowledged · 1 declined · 8 overdue · 0 pending — 67%" — with derived zones as filterable ruled rosters, state carried by icon + word + count triads in green, amber, plum, red, never by heat. The **reader surface** is where the document becomes the hero: 700-weight headings in IBM Plex Sans Arabic at ≥1.7 line-height, the locale toggle with the governing-locale steel mark beside the side the law load-balances on, read evidence recorded as quiet locale-open states beside the toggle. The **attestation gate** sits after the body in focus order and in geometry — a typed-name confirm, a first-class Decline peer, and on success the product's single motion: the **Ledger Line**, one hairline writing itself along the reading axis in ≤120ms while the fact settles beside it — "v2.3 · 21 Oct 2026, 14:03 — recorded in the ledger." The **attestation ledger view** is the accountability spine made visible: actor, action kind (the constructed three-rules glyph on attestation entries), target, timestamp, filterable and keyboard-navigable and exportable — an instrument that looks like what it is: evidence, ruled in denim, priced for the team the category forgot.
