## Indirect competitors (3)
- **Spreadsheets + chat groups** (the actual regional status quo): free, flexible, zero parity control — the behavior to displace.
- **Agency project tools** (work-management generic): manage tasks, not language-variant publication slots.
- **Native channel schedulers** (in-platform scheduling): free but single-channel, mono-locale.

## Positioning gaps
1. **Dual-calendar blindness**: every competitor is Gregorian-only; regional teams maintain Hijri overlays manually → dual-calendar grid with method disclosure is the headline.
2. **Variant parity is manual**: paired ar/en assets live as separate cards with no linkage → slot-level variant pairing with parity status (planned/one-missing/draft-diverged).
3. **Seasonal intelligence absent**: Ramadan timing patterns (iftar/suhoor), quiet hours, and moments library exist only in agency tribal knowledge → season mode.
4. **Planning/execution bundling forces price bloat**: teams that already publish elsewhere pay for distribution they don't use → planning-only pricing posture.
