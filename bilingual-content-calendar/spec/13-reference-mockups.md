   Take: crisp-motion discipline on dense grids; keyboard navigation quality bar for grid surfaces (our 09 keyboard map's ambition level). Not taken: neutral cold identity (ours is spring/lantern, D-radius warmth).

4. **Airtable calendar views** — https://www.airtable.com
   Take: the flexibility bar teams expect (custom fields on slots, saved views P08) and the honest lesson: flexibility without opinion becomes a spreadsheet — our moments/season intelligence is the opinion layer.

## Original proposals

- **Moment Placement ripple** (signature micro-interaction, T2 obligation): dropping/placing a Ramadan moment chips the horizon and its timing pattern ripples across the affected week at 160ms stagger (≤5 elements), showing planners what the week becomes before any slot exists. Reduced-motion: pattern bands appear instantly with a polite summary announcement. A11y: placement is equally doable via moments panel + apply (never drag-dependent).
- **Dual-header breathing**: the Hijri/Gregorian header cross-references on focus with a subtle secondary-date reveal (not hover-only — 09), teaching the dual system without a tutorial. This is the product's quietest differentiator and deserves its own craft pass.
- **Season mode reshape** (described in 05 motion): the grid re-composes around iftar/suhoor windows as banded day segments — a layout change, not a filter, so planners feel the season rather than read it.
