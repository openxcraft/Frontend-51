## Persona 1 — Abdulaziz, Family-Waqf Mutawlli (primary · owner seat)

**Role**: retired bank branch manager, 61; mutawlli of "وقف آل الراشد" — the endowment his grandfather founded in 2000 (deed WQR-1187-1421). Administers 3 assets (two rental properties, one cash placement) for 10 beneficiaries: 8 family descendants, a Quran-memorization society, and the deed's charitable end-designation (the poor and needy). Answers to the family once a year at the annual meeting; answers to his own conscience monthly.
**Context**: Arabic-first (MSA, comfortable formal register; his English is read-mostly). Runs the waqf today in one spreadsheet workbook plus a messaging thread with the property manager; the deed itself is a certified paper copy he keeps in a fire box. Every Eid and every family dispute, he rebuilds "who is owed what" by hand.
**Goals**: one authoritative register of the endowment as the deed states it; a distribution he can defend line by line when his cousin challenges a number; a yearly report he can hand out at the family meeting instead of presenting a spreadsheet on a laptop; a record that outlives him — "an endowment's records must outlive its administrator" is his own stated fear, and this package's first invariant (07a).
**Frustrations**: the spreadsheet's silent edits (his own typos included — there is no history, only the current state); share arithmetic done by hand against fractions like 3/24; the succession question he is avoiding (one beneficiary deceased since August, successor registration pending — the 07c B-6 fixture is his real situation); being his own auditor with no trail to audit.
**JTBD**: "When the month closes, I want the period's income recorded and the distribution computed from the deed's own shares — so I stop re-deriving who is owed what by hand, and the annual meeting gets a document instead of a performance."
**Triggers**: a cousin's challenge to a distribution number he cannot reconstruct; the annual meeting deadline; realizing his spreadsheet has no record of WHY an amount differed one month; the succession letter he has not yet answered.
**Objections + responses**: "another system my grandchildren will have to learn" → the ledger is append-only and reads like a book — the register is legible to a non-technical heir precisely because it never edits itself; "will it replace the deed?" → the deed summary is metadata recorded from the paper deed; the product applies the recorded policy, it never rules on it (the sharia honesty note, 00/11); "I don't want a subscription for a family duty" → one administrator seat, viewer seats free (12 Q-3), recording never metered.
**Onboarding motive**: register the real endowment, record October's rent, run one allocation, seal one report — the value checklist (P22); the seeded sample endowment lets him watch the seal settle before he types his own deed (07c W-S).
**Verbatim (paraphrase, persona voice)**: "The waqf is a trust with my name on it. I want the numbers to be the deed's numbers, not my typing."

## Persona 2 — Muna, Foundation Operations Administrator + Beneficiary-Relations Officer (primary · administrator seat)

**Role**: operations administrator at "مؤسسة الأوقاف الراشدية" (Al-Rashidiah Awqaf Foundation), a private awqaf foundation; 32 endowments under administration, 4 administrative seats. Muna's day is the daily loop (03 CP-2): recording inflows and expenses as evidence arrives, running monthly allocations, recording deliveries — and she is also the foundation's **beneficiary-relations officer**: the person a beneficiary calls, the person who prepares statements when someone asks "where is my share?", the person who attends the annual beneficiary meeting with print bundles.
**Context**: bilingual by profession (Arabic-native, working English for the auditor and one English-side board member); lives in the ledger tab, the distribution studio, and the reports surface; her queue is evidence first, arithmetic second, questions third.
**Goals**: a ledger she never has to apologize for — no silent edits, corrections that append with reasons; allocations she can hold and adjust with a recorded justification instead of re-running everything; delivery tracking that survives "did Salman actually get October?"; the quarterly board report generated from the ledger state instead of copy-pasted under deadline (the 01 pain, verbatim category evidence [9]).
**Frustrations**: the accounting tool's chart of accounts has no concept of a deed share; the transparency report is assembled from three exports and one long night; when a beneficiary disputes a year-old distribution, the evidence lives in email; when the auditor asks for the distribution trail, "trail" means a storyboard she builds by hand.
**JTBD**: "When a period closes across my endowments, I want each allocation computed from recorded entries and pinned policy, each hold and adjustment recorded with its reason, and each report sealed from ledger state — so the board pack, the auditor request, and the beneficiary call all answer from the same book."
**Triggers**: the board treasurer asking why the administration ratio moved; an auditor request for one endowment's full distribution trail; a beneficiary's "where is my share?" call; a deed-conditioned edge (a student beneficiary's deed-conditioned increase — 03 CP-3.4) that the spreadsheet handles by silent exception.
**Objections + responses**: "we already have accounting software" → it models a business, not a deed: no shares, no seal, no beneficiary statement (01); "the board likes dashboards" → the report IS the dashboard, sealed and pinned — ratios with their arithmetic shown, not a live view that recomputes (00); "importing 30 endowments of history is a project" → the batch import lands valid rows and names the invalid ones (CP-2.4, partial policy) — the foundation starts with this year and imports backward per endowment.
**Onboarding motive**: import one endowment's monthly inflow rows with one malformed row visibly named-and-skipped — the honesty of the partial result is the demo (07c).
**Verbatim (paraphrase, persona voice)**: "If the ledger is right, every question has an answer. My whole job is making the ledger right."

## Persona 3 — Dr. Yaqub, Board Treasurer / Trustee (secondary · viewer seat)

**Role**: semi-retired professor of finance; treasurer of the foundation's supervisory board (and, in the family-waqf shape, the senior trustee relatives look to). Reads every transparency report before the board meeting; asks the ratio questions; signs off.
**Context**: bilingual reader, English-preferring for finance vocabulary, Arabic for the deed's terms; opens the product quarterly, not daily — the report surface is his product (04: Reports earned their own rail section for exactly him).
**Goals**: a report whose numbers trace to entries he can walk (every figure a ledger walk, ratios with the division shown — 03 CP-4.2); the variance section that explains the movement he must explain to the board; confidence that what he read in October is what the auditor reads in November — which is the seal's whole point (02 → 07a: sealed = immutable snapshot pinned to ledger state).
**Frustrations**: live dashboards that change between his read and the meeting; administration ratios he cannot decompose; "reports" that are spreadsheets with a header pasted on; asking for a beneficiary class breakdown and receiving a manual pivot.
**JTBD**: "When I review an endowment's period, I want a sealed report whose ratios, variances, and remainder destination are derived from a pinned ledger state — so my board questions and the auditor's questions land on the same immutable numbers."
**Triggers**: a variance he cannot explain from the report alone; a dispute between the administrator's summary and the auditor's copy; succession events (a trustee's death) that make him ask which records survive.
**Objections + responses**: "I only read, I don't need an account" → the viewer seat is free and read-only by contract (07b: PERMISSION on any mutation — his seat cannot corrupt what it audits); "why can't it just email me?" → report delivery is an abstract channel with download as the always-available path (08 N3 — LOOP-5); "dashboards were real-time" → real-time recomputes; a trust artifact is pinned — that is the upgrade, not the regression.
**Onboarding motive**: read the sealed sample report for the sample endowment — the pin line ("pinned to seq 214") and the ratio arithmetic visible without a single click of setup.
**Verbatim (paraphrase, persona voice)**: "Show me the number and the road it took. A ratio without its road is an opinion."

## Persona 4 — Yasmin, External Auditor (secondary · viewer seat, export access)

**Role**: audit senior at a regional firm; leads the annual endowment engagements for two awqaf foundations and one family waqf (engaged directly by the mutawlli after a beneficiary dispute — the WLF-shaped pressure 01 documents in India [7]). Works in English-first LTR sessions (09: auditor-facing locale flip is a designed path, 05).
**Context**: reads ledgers for a living; wants the trail, the evidence references, and the export artifacts; her engagement letters say "obtain the distribution ledger and supporting evidence" — today that means PDFs and screenshots.
**Goals**: a ledger she can walk end to end (per-entry seq, reversal-not-edit, evidence notes); export artifacts that arrive before expiry and regenerate deterministically when they do not; the beneficiary statement as a pre-built analytical artifact instead of something she reconstructs; zero mutation surface anywhere (her seat's integrity depends on it — 07b rule set).
**Frustrations**: records that were edited after the fact with no mark; evidence that lives in a drawer; asking "what happened in October" and receiving three different October stories from three different exports.
**JTBD**: "When I audit an endowment's period, I want an append-only ledger with evidence references and sealed reports pinned to sequence numbers — so my walkthrough is a walk, not an archaeology."
**Triggers**: engagement planning; a beneficiary dispute that escalates to a request for formal records; the moment she finds a number that only exists in a summary.
**Objections + responses**: "our workpapers live in our own tool" → ledger CSV and print-bundle exports feed her toolchain (07b exportReport formats); "is this system the system of record?" → the ledger is append-only by grammar, the seal is one-way by state machine, and the determinism contract means her re-derivation must match (07c rule 5); "can I trust an in-browser register?" → her seat is read-and-export only; every mutation she could attempt is a typed PERMISSION refusal (11).
**Onboarding motive**: pull the sample endowment's sealed report export and the ledger CSV, and reconcile one distribution line back to its inflow entries — the audit walkthrough in ten minutes.
**Verbatim (paraphrase, persona voice)**: "I don't need clever. I need the book to be the book."

## Voice derivation (SME-J — binds 11)

**The registrar of a trust**: plain, unhurried, states amounts and actors by name, never dramatizes a number, never blames a person (copy-voice §1/§6 — warmth lives in success and empty states only). Errors follow [what]+[why]+[next]. Numbers are facts, not events: no count-ups, no exclamation of sums (the stately register, 05). English is professional-neutral, contractions off (specialized family, copy-voice §4). Arabic is فصحى بيضاء (plain MSA), unvocalized, softened imperatives with لطفاً sparingly.

**Terminology lock (one concept, one term, everywhere — 07a entities + this register)**: الوقف=endowment · الصك=deed (صك الوقف) · الواقف=donor · المتولي=administrator · الأصل=asset (corpus) · المستفيد=beneficiary · الحصة=share · سياسة التوزيع=distribution policy · سجل التوزيع=distribution ledger · القيد=entry (ledger entry) · إيراد=inflow · المصروف=expense · المصاريف الإدارية=administration expenses · الاحتياطي=reserve · القيد العكسي=reversal entry · التوزيع=distribution · التسليم=delivery · تقرير الشفافية=transparency report · كشف المستفيد=beneficiary statement · الختم=seal · خُتم=sealed (state) · المتبقي=remainder (حصة المتبقي=the remainder line) · الفترة=period · النسبة=ratio · سجل النشاط=activity log · الاستيراد=import · التصدير=export · سقف الإدارة=administration cap. Synonym drift (مهمة/عمل-class mixing) is a defect per copy-voice §3 — "التوزيع" is never loosely traded for "الصرف" in chrome.

**Numeral register (ledger row, 05)**: Western numerals in every data surface and every utility string both directions — amounts, shares, ratios, seq, dates, counts-in-progress; AR-indic (٠-٩) renders ONLY in Arabic prose panels when the workspace policy enables it (deed-purpose prose, authored evidence-note prose), never in tables, never mid-sentence mixed (copy-voice §1.7; 07a NumeralsPolicy).

**Bilingual authoring posture**: identity-bearing fields are two labeled inputs, authored both sides, never machine-filled (09 forms rule; 07a BilingualText contract) — the product's copy treats a one-script deed field as an error state to name, not a default to accept (11 C).
