# 00 — Brief: contract-lifecycle-manager

Family: business_ops · Tier: 3 · Roster status: researched proposal, pending owner confirmation · Seed: none supplied.

## What it is

A bilingual contract vault with obligation-tracking workflow for small legal teams in MENA: one authoritative register of the agreements a company is party to — each contract a record carrying its document (uploaded, versioned), its metadata (counterparty, type, value, currency, dates, notice window, auto-renewal, owner), and its obligations (who owes what, when, with reminders that actually compute) — plus a lightweight workflow that carries a contract from intake request through legal review to a recorded execution, and an append-only activity log that makes the whole register auditable. The vault is table-first: a virtualized register grid, a contract detail surface built around a bilingual metadata panel, an obligations-and-reminders console driven by derived urgency zones, and a counterparties directory. This is deliberately a *vault + workflow*, not a drafting suite: the product's truth begins when a document exists; it ends at renewal, termination, and audit export.

The unique feature is **bilingual contract metadata with obligation-extraction reminders**. In Gulf practice the same agreement routinely lives in two scripts — an English governing text whose counterparty is filed under an Arabic commercial-registration name, a lease whose Arabic title is what the team actually calls it — so every identity-bearing field (contract title, counterparty legal name, obligation summary) is authored in both locales, rendered per-side direction, and searchable through Arabic-aware normalization (rtl-rules §4). Obligations are the second axis: renewal-notice windows, expiry dates, payment cadences, insurance certificates, deliverable milestones — each a structured record with an owner, a due date (one-time or recurring), and a derived reminder schedule computed from workspace-default lead times (30/7 days, overridable per obligation). The AI stance is **conditionally-AI** (ai-native-patterns §1): an obligation-extraction **assist** may propose metadata and obligation drafts from an uploaded document when the capability is bound and consented; every suggestion is draft-by-default until a human accepts, edits, or dismisses it (P15); and when the assist is unbound, exhausted, or refused, the product degrades to fully manual entry — the hand-entry wizard with the top-ten field checklist practitioners already run by hand (research [19][21]) — while the deterministic reminder engine keeps firing unchanged. Extraction is an accelerant on top of a workflow that must work without it; the reminders are the product's spine and are never AI-dependent.

The buyer's reality (01 evidence): the status quo at a 2–5 seat legal team is a shared-drive folder plus a tracker spreadsheet that someone maintains by memory — the Reddit-shaped micro-team (2 attorneys + 1 paralegal, contracts in folders [2]) and the missed-auto-renewal war stories are category canon ([23][24][27]). Category CLMs solve this at enterprise shape and price ([9][14][15]); regional Arabic tools are template-and-e-signature-shaped ([35]–[38]); and the bilingual metadata problem — the thing that makes a regional vault hard to keep honest — is handled by staffing bilingual analysts rather than by software ([30]–[33]). This product is the small GC team's instrument: register first, obligations as first-class data, both languages as first-class fields, audit trail as the default grammar.

## Buyer

Legal operations and small general-counsel teams at MENA businesses running 50–1,500 live contracts: GC-led departments of 1–5 legal seats at trading groups, contracting companies, clinics and school groups, real-estate and facilities operators, growing tech companies. The economic buyer is the GC or legal-ops lead who answers when a renewal window is missed or an auditor asks for the register; daily users are counsel and paralegals who intake and abstract contracts, plus business-side contract owners (procurement, ops, department heads) who hold obligations and need viewer access without a paid seat. Per-seat pricing for the legal seats; viewer seats free (12 Q-3). Full personas in 02-user-research.md.

## Surface

Web application, desktop-first (≥1280px primary: register grid, contract detail, obligations console, counterparties directory), responsive to 360px in review and complete-an-obligation modes. Zones: vault home (register + renewal/obligation digest), contract detail (bilingual metadata panel, document versions, obligation ledger, review track, activity), obligations & reminders console (derived schedule, urgency zones), intake wizard (create + upload + optional extraction), counterparties, activity log, jobs console, settings. ⌘K command palette (P05 — the register exceeds three list surfaces). No mobile app; no public API at v1.

## Unique feature

Bilingual contract metadata with obligation-extraction reminders, as two interlocking commitments: (1) every metadata and obligation field is bilingual by construction — EN + AR rendered per-side direction, either editable, both searchable, no auto-translation ever fabricating the other side; (2) obligations are structured records producing a computed reminder schedule (lead-time defaults, per-obligation overrides, renewal-notice deadlines derived from the contract's own window), not calendar notes. Defensible on three grounds (01): no verified competitor in the category treats bilingual ar/en metadata as a first-class schema rather than a locale setting; obligation tracking exists as enterprise CLM modules and analyst staffing, not as the affordable spine of a small-team vault; and the reminder engine's determinism (same obligation state → same schedule, same zones, same deadlines) is exactly what an audit-facing artifact requires.

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)

- **No e-signature at v1** — signature capture is an abstract execution channel (08 N2): the vault records that execution happened (date, channel, executed copy uploaded) but never hosts the signing flow itself; the manual alternative (sign outside, upload the executed copy, record execution) is the primary path.
- **No redlining editor** — no clause-level editing, commenting, or track-changes on contract text; the document is stored as uploaded bytes, versioned whole. Negotiation tooling belongs to the drafting class we are not.
- **No clause libraries, template authoring, or playbook management** — no clause repositories, no contract templates, no fallback-standard guidance. Drafting support is out; the vault begins post-negotiation.
- No contract drafting/generation of any kind (follows from the above; the intake wizard captures metadata about an agreement, never drafts one).
- No counterparty risk scoring, credit checks, or sanctions screening (a registry lookup capability is abstract and optional — 08 N4 — and returns identifiers only, never risk judgments).
- No financial posting — values are register metadata for triage ([19]'s rank-by-value advice); accounting systems own the money.
- No obligation enforcement/payment execution — the product records, reminds, and evidences; it never pays, blocks, or integrates with payment rails.

## Success signals (Mock-Adapter Definition-of-Done)

A GC-team user can, in one first session on the Mock Adapter with zero provider-specific knowledge in the UI: create a counterparty with bilingual names; intake one contract with its document, bilingual metadata (title, value, dates, notice window, auto-renew flag, owner); add an obligation by hand (the always-available path) and watch the derived reminder schedule appear with correct zones; run the extraction assist on the uploaded document, review its suggestions, accept one, edit one, and dismiss the planted false positive (P15 gate exercised); complete an obligation with evidence and see the overdue zone clear; walk a contract through review → awaiting-execution → record execution (manual path) → active; read the activity log entry for every mutation above; and export the register with obligations appendix before artifact expiry. The full loop must complete with the extraction assist unbound and every abstract capability off: manual metadata entry, manual obligation entry, deterministic reminders, manual execution recording, direct-download exports (LOOP-5 bar).
