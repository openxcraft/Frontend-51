## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Vault Desk: a slate chamber where contracts rest as paper. The register is a ledger grid on cool canvas (#F2F5F9); each contract row opens into a detail surface that rises one stratum — a raised panel (#FCFEFF) on which the contract itself sits as a warm paper card (#FAF8F4), the uploaded document rendered like the physical artifact it is. The spine of the detail surface is the **bilingual metadata panel**: paired EN/AR fields as two ruled columns meeting at a center hairline, each field rendering in its own direction, the pair never merged — when one side is missing, the empty field states it in text ("Arabic title not yet recorded") rather than pretending symmetry. Urgency lives only where it is earned: the obligations console groups by zone with counts as printed arithmetic ("1 متأخر 20 يوماً"), the renewal window bar states its three dates and its arithmetic in plain figures (term end, window, deadline), and nothing on any surface pulses. The extraction assist is visually silent by default: a thin scan-line mark and a written proposal with a confidence figure — accept, edit, or dismiss — because a vault does not sparkle; it records. The one stately moment is **the Seal Settles**: when execution is recorded, the circular slate seal drops once onto the contract header (360ms, decelerate), the four core fields stagger beneath it, and the paper stratum occludes a step — a contract has taken its place in the chamber, and the announcement says exactly that, with the date, the channel, and the count of obligations now scheduled.

## Mood anchors

A registrar's chamber: slate ink, ruled columns, paper that means something · two scripts meeting at a hairline center rail, each keeping its own direction · urgency as printed arithmetic, never as alarm · one weighted seal-drop when a covenant is executed — then the ledger continues.
