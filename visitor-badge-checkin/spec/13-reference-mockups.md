## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

**The Sky Ledger lobby.** A cold sky-teal over warm paper: the kiosk is one calm column of questions — Almarai at arm's length, the visitor's own name in 800-weight at the confirmation moment settling once like a stamp on a ledger line — while the desk is a ruled day-sheet: the today board's three zones (expected · in-building · no-show) as border-weight columns on paper, counts printed as text at every column head, durations ticking in plain numerals. The badge is the only "deep" object on any screen and it earns it honestly: a flat printed artifact preview — band strip, deep-variant type, the code pinned LTR in tabular figures — rendered from the spec as a semantic list first and a picture second, so the screen-reader meets the same badge the printer does. The signature surface is the **correction chain read**: a voided badge and its replacement shown as two linked cards on the visit timeline, the reason printed between them like a correction line in an accounts book — the product's whole honesty in one glance. Motion is minimal by ledger: nothing moves except opacity; the arrival moment's single settle and the host-chip state flip happen in the same frame, and under reduced motion they are announcements with the same words.
