## Curated references

1. **RetailNext — Store Performance Management**
   https://www.retailnext.net/ and https://www.retailnext.net/solutions/store-operations
   Take: the operations-buyer framing ("dozens or hundreds of locations simultaneously"; identify underperformers, benchmark best practices) and the normalization instinct ("traffic-normalized conversion metrics reveal what raw sales figures hide") — the exact argument our per-open-hour and per-labor-hour normalization generalizes to shift classes. The cross-store visibility posture (see every store at once, act before numbers slip) is the S2 board's register. Not taken: the sensor-hardware dependency, the enterprise program posture, and the AI querying affordance (our product is declared non-AI with zero model affordances); their conversion metric requires their traffic stack — ours treats traffic as optional imported data.

2. **KORONA POS — Retail KPIs guide**
   https://koronapos.com/blog/retail-metrics-kpis/
   Take: the practitioner KPI canon and its four-bucket grouping (sales performance / customer experience / inventory / operational efficiency) — our scorecard's KPI set is drawn from this published list, which makes the definitions surface familiar to exactly the analyst who will audit it (02 Khalid). Not taken: the POS-suite scope around it (registers, payments, inventory management) and the flat period-reporting posture — KPIs without a comparison methodology is the gap we occupy.

3. **Lightspeed Retail — Pricing page**
   https://www.lightspeedhq.com/pos/retail/pricing/
   Take: the tiered per-register ladder ($89/$149/$289, advanced reporting gated upward, multi-location custom-quote) as the pricing anchor and the anti-model: pricing the analysis seat off a register bill is what our per-seat posture refuses (12 Q-18). Not taken: the product itself — period-over-period reporting inside a POS is the incumbent our comp policy and week-matched basis replace.

4. **Klipfolio — Spreadsheet-power dashboards**
   https://www.klipfolio.com/
   Take: the honest positioning against the spreadsheet incumbent ("using Excel as the backbone of your reporting process is unnecessary heavy lifting") — our competitor is the pivot table, and the messaging register that respects the analyst's spreadsheet mastery while selling the governed method is the voice we adopt (02). Not taken: the dashboard-of-charts posture — a comparison instrument is not a metrics wall; and the connector sprawl (200+ services) that would blur the product's boundary.

## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

**The Citrine Ledger-board.** The scorecard reads like a market scoreboard, not a bank terminal: warm paper canvas, a ranked board whose rows are soft-lift plates, and on each plate the KPI figures set in Bebas Neue signage caps with ink-dark numerals — the register of a grocery wall price board, which is exactly the world the buyer walks every morning. Arabic store names sit beside their Latin codes at their own direction, isolated, never forced; Lalezar carries the Arabic card headers with its bold display naskh. The basis band runs above the board like the market's chalk header — window, method, policy version, weights version — because in this product the rule that produced the number is part of the number. The signature surface is the **shift lens**: stores down the rows, the four shift classes across the columns with their duotone sun/moon marks, each cell carrying its normalized figure with the YoY delta as text and a quiet citron or red-family wash — the number always present, matched classes only, "no trading" said out loud where a store never opens that class. When a run completes, the board settles in place with one controlled overshoot on the run-complete chip — the energetic personality's single allowed celebration — and the rank-change count is announced, never raced. The composition bar sits under the window selector like a small almanac strip: this window's season shares against the prior's, and when the misalignment flag fires it says so in plain text and offers the season-anchored basis in one click. Success is a state chip and one line of arithmetic ("11 متجراً مرتّباً… RYD-03 أعلى، JED-02 بحاجة إلى انتباه") — the floor-manager's kind of celebration. Motion discipline: everything settles ≤250ms, nothing bounces except the one success chip, and the only thing that moves twice is nothing.
