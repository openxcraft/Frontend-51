## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Timeline traversal chevrons (inline-start/end): directional — **flip**. External-link arrow (lineage links, artifact links): **flips**. Reschedule from→to arrows in the ledger: directional — **flip**. Calendar, clock, file/file-text, link, shield, check/check-circle, alert-triangle, bell, search, clipboard: semantically anchored — **never flip**. The custody strip's seq numerals and day-counts: physical order stable in both directions — no flip, no re-sequence (rtl-rules §4). No gavel glyph exists in the product's vocabulary — the signature is motion and state, never an icon (05).
