## Typography (P16 — Scheherazade New + Libre Caslon Text, textbook-classical)

- **Arabic: Scheherazade New** (real weights 400/500/600/700) — a traditional naskh with scholarly, courtroom-document temperament: matter titles, event titles, outcome summaries, all Arabic chrome. Its vertical metrics are famously generous (deep descenders, stacked diacritics), so the product binds **Arabic line-height ≥1.8** (above the rtl-rules §3 floor of 1.6 — Scheherazade clips its own marks at 1.6) and **Arabic body at ≥17px** in timeline and detail surfaces; 15px is permitted only in navigator rows, where 1.8 line-height is still mandatory.
- **Latin: Libre Caslon Text** (real weights 400/700 + italic) — a Caslon text cut: case numbers, party names in Latin, day-counts, the single H1 per screen at 700; labels at 700 in small sizes (Caslon has no 500/600 — the weight strategy is two-step 400/700, designed within the family's real axis, rtl-rules §3; no synthetic bold ever).
- Day-counts, case numbers, hearing sequences, and chain sequence numerals render in the Latin family at **tabular-figure settings where the font build provides them**; where the cut lacks tnum, the day-count column reserves fixed cell widths so alignment never depends on figure widths (declared binding at implementation — never a fallback discovered in QA).
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; docket data surface per design-language-map §7): dates, day-counts, case numbers, sequence numbers all 0-9 — including inside Arabic chrome strings (never mixed systems mid-sentence, copy-voice §1.7). Storage canonical; display formatted.
- Bilingual field pairing renders Arabic in Scheherazade and English in Libre Caslon at matched optical weight (400↔400; Arabic titles at 500↔Latin 700 where emphasis must match, re-bound never synthesized).

## T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Gavel Settles")

On `recordHearingOutcome` success (03 CP-2.5 — the product's single most important action: a hearing passes from *expected* to *decided*): the hearing card's countdown ring drains to its close over 360ms (emphasized decelerate), the card settles one stratum down into the timeline bed as a completed event (scale 1.03→1.0, 320ms), the outcome chip (adjourned / verdict / ruling / other) stamps in after it (≤60ms stagger, ≤3 moving elements total), and — when the outcome schedules a next hearing — the NEW hearing's card rises into the upcoming bed with its own countdown ring already running. Reduced-motion: cards appear instantly, the outcome chip renders, and the polite announcement carries identical content ("Hearing 4 of case 2041/45 adjourned to 12 Nov 2026 — next hearing in 21 days. Outcome recorded by Layla."). Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the stately standard set.
