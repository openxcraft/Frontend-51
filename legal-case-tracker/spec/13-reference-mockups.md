## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Dossier Chronicle.** A courtroom chronicle drawn in one deep Qada Indigo line — Scheherazade's naskh beside Caslon's figures, paper strata, no glass, no gradient, no AI-purple anywhere. The docket home is the morning instrument: the hearing board reads like a judge's daily list — overdue hearings first in red-deep, then today, then imminent, each row a matter title in two scripts with its day-count set in tabular figures beside a small countdown ring, the ring's color earning its warmth only as dates approach (green to amber to red — time turning into consequence, never decoration). The matter view is the chronicle itself: a **recessed timeline bed** the events sit *in* — hearings, filings, notes, deadlines as cards rising one step out of the surface, the date rail running along the reading edge, countdown rings ticking at the head of upcoming hearings, generated deadlines carrying their rule names like marginalia ("مهلة الاستئناف 30 يوماً — Appeal window (30 days)" beside the deadline it made). Documents sit ON the desk, not in the bed: the register's rows on raised panels, and inside a document, the **custody strip** — a horizontal chain of umber seq nodes, received → registered → sealed → checked-out → presented → checked-in → returned, each node a small instrument with its actor and timestamp, the whole strip verifiable with one command that recomputes every fingerprint and says so in writing. The closure gate is the product's one drama kept quiet: a bordered manifest at the chronicle's end that lists what stands in closure's way — a hearing without an outcome, a deadline still open, an original still out — and refuses, naming each, until the manifest is empty. And once, on the single most important action in the product, the chronicle moves: recording an adjourned outcome **settles the gavel** — the hearing's ring drains closed over 360ms, the card settles down into the bed as history, the outcome chip stamps in, and the next hearing rises with its own ring already counting — three moving elements, then stillness again (05's signature, unchanged). Everything else is register-quiet: stately crossfades, border-step hovers, chips that arrive and never bounce, and the Arabic running right-to-left through all of it with its Latin case numbers held stable like exhibits.
