## Motion personality: stately (motion-language §1)

Durations 280–480ms; emphasized decelerate; **staggered reveals ≤60ms capped at 5 elements; zero overshoot, zero bounce** (stately is weighted, not playful — errors arrive, they never shake). Micro-interaction mapping (motion-language §2): hover = affordance discovery (border.interactive emphasis + navy edge bar, no scale); press = ≤80ms tactile confirm; focus = visible ring (motion never replaces focus indicators); success = the fold and the seal (the only ceremonies); error = arrive-and-settle panels (140ms slide-in, then stillness); streaming = not applicable (no AI text surfaces — the conditionally-AI surface is capture guidance, which is state chips, not streaming text); empty→filled = step cards settle in place (never spin in).

### The motion signature — the fold (SME-I original)

A KYC application is a document that must survive interruption, and the product's recognizability is the motion of a document being **folded and unfolded**: when a step completes, its card folds into the Progress Spine — a 320ms emphasized-decelerate translation into the spine slot with a depth settle (the layered shadow collapses to the fold-mark chip) — leaving a navy fold-mark that IS the step's completed state. When an applicant resumes, the completed folds **unfold in a 5-element cascade (≤60ms stagger)** — the application visibly remembers where it was. The submission fold settles the entire spine at once (the one full-ceremony moment). The counter-cadence is **the seal**: a reviewer's decision commits as a 360ms weighted press-settle on the dossier's verdict panel (scale 1.0→0.985→1.0, the navy seal chip remains) — evidence gaining a verdict, never a celebration. Fold and seal are the only two places the personality expresses; everything else is the stately standard set. The signature does information work: the fold state IS the resume state (an applicant can see at a glance how much of the document is done — and so can a reviewer glancing at the spine in the dossier).

### Tier-1 motion obligations (motion-language §4) — three original interactions delivered

1. **The Progress Spine + the fold** (13 §B1): the resumability signature — steps as fold-marked rail; resume cascade; a11y implications and reduced-motion contract in 13.
2. **The Capture Coach** (13 §B2): the guidance signature — frame guide, live gate checklist, assist hints, screen-reader guidance strings, manual-upload alternative.
3. **The Evidence Dossier + the seal** (13 §B3): the accountability signature — four evidence layers with occlusion depth, mismatch flags, reason-coded decisions, the seal settle.

Each proposal states the work its motion does; none is ornamental. Route transitions: crossfade ≤280ms (stately's route band); queue row → dossier is an in-place drawer expansion; sheets scale 0.96→1.0 + fade 140ms with focus trap and return (a11y-baseline §3); stagger caps honored everywhere.
