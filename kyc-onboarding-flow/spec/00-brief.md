# 00 — Brief: kyc-onboarding-flow

Family: specialized · Tier: 1 (FLAGSHIP) · Roster status: researched proposal, pending owner confirmation · Seed: none supplied.

## What it is

An applicant-facing multi-session onboarding flow plus a reviewer console for identity verification at MENA fintechs. Every wallet, lender, and trading platform in the region runs the same funnel: a motivated applicant opens an account, meets an identity-verification wall, and — in the retrieved band, 40–80% of the time — quits before the first transaction (01 §4: Signicat 68%, Jumio 60–80%, Lorikeet 40–70%, with document upload the single worst step at 30–40% drop-off). This product owns the two surfaces where that money is lost. On the **applicant side**, an application is not a page — it is a durable, resumable object: every answer autosaves (P10), every step is independently valid (P09), prior answers are recalled and marked as recalled across sessions (P11), the session warns before it times out and offers to extend (a11y-baseline §7 fintech overlay — assertive announcement, extend action), and a resume link returns the applicant to the exact step they left, on any device, for a declared window. On the **console side**, submitted applications compile into an evidence-first review queue: each task carries its documents, its deterministic capture-quality reports, its extracted-data mismatches, and its full audit chain; reviewers decide with reason codes at keyboard speed; every decision, rework request, and escalation is ledger-grade (P17).

The flagship mechanic is **document-capture guidance**. The document step is where onboarding funnels die (01 §4.2), and the category's answer is either a black-box SDK or a wall of error messages after a failed upload. Here the capture step is a coached, inspectable instrument: a frame guide with real-time quality feedback — glare, blur, framing, alignment, edge completeness — evaluated by **deterministic rule gates whose exact thresholds are published in the spec (07c §2.1)** and rendered to the applicant as a live bilingual checklist that ticks as each gate passes. The AI stance is **conditionally-AI** (declared in 07a/07b/08): exactly one capability — the document-capture quality assist — may be enriched by an abstract model capability when a workspace binds one; when unbound, rate-limited, or failed, the product degrades to the checklist guidance and the rule gates, total and instant, with engine provenance displayed honestly. No other surface reads a model. The product never decides identity: the automated document-verification and face-matching engines are **abstract capabilities (08 N1)** whose machine assessments arrive as review inputs, never as verdicts — the human reviewer decides, always (LOOP-10).

The product ends at the decision's edge, deliberately. It is not an identity decisioning engine (institution-side, abstract — the buyer's existing or future verification binding produces assessments this console presents); it has no liveness hardware and ships no sanctions or watchlist screening (LOOP-10, both excluded in the roster row); it processes no payments and holds no funds. What it produces instead is **evidence with continuity**: an application record whose every step, capture, quality report, rework round, and decision chains forward immutably (07a state machines), exportable as the audit artifact a compliance officer hands a regulator (02 Sami), and an applicant experience whose resumability converts the region's abandonment band into recovered applications — the highest-ROI purchase in the specialized family (01 §7).

## Surface

Two shells over one IA (04). The **applicant flow** (mobile-first, ≥360px, touch, RTL-first, one question per screen — 01 §4.4: the one-question-per-screen pattern is the category's own documented conversion practice) carries: entry (start or resume doors), the step flow with the Progress Spine, the capture step with the Capture Coach, review-and-submit with attestation, and the live status page. The **review console** (desktop-first, ≥1024px, keyboard-first — j/k queue navigation, one-key decisions) carries: the review queue with SLA ribbons, the review workspace with the Evidence Dossier, the flow designer (step program, thresholds, session policy), applicants directory, templates studio (applicant-facing notices), team, ledger & jobs, and inbox. The applicant shell is the product's trust surface (bilingual-ar, فصحى وسطى register for notices); the console is its accountability surface (bilingual, keyboard-speed). No vendor, provider, or engine name appears anywhere in the UI — capabilities are abstract (08), the adapter is the only boundary (07b).

## Unique feature

**Document-capture guidance with resumable multi-session applications.** Two interlocking mechanisms, both inspectable:

1. **Resumability as a first-class entity, not a cookie.** Retrieved evidence (01 §4.3): save-and-resume is named best practice for multi-step forms (FormAssembly, WeWeb, WebEyez), saved forms resume "an hour or months later" (Smart Communications), and 38% of abandoners lacked their document at hand (Signicat via Sortment) — meaning the winning flow must expect interruption by design. Here the application is a durable object with per-step autosave, a resume token with a declared window, cross-device continuation, P11 recall marking (prior answers render marked as recalled, never silently re-asked), and session-timeout warnings with an extend action — the a11y overlay's fintech obligations (a11y-baseline §7) are contract rules (07b), not UI options. No retrieved competitor ships resumability as an entity with expiry semantics and nudge links (observed absence, 01 §6).
2. **Capture guidance you can read.** The category's capture failure taxonomy is public (01 §4.2: glare, blur, framing, rotation, cropped edges, curved documents); the winning pattern is real-time gate evaluation with auto-capture (Incode's published design guidance). This product formalizes both: deterministic rule gates with published thresholds (07c §2.1) + a live checklist surface + the degrading assist. Reviewers see the same quality reports the applicant's capture produced — the loop closes, because a blurry ID is not a UX problem, it is a blind spot for fraud (01 §4.2, NV Global).

## Exclusions (narrow scope, enforced by LOOP-10)

- **No identity decisioning engine** — automated document verification, forgery detection, and face matching are abstract capabilities (08 N1) that return machine assessments as reviewer inputs; the product renders them, never trusts them as verdicts, and remains fully functional with the capability unbound (reviewers read raw evidence — LOOP-5 manual alternative).
- **No liveness hardware** — no specialized capture hardware, no depth sensors, no hardware attestation. A presence-capture step (selfie class) exists in the flow program as POLICY-optional with a declared alternative path (a11y-baseline §7: no biometric step is the only path); liveness analysis itself belongs to the abstract verification capability, never this product.
- **No sanctions/watchlist screening** — PEP/sanctions lists are a separate institution-side system (roster exclusion); the console records their absence from the decision surface honestly (11 copy names the boundary).
- **No AML program management** — ongoing monitoring, SAR filing, periodic review cycles are out of scope; this product ends at the onboarding decision and its audit trail.
- **No funnel analytics** — drop-off exploration, segment annealing, and funnel cohorts are funnel-drop-off-explorer's family boundary (LOOP-3 mirror, 12 Q-5); this product shows operational queue counters only.
- **No applicant chat** — communication is structured and versioned: resume nudges, decision notices, rework requests (templates, 08 N3). A chat thread would destroy the evidence discipline.
- **No identity decisioning by AI** — the single conditionally-AI surface is capture-quality assist guidance, labeled and degradable (ai-native-patterns §1); no model approves, rejects, or scores an applicant anywhere in the contract.

## Success signals (spec-level definition of done)

Against the Mock Adapter, with zero provider knowledge anywhere in the UI:

1. **A full applicant journey on a phone-sized viewport (360px, Arabic RTL), interrupted by design**: Amal starts an application, answers the identity steps with autosave visible, opens the document step, times out (session warning announced assertively with extend offered), leaves, returns the next day through the resume link on a different device, finds her prior answers marked as recalled, captures her residence permit through the coached frame (two glare retakes, each with the failing gate named), reviews the summary, attests, and submits — total applicant-session time under 12 minutes of active work (inside the 14-minute abandonment patience band, 01 §4.3), replayed deterministically twice with byte-identical state.
2. **A reviewer clears a 12-task queue in under 5 minutes** on the console: every row shows deadline, aging, rework rounds, and capture-warning count without opening the dossier; one keystroke opens the evidence; reason-coded decisions (approve / reject / request-rework / escalate) commit with mandatory rationale where the policy demands it; a rework decision round-trips to the applicant as a needs-attention notice naming the exact steps to fix.
3. **An ops lead stands up the spine in under 30 minutes**: a five-step flow program (identity → document → presence-optional → review → submit) with document types, capture thresholds, a 20-minute session policy with 30-day resume window, and two bilingual notice templates — every policy renderable as the same sheet applicants and reviewers see.
4. **The dispute test**: a regulator asks why an application was rejected; the ledger view shows the submitted documents with quality reports, the machine assessments with provenance, the reviewer's decision with reason codes and note, the rework rounds, and the attestation — in two clicks, exportable as the audit artifact before its expiry window closes.
5. **The degradation test**: with every abstract capability unbound (verification assessment, capture assist, resume-link channel), the product remains a complete onboarding desk — manual document review against the published quality reports, checklist-guided capture, resume links delivered by copy/paste, full ledger (LOOP-5 bar).
