## What it is

A **cohort grid** analytics product for revenue operations teams at MENA retailers and e-commerce merchants: customers acquired in a defined window as rows, their repeat purchasing and cumulative revenue as columns over subsequent periods, computed over order data supplied by the buyer's existing commerce source through the adapter (the product binds a source; it is not a CRM and never becomes an order store). The grid itself is vendor-established craft (01: Mixpanel, Amplitude, and PostHog all ship retention grids; the acquisition-window × subsequent-period triangle with its period-0 column and its cell-to-member drill is the shape analysts already read). What no vendor ships — and what no storefront analytics console even attempts (01: WooCommerce's mature reporting stops at period comparison, with no cohort construct and no seasonality concept) — is the part this product owns: **the grid is lying to MENA retailers about their seasons, and this product stops the lie.**

The MENA retail calendar is dominated by two different clocks. Lunar seasons — Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha — drift 10–11 days earlier every Gregorian year because the Islamic calendar carries no intercalation (research S7). Fixed-season events — White Friday, national days, back-to-school — hold their Gregorian dates (research S13). A plain Gregorian monthly cohort grid silently smears both: a "March cohort" is pre-Ramadan buildup in one year, peak-Ramadan acquisition in the next, and post-Eid wind-down in a third; comparing March to March then compares three different retail moments. The classic fix in official statistics is year-on-year comparison to avoid seasonality contamination (research S11), but YoY on drifting seasons still compares misaligned windows — the Ramadan of this year simply is not where March was last year. **Season-overlay cohorts** are this product's answer: the workspace declares **season windows** (dual Hijri/Gregorian dates, phase maps — build-up, peak, wind-down — and a ±1-day drift disclosure on moon-sighting-dependent windows, per portfolio Q-5's Umm al-Qura presentation discipline), the grid overlays those windows onto the acquisition axis, and cohorts can be **season-anchored**: defined by acquisition during a named season window ("Ramadan 1447 peak-window acquirers") and compared against the same named season across years — Ramadan 1447 versus Ramadan 1446, eleven Gregorian days apart, the same retail moment. Every comparison carries a **season-composition disclosure** (what share of each cohort's window each season occupied), so a reader always knows whether two cohorts are seasonally comparable before reading their numbers.

The analytics are deterministic cohort arithmetic — every metric, threshold, and comparison rule is published on an in-product definitions surface and versioned with every grid snapshot (07a/07b/07c: acquisition assignment, period bucketing, the four canonical cell metrics with exact formulas, small-cohort caution at N<30 and count-only suppression at N<10, incomplete-period marking, weighted-mean disclosure). The AI stance is **declared non-AI** (ai-native-patterns §1): no generation, no scoring, no prediction — the intelligence is in the season calendar discipline and the arithmetic's inspectability, and zero AI affordances appear anywhere in the UI. The product deliberately ends at analysis (LOOP-10): it manages no customer records (the CRM is the bound source's territory — the roster's "no CRM ( adapter binds)" exclusion), it does no forecast modeling (observed cumulative revenue only; the prediction half of classic CLV is refused — research S15), and it does no territory mapping. Its deliverable is the **cohort evidence pack** — an exportable artifact of grid, comparison, and the season definitions it ran under, reproducible by construction because the deterministic engine recomputes it from the same inputs.

## Unique feature

**Season-overlay cohorts (Ramadan/White Friday) for MENA retail cycles.** Defensible on three grounds (01 evidence): (1) the vendor retention grid is event-space, not revenue-space — Mixpanel/Amplitude/PostHog cohort around product events for growth teams (01 §2), not around first-order revenue for revenue ops, and none carries a retail-season calendar; (2) the seasonality instinct exists in official statistics as deseasonalization methodology (S11) but as no retail-analytics product feature — and deseasonalizing is modeling we refuse (LOOP-10); re-anchoring to named seasons with composition disclosure is deterministic and honest in a way statistical adjustment is not; (3) the storefront incumbents (WooCommerce-class analytics, 01) ship neither cohorts nor seasons — the practitioner gap is real and hand-built today (S12 + the spreadsheet status quo). The season calendar itself is workspace data (editable windows, phase maps, drift disclosure), seeded with curated MENA defaults; binding a curated moment library owned by a planning sibling is an owner-level future decision, not a v1 surface (12, LOOP-3).

## Exclusions (narrow scope, enforced by LOOP-10)

- **No CRM** — no customer record management, no contact profiles, no segmentation-for-campaigns execution, no pipeline. Customer data arrives as references from the bound commerce source through the adapter; the roster's exclusion verbatim: "no CRM (adapter binds)". Acting on a cohort (emailing, targeting) happens in the buyer's own systems; this product's handoff is the export of a cohort's customer list.
- **No forecast modeling** — no predicted CLV/LTV, no churn prediction, no season-spend projection, no statistical seasonal adjustment (deseasonalization is modeling — S11 methods are refused). Observed cumulative revenue and repeat rates only; the definitions surface says so explicitly.
- **No territory mapping** — no geographic sales mapping, no rep/territory hierarchies, no geo heat maps. Market (country-level) is a filter dimension, not a mapped surface.
- **No order storage** — the product stores its own specs (season windows, saved views, comparisons, exports) and computes grids over the bound source or a bounded pasted/imported sample; it never becomes an order warehouse (the adapter binds — the funnel-drop-off-explorer posture, applied to order data).
- **No campaign execution or messaging** — no sends, no journeys, no automation rules; retention tooling ends at evidence.
- **No AI features** (declared non-AI) — no generation, no anomaly narration, no scoring intelligence; zero AI affordances in the UI.

## Success signals (spec-level definition of done)

Against the Mock Adapter, with zero provider knowledge anywhere in the UI:

1. **A first grid in under 10 minutes**: a new analyst opens the workspace, the seeded commerce source is bound and fresh, the default monthly revenue cohort grid renders (24 months of coverage, October 2026 marked "period in progress"), the analyst switches granularity to weekly, turns the season overlay on, and reads the Ramadan 1447 band across the acquisition axis — no SQL, no spreadsheet pivot, no data-team request.
2. **The full season journey on the Mock**: monthly grid → season overlay on → Ramadan 1446 and Ramadan 1447 windows visible on the acquisition axis with their ±1-day drift disclosure → season-anchored view → "Ramadan 1447 peak" cohort (N₀ 214) opens in cohort detail → compared with "Ramadan 1446 peak" (N₀ 178) in the aligned comparison, composition disclosure naming each cohort's window composition → repeat-rate metric at period 6 read for both → customer drill into one cohort cell → evidence pack exported with the season definitions it ran under — replayed deterministically twice with byte-identical state (07c §6).
3. **The honesty tests**: a small cohort (N₀ < 10) renders count-only with the suppression note; a cell with denominator < 30 carries the small-cohort caution; the incomplete period (October 2026) is marked and excluded from means; with the bound source absent and only a pasted sample present, the full flow — grid, overlay, anchoring, comparison, export — still completes on the sample (LOOP-5 bar, 08).
4. **The keyboard-speed test**: a working analyst moves cell-to-cell, switches metrics, toggles the overlay, opens cohort detail, and stages a comparison entirely from the keyboard (arrow grid navigation with physical mapping stable in both directions, M metric, S overlay, D detail, C compare — 09) with every state change announced per the live-region schedule and zero color-only carriers anywhere in the grid (09; a11y-baseline §4).
