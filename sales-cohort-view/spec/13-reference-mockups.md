4. **WooCommerce — Analytics and Sales Reports** — https://woocommerce.com/document/woocommerce-analytics/ (S12)

## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Marine Ledger Desk: a flat-line, marine-ruled revenue instrument where the grid sits IN the surface, not on it. The workspace reads as one continuous ruled sheet — the cohort grid occupies a recessed bed (a half-step down in tint, a hairline border, never a shadow), so cells feel engraved into the desk while the metric switcher and freshness chip sit ON the raised chrome above it. The signature surface is the **season rail**: a thin band running along the acquisition axis where each declared season window lands as a bordered band with phase stripes (build-up hatched, peak solid-bordered, wind-down dotted) and a drift tick on every lunar window — the eleven-day walk of Ramadan across two years is visible as geometry before it is visible as numbers, and the composition sentence under the grid header translates that geometry into plain arithmetic the moment the overlay settles (140ms, one settle, nothing moves twice). Season chips never decorate: six kinds, six fixed marks, each icon+text and each carrying its contrast contract, so a printed screenshot in grayscale still reads. The comparison duel is two cohort columns meeting at a center rule — curves aligned at period 0 regardless of Gregorian dates, deltas stated in text in every row, the truncation note saying exactly where the younger cohort's data stops. Success is a state chip and one quiet line of arithmetic ("11.0% مقابل 7.0% — بفارق 4.0 نقاط، والمقامان 214 و178"), the analyst's kind of celebration: nothing bounces, nothing sparkles, the numbers do the talking and the desk proves them.
