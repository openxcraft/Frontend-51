## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius D (pill, 20px+)**: objective cards 20px; chips, state badges, confidence dial segments, and all controls fully-rounded (the D family's geometry); the KR progress bar is the one declared exception — a 10px-radius track (a measurement instrument, not a capsule; deviation recorded here per house pattern).
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: one ambient diffuse shadow on raised cards over warm paper; the cascade's company tier sits one soft step above team lanes — hierarchy by a single lift, never stacked shadows or occlusion.
- **Icons: duotone**: two-tone glyphs (lime-deep + lime light-tone; state icons in their semantic pair) — flag (objective), target-with-notch (key result), pulse-line (check-in), lattice (alignment link), dial (confidence), clock-with-dot (period). No sparkle glyphs anywhere — the drafting assist's affordance is a pen-tip glyph with a written "Draft proposals" label (ai-native-patterns §1: no magic affordances).
- **Motion: energetic** (motion-language §1): 150–250ms, standard easing, ONE controlled overshoot (≤8% travel) reserved for the single success moment below; errors never bounce; route changes crossfade ≤180ms; the coverage grid's state changes pair a ≤200ms cell transition with the text delta (motion never carries information alone — a11y-baseline §4).
- **Signature micro-interaction (T3: exactly one) — "the Cascade Settle"**: on check-in submit, three things settle in choreographed sequence within 250ms total — the KR's bar fills to the recomputed progress, the parent objective's rollup number ticks to its new value with the single controlled overshoot (1.0→1.04→1.0 scale on the number chip, ≤8% travel), and the company-tier ring advances its arc. One polite announcement mirrors the numbers ("Checkout conversion 2.4 of 3.0 — 42% · objective 38%"). Reduced-motion: the overshoot collapses; values change in place with the identical announcement (perceivability preserved by construction). The Settle is the product's emotional register in one interaction: the number moving is the celebration — no confetti, ever.
