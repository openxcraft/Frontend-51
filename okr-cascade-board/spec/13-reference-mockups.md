## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Waraq Board: a lime-lit greenhouse ledger. The board is warm paper — a grower's notebook, not a war room — with the company tier sitting one soft lift above the team lanes like the upper shelf of a greenhouse, and every objective card a paper pill with the bilingual pair rendered as two lines of one thought (EN and AR at their own directions, the parity indicators between them like a binding stitch). Key results run as ink-ruled meter bars — hairline-bordered tracks with a lime fill and the number always printed beside in text, the way a grower writes the reading next to the gauge. The cadence dashboard is the weather wall: the KR × period grid in paper and ink with four quiet states — on-track green ink, in-grace lime, at-risk amber (with its deeper amber text), overdue red — every cell carrying its state as words, because the leadership meeting reads, it does not decode. The assist is a pen tray that slides in from the inline edge: a focus field, then proposals arriving as typed paper slips in the reserved space, each stamped "drafted by the assist — yours to judge" — accept slips into the composer, edit opens it, dismiss files it with a soft ink line-through, and when the assist is unbound the tray simply is not there and the pair editor stands complete on its own. The one celebration is the Cascade Settle: a check-in submits, the meter fills, the objective's rollup number ticks once with a 4% pop, and the company ring advances — then stillness. Mood: a quiet greenhouse at 09:30 on a Monday — everything measured, nothing shouting, one lime highlighter doing all the pointing.

## Mood anchors

Warm paper under one soft lift · a lime highlighter that only marks progress and selection · two scripts as two lines of one thought, stitched by parity marks · the weather wall of the cadence — states in words, never a heatmap · the Settle: one number ticks, everything else holds still · a pen tray that is honestly absent when unbound.
