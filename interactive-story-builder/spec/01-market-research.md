## Category overview

Scrollytelling/visual-storytelling builders are an established, actively compared category (2026 roundups: Maglr [16], Pandasuite [17], Gitnux [18]): editorial long-form tools (Shorthand), data-viz suites (Flourish), map-centric builders (ArcGIS StoryMaps), education suites (Genially), no-code/enterprise platforms (Vev, Ceros). The category job: free newsrooms, NGOs, and universities from developer-dependent bespoke builds. The regional gap is structural: no surveyed leader documents RTL-native layouts or ar/en chapter synchronization as first-class concepts [1][7][10][13][14][16] — Arabic is localization debt; bilingual stories are two separately maintained artifacts.

| Vev | no-code scrollytelling components [14] | design control | designer-oriented; enterprise weight; no bilingual construct |

## Indirect competitors (4)

- **Storyraise** — nonprofit digital-report/donor platform [20]: fundraising-personalization-locked, not a general builder.
- **Custom-coded newsroom stacks** — the developer bottleneck the category exists to remove [16][17][26].
- **CMS-embed workflows** — free embeds pasted into newsroom CMSs [19]: fragmented, no review loop, no alignment.
- **PDF annual reports / slide decks** — the incumbent format NGOs/universities actually ship [27a]: zero interactivity, zero parity tooling.

## Market/demand evidence

- Newsroom need: data/visual journalism carries "heavy expectations and limited support" [26].
- MENA expansion: Reuters launched a dedicated Arabic platform Sep 2025 [21]; regional narrative-format appetite documented [22]; RTL-first development is marketed as a regional specialty [23] — global tools leave RTL to local effort.
- NGO demand: annual-report best practice now prescribes interactive digital reports [27a][27b]; nonprofit storytelling platforms exist [20]; Shorthand sells a nonprofit vertical [6].
- University demand: story maps embedded in instruction and library guides across institutions [24a][24b][24c][25].

## Pricing landscape (as reported — unverified estimates)

Shorthand $60–200+/mo depending on source/plan [2][3][4] (per-story pricing exists [4]); Ceros $2,000–5,000+/mo [15]; ArcGIS nonprofit ~$100/user/yr [12]; Genially/Flourish free tiers [7][13]. Our segment sits between free-fragile and $200+ enterprise: a per-seat mid tier with a published-story cap is the open position.

## Positioning statement

For bilingual newsrooms, NGOs, and universities that must publish scroll-driven stories in Arabic and English without a developer, interactive-story-builder is the scrollytelling builder whose step layouts are RTL-native and whose chapters keep both locales structurally in sync — unlike editorial tools that treat Arabic as an afterthought and GIS builders that assume maps. Structure-first authoring, directional preview, and an honest QA gate replace bespoke builds and duplicated single-locale stories.
