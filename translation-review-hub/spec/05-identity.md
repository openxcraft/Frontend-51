- Line-height: Arabic 1.6 / Latin 1.5 (tashkeel-bearing segment content gets the ≥1.8 reservation when seeded copy carries diacritics — rtl-rules §3); tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level obligation); no synthetic bold — missing weights re-bind to 400/700.
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; data surface per design-language-map §7): character counts, ratios, limits, severity counts, segment numbers all 0-9 — including inside Arabic chrome strings (never mixed systems mid-sentence, copy-voice §1.7).

## Direction model (rtl-rules §1)

**bilingual-ar** (proposed, LOOP-6 pending owner confirmation): the reviewer layer across MENA is Arabic-first in practice (02 Yusuf/Rana) while jobs run both directions (en→ar and ar→en are both first-class, 00). The structural rule is the **pane-direction contract**: in the side-by-side pair view, the source pane renders at the source locale's direction and the target pane at the target locale's direction — an en→ar job places an LTR pane inline-start beside an RTL pane inline-end, and an ar→en job mirrors it; panes never inherit the workspace chrome's direction for content. Navigator rows, findings rail, glossary tables, and all chrome follow the workspace locale (either direction, full citizen — flip re-renders everything, verification matrix in 09). Mixed-script segment text is bidi-isolated at render (rtl-rules §2); segment numbers and character counts keep physical order stable in both directions (rtl-rules §4).

## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: panes and cards 8px; navigator rows 6px; chips fully-rounded ONLY as the removable filter-chip exception (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here).
- **Elevation: flat-line**: hairline teal-tinted borders carry all structure; the pair view is the "deepest" surface by border weight alone; zero shadows anywhere (including focus — focus is the ring, not a glow).
- **Icons: line-2** (standard 2px stroke outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary per the skill's icons.csv — columns/split for pane layout, flag for term violations, alert-triangle for length warnings, link for consistency siblings, check-circle for decisions/seal, clipboard for paste intake, globe for direction pair). No sparkle glyphs anywhere — non-AI product, no magic affordances (ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, **zero overshoot ever**. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; navigator selection settles in place (nothing spins in); finding chips arrive (120ms fade+settle) and dissolve on resolution (140ms) — arrival/departure always accompanied by the rail's live-region text (a11y-baseline §4 — motion never carries information alone). Reduced-motion: hard cuts + announcements, identical perceivability.
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Gate Clears")**: on sign-off success, the gate's blocker list collapses row-by-row — stagger ≤60ms capped at 5 rows, 120ms each, ease-out — into the signed state chip with actor + timestamp (the product's single most important action, 03 CP-5.3). Reduced-motion: rows vanish instantly, the chip renders, and the announcement carries the full summary. Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the crisp standard set.
