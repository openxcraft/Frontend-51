The topic workspace reads as one continuous ruled sheet: the keyword table's hairline rows flow upward into cluster cards separated only by border-weight steps, so the eye traces evidence → structure without ever crossing a "module boundary". Crisp motion throughout: everything settles in ≤180ms, nothing ever bounces, and the only thing that moves twice is nothing.
