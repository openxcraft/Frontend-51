## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Sarf Ledger.** A money instrument drawn in one mid blue on cool paper — Sarf Blue #5073B5 as the signal color, blue-deep for every figure that matters, soft-lift reserved for the computed artifacts (the KPI band, the timeline card, the reconciliation pair) so the page reads as chrome-quiet with lifted evidence. The dashboard is a ledger first and a chart second: the period total sits top-inline-start in Sora 600 tabular figures with the snapshot version chip locked beside it — the number and its version are one visual unit, because in this product the version IS part of the number. The timeline runs the full window beneath it: the daily series as a restrained blue line, overlay bands as the only warm regions on the page — gold-tinted rectangles with gold-deep boundaries and bilingual labels (الجمعة البيضاء in one market's band, Yellow Friday in another's over the same weekend), the control window ghosted as a bordered silhouette so the comparison's baseline is visible, not implied. Click a day and the breakdown answers below: one row per channel, native currency in its own quiet column, the reporting-currency amount in tabular figures, and the receipt expander beside every converted figure — native amount, rate, basis, policy version — evidence nested exactly one glance under every claim. The reconciliation console is the product's courtroom: two line-item panels facing each other (the agency statement left, the marketplace console right — mirrored in RTL), the match evidence between them (external key, day, delta, score), and the resolution actions below with the reason field standing open — because the reason is the artifact finance will read. Overlays get their own room: the moment library as a bilingual index of the retail calendar, the comparison view as three windows side by side (this occurrence, prior occurrence, control) with the lift ratio stamped between them and the method disclosure one focus away. The whole surface leans toward one moment: the consolidation run completing — "The Total Stamps" — the figure settling into place with its version chip in a single crisp beat, announced in one sentence. Everything else is stillness: bands arrive with their labels, receipts expand in 140ms, findings arrive and dissolve with live-region text, and nothing on the page ever moves twice.
