## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Next/previous-day chevrons on the timeline: directional — flip (RTL timeline runs right-to-left, so "next day" points inline-start in RTL). Drill-down arrow (breakdown → line items): directional — flip. External-key link arrow: directional — flips. Download/export arrow: directional — flips. Trending-up (pacing): semantically anchored to the chart's up axis — never flips. Calendar, coins, receipt, layers (channel stack), git-compare (revision delta), link-2 (overlap pair), filter, pie-chart, alert-triangle, check-circle, refresh-cw, globe: semantically anchored or symmetric — never flip. Band boundary markers: symmetric — never flip.
