## Typography (P04 — Cairo + Sora)

- **Arabic: Cairo** (weights used: 400/600/700/800 — the family ships a 200–900 range; the spec designs within 400–800 and never synthesizes a missing weight, rtl-rules §3): geo-modern kufi-derived sans — confident, architectural, exactly the temperament a money ledger wants in Arabic chrome; moment names (الجمعة البيضاء، عيد الفطر) render in Cairo 700 at ≥16px; body chrome Cairo 400 at 15–16px.
- **Latin: Sora** (weights used: 400/500/600/700): geometric display-grade sans with a confident skeleton — 600 is the data-surface voice (table headers, KPI values, receipt figures), 500 for labels, 700 reserved for the single H1 per screen and the period total. Amounts, rates, days, and ratios render at **tabular-figure settings** (the mono-for-data instinct adopted as a rule, not a third family — font-variant-numeric where the stack supports it; the amount column never reflows when a digit flips).
- Line-height: Arabic 1.6 / Latin 1.5 (moment names with diacritics get the ≥1.8 reservation — rtl-rules §3); tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level obligation); no synthetic bold — missing weights re-bind to 400/700 equivalents.
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; data surface per design-language-map §7): amounts, rates, ratios, days, occurrence years, version numbers all 0-9 — including inside Arabic chrome strings (never mixed systems mid-sentence, copy-voice §1.7). Currency codes (SAR, AED, USD, EGP) render in Latin always, bidi-isolated inside Arabic prose (rtl-rules §2).

## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: data cards 8px; table rows 6px; chips fully-rounded ONLY as the removable filter-chip exception (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here); receipts render as 6px inset panels.
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: one diffuse ambient shadow step under raised data cards (the KPI band, the timeline card, the reconciliation pair panels) — `shadow.softlift` resolves to a low-blur, low-opacity ambient; NO stacked layers, NO occlusion, no shadow on focus (focus is the Sarf ring). Flat paper carries structure by surface step; the lift marks "this is a computed artifact" vs the paper's "this is chrome".
- **Icons: duotone** (two-tone constructed set): first tone Sarf Blue #5073B5, second tone Blue-deep #2E4B83 — Lucide-class vocabulary as the shape reference (layers, calendar, coins, receipt, git-compare, link-2, trending-up, filter, pie-chart, alert-triangle, check-circle, download, globe, refresh-cw), rendered as a duotone set: keyline at 1.75px stroke in first tone, interior accent fill at 25% second tone. Semantic overrides: over-pace/alert icons take their semantic color pair (red + red-deep 25%); overlay-band icons take the gold pair. No sparkle glyphs anywhere — non-AI product, no magic affordances (ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, **zero overshoot ever**. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; timeline day-focus settles in place (nothing slides); band labels arrive with the band (120ms fade+settle); receipt expanders open 140ms; finding chips arrive/dissolve 120/140ms with live-region text equivalents (a11y-baseline §4 — motion never carries information alone). Charts update by redraw-settle, never by animated tween of the series (a number that moves is a number that changed — and the change is announced).
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Total Stamps")**: on consolidation-run completion — the product's single most important action (03 CP-1.9) — the period total stamps in: the figure renders in Sora 600 tabular figures with a single soft-lift settle (scale 0.98→1.0 + fade, 140ms, ease-out), the snapshot version chip locks beside it in the same beat (slate border-step), and the live region announces the total, the channel count, and any open overlaps in one sentence. Reduced-motion: instant render + the full announcement carries the summary. Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the crisp standard set — breakdown rows, band labels, and receipts are standard-set.
