- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: mapping table rows 6px; channel cards and panels 8px; readiness chips fully-rounded ONLY as the removable filter-chip exception (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here).
- **Elevation: flat-line**: hairline violet-tinted borders carry all structure; the mapping table is the "deepest" surface by border weight alone; zero shadows anywhere (focus is the ring, not a glow).
- **Icons: outline-geometric** (constructed-grid outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary per the skill's icons.csv — the skill's `grid` row is the layout-tile glyph): columns-split for mapping rows, check-square for checklist rules, alert-octagon for blockers, alert-triangle for warnings, globe for locale variants, link for mapping dependencies, upload-cloud for publish handoff, file-down for exports, lightbulb-outline for suggestions (an outline glyph — see the integration record's sparkle ban). No sparkle glyphs anywhere — the assist is labeled by provenance text, never magic affordances (ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, **zero overshoot ever**. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; mapping-row selection settles in place; finding chips arrive (120ms fade+settle) and dissolve on recompute (140ms) — arrival/departure always accompanied by the readiness rail's live-region text (a11y-baseline §4 — motion never carries information alone). Reduced-motion: hard cuts + announcements, identical perceivability.
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Checklist Closes")**: when a listing's last open blocker resolves (recompute passes after an edit, or a rule-pack change clears it), the listing's checklist column collapses its blocker rows — stagger ≤60ms capped at 5 rows, 120ms each, ease-out — into a single green **ready** chip carrying the count of rules passed ("14 rules passed"). The product's single most important action is a listing becoming publishable (03 CP-3.6→CP-4). Reduced-motion: rows vanish instantly, the chip renders, and the announcement carries the full summary ("Listing ready — 14 rules passed, 2 warnings acknowledged"). Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the crisp standard set.
