## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Sarraf Desk.** A money-changer's counter drawn in one violet line — flat borders on cool paper, no shadows, no gradient, no glass. The field map studio is the counter's ledger: a mapping table that reads like a ruled book of exchange — channel attribute on one edge, master field on the other, and between them the pair marks (ar/en/none) as small plum stamps and transform chips that say exactly what they do ("truncate at word boundary, 75"). The attribute-catalog rail stands beside the table like a posted rate board: required attributes carry their constraints in tabular figures, and the unmapped ones hold a quiet outline mark until someone maps them — mapping freedom, readiness honesty. The readiness palette speaks in verdicts the way a trader's marks speak in grades: red blockers, amber warnings (amber-deep for the words themselves), green ready, slate stale — every one carrying its arithmetic as text, because a seller trusts numbers, not tint. The mapped preview is the counter's glass top: each attribute key with its value flowing its own direction — Arabic rightward, English leftward — the override stamps sitting visibly ON the glass, never pretending to be master data. And the whole desk leans toward one moment: **the checklist closes** — a listing's blocker rows fold away in a crisp ≤60ms stagger and leave a single green chip with its count ("14 rules passed"), the sound of a stamped receipt, not a celebration. Everything else is stillness: rows settle, chips arrive and dissolve at crisp speed, nothing bounces, nothing sparkles — and the suggestion panel, when it is connected at all, shows its confidence like an exchange rate: a number, a formula beside it, and the trader's own hand on the accept button.

## Mood anchors

A money-changer's counter ruled in one violet line · a ledger of field exchange with plum pair-stamps between the columns · a posted rate board of channel requirements in tabular figures · verdict marks that carry their arithmetic · one green receipt-stamp when a listing is ready for its channel — then back to the next row of the map.
