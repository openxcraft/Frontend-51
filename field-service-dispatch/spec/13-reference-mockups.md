## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Mawqi Board: a deep-teal ruled operations desk in soft-lift — cards that sit one shadow above the paper and one more when they move. The board reads as one working day: eight technician lanes and the intake lane, each lane a quiet column of job-card tiles with the gate chip pinned to the card's inline-start edge — amber-deep while it waits, forest-green the moment the customer answers. The signature surface is **the gate itself**: every card carries its permission state as a small barrier glyph and a written word ("بانتظار التأكيد" / "awaiting confirmation"), and the only moment the board moves with intent is The Gate Clears — a 140ms chip flip and a one-step lift when a confirmation lands, then stillness again. Unconfirmed dispatches are not hidden: a card dispatched on a waiver wears its reason as a permanent written mark, and the day header's tally is one honest line of arithmetic ("5 مؤكدة · 0 رفضاً · 1 منتهية · 1 بلا ردّ · 3 معلّقة") rather than a dashboard of gauges. The technician's phone is the board's quiet other face: a stack of cards, each with one enormous verb button — arrive, start, pause, done — in Mada 700, one thumb, no chrome. Exports look like documents, not screenshots: the day sheet a dispatcher can hand to a driver, the job history a timeline a supervisor can hand to a disputing customer, both bilingual by locale. Crisp motion throughout: everything settles in ≤180ms, nothing bounces, and the only thing that moves twice is nothing.

## Mood anchors

A deep operational teal over light paper · lanes like a well-ruled duty roster · the gate chip as the day's traffic light, always with its word attached · one quiet lift when permission arrives, then back to work · the technician's phone carrying four large verbs and nothing else · an audit trail that reads like a ship's log, not a dashboard.
