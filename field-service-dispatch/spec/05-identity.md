## Typography (P15 — Mada + Instrument Sans)

- **Arabic: Mada** (real weights 400/500/700) — contemporary-grotesk Arabic that stays legible at board-card density and in one-thumb mobile buttons; body ≥15px equivalent in tables/lanes, 16px+ on the mobile job card.
- **Latin: Instrument Sans** (400/500/600/700) — current grotesque partner; **weight strategy (differentiating vs the P15 co-users, esp. seo-brief-builder's 500/600-weight analyst chrome): a heavier top-of-scale** — the day header and the single H1 per screen render Mada 700 / Instrument Sans 700; lane headers Instrument Sans 600; job-card titles Mada 500; body 400. The dispatch board is a display-surface-first layout (lanes, chips, tallies) rather than a table-first one — weight carries the hierarchy the analyst products carry with hairlines.
- Line-height: Arabic 1.6 / Latin 1.5 (tashkeel headroom reserved on seeded strings that carry diacritics); tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level, rtl-rules §3); no synthetic bold on missing weights (re-bind rule per rtl-rules §3).
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; dispatch is a data surface — windows, counts, phone fragments, job codes all 0-9; storage canonical, display formatted). Tabular figures (`tnum`-equivalent settings where the implementation supports them) on windows and the day tally so lane columns never jitter (10).
- One pairing for reading AND chrome (T3 discipline; no third family — the mono-for-data instinct from the skill scan is adopted as a rule, not a font: codes and times render in the pairing at tabular settings).

## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: job cards 8px, state chips 6px, dialogs 10px. Declared deviation: fully-rounded removable filter chips ONLY (P08 chip semantics — the same recorded exception as the P15 co-user discipline).
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: job cards lift one diffuse shadow step off the canvas; the dragged card lifts a second step (the only two-step lift in the product); the day header is raised; no occlusion layers, no glass, no borders-as-structure (that is the flat-line co-user's grammar — separation held).
- **Icons: outline-geometric** (constructed-grid outline set): job-card glyph (slotted ticket), gate glyph (half-open barrier — the product's signature mark, used on every gate chip), technician glyph, window glyph (clock-in-bracket), touchpoint glyphs (speech-line for requests, check-badge for confirmed, slash-badge for declined, hourglass-outline for expired). Lucide outline rows verified in the skill CSV (calendar, clock, map-pin, check, check-circle, phone, smartphone) converge with the constructed set and are adopted as vocabulary reference. Directional glyphs flip in RTL (arrows, chevrons, send); the gate, ticket, clock, and state badges never flip (semantic anchors — decisions recorded in 06).
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, enters may decelerate, **zero overshoot ever**. Card drag follows the pointer directly; drop settles in ≤80ms press-confirm. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; drawer/page slides reveal along the reading axis (inline-start in RTL). State transitions arrive — the chip flips in 120ms with the text change always accompanying (motion never carries information alone, a11y-baseline §4). Success moments: state chip + polite announcement; nothing bounces, nothing celebrates routine closes.
- **Signature micro-interaction (exactly one, T2/T3 discipline)** — **"The Gate Clears"**: when a pending confirmation turns confirmed, the job card's gate chip flips state in 140ms — outline-hourglass swaps to check-badge, the chip's border steps from amber-deep to confirmed-green, and the card lifts one soft-lift step and settles — the day's permission to move, made visible once. Accompanied by a polite announcement ("JOB-1024: customer confirmed") and by the tally increment. Reduced-motion: hard cut to the confirmed chip + the same announcement (perceivability never depends on the animation).
