Not taken: marketing-suite breadth (automation, campaigns) — single issues and their send jobs are our whole surface; AI writing assistant placement (our assist is one narrow, honest surface, not a co-writer).

3. **Email rendering-QA tools' client-matrix thinking** (Litmus/E-mail on Acid-class previews; Mailtester's RTL debugging write-ups) — https://www.litmus.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-web-fonts and https://mailtester.com/blog/debugging-right-to-left-email-display-problems
   Take: the mental model of "render is a claim that must be verified" compressed into our direction/width preview + 10-check pre-flight gate — QA inside the composer instead of a separate testing product. Not taken: standalone testing scope (we don't QA arbitrary HTML), screenshot-matrix UI density, and per-client screenshot promises we cannot keep from a composition tool.

## Original direction (prose sketch, identity-driven)

**The bilingual issue bench.** A warm cream desk (marigold working color, warm-brown ink — 05) where an issue lies open as two columns: Arabic flowing right-to-left, English left-to-right, with quiet alignment links between block twins and sync chips that tell the truth about drift. Above the columns sit locale tabs — the subject-variant sets live there, each variant with its live character budget and a tiny inbox-row preview showing exactly where mobile will cut it. When QA runs, a checklist card fills the right rail with calm, worded statuses — the only saturated color on the screen — and every fail is a jump-line back into the text that caused it. The send desk is two honest shelves: a channel binding that either verified today or didn't, and the export package that is always ready to be "the send" instead. Nothing bounces; the one animated moment is a completed checklist settling like a stamped page. The overall feel: a proofing desk at a bilingual newsroom at 08:50, ten minutes before send — everything checked, nothing hidden.

## Mood anchors

Marigold ink on cream stock · two scripts flowing toward each other · a checklist that means it · the export package sitting as proudly as the send button · calm at deadline.
