## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Tanbih Paper Console: a vigilance instrument on plum-tinted paper — bright Tanbih Magenta-Plum chrome over ink-dense arithmetic rows, red held back as semantic ammunition (≤5% per screen), nothing glowing, nothing glass, no gradient anywhere near the hue. The console home is a queue, not a wall: DetectionRow list items settle into place under the page-class banner's single permitted red rule line, each row carrying its feed name at its own direction inside the opposite chrome, its observed-vs-baseline delta in Western numerals, its tier chip as icon+text+color — the feed-health strip below is three chips per feed, watch chips beneath that, and no gauge anywhere on the surface. The signature surface is the ThresholdExplanationCard: two complete authored sentences — English left-to-right, Arabic right-to-left, each in its own bidi-isolated region with its own language tag — where the number slots (baseline, observed, band edges) carry the magenta-deep emphasis and the "why this baseline" disclosure folds out beneath in readable text; the series context renders as a bullet-band chart whose flat zones edge every threshold with its number in the legend, season windows shaded, the offending point flagged with icon and text — never a pulse. The digest document is a paper artifact: soft-lift panels stacked as a document — header with counts in both languages, the page-class section held at the top of BOTH language sections, per-feed sections with the explanation pairs echoed verbatim (immutable echoes, 07a), the season note when a window is active, and the quiet day rendered as one honest sentence with its own arithmetic ("6 feeds watched, 89 points checked, 0 detections" — 11 §A). The watch editor renders the policy as prose — the same template engine that writes explanations writes the policy's readable sentence, bilingually, with the backtest count as its numeric conscience before save. Motion is energetic but rationed: the one signature moment is The Explained Arrival — a new detection slides 8px along the reading axis and settles with the personality's single capped overshoot (≤8%, 200ms) while the live region announces the same numbers politely; everything else is a crossfade, an opacity step, or a settle — errors arrive, they never bounce.
