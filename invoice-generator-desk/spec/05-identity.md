## Typography (P06 — Almarai + Inter)

- **Arabic: Almarai** (real weights 300/400/700/800 — rtl-rules §3 honored, no synthetic bold): 400 body and document blocks, 700 Arabic headings and the Arabic side of field labels. 300 and 800 deliberately unused — the weight strategy is figure-forward (see below), not display-forward.
- **Latin: Inter** (400/500/600/700): **500 for the money voice** — every figure in the totals rail and the document renders at 500 with tabular figures (`tnum` where supported); 600 for the single H1 per screen and the grand-total line on paper; 400 body. The tabular-figures rule (06/09): money columns are scanned vertically; proportional digits misalign the scan.
- **Weight strategy (composite separation from the P06 co-users)**: Inter 500/600 carries authority; Almarai 400/700 carries chrome. The document's display hierarchy is scale + tabular figures + primary-deep ink, never heavier faces.
- Line-height: Arabic 1.6 / Latin 1.5; tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level, rtl-rules §3). Currency codes ("SAR", "AED") are Latin tokens inside possibly-Arabic lines — bidi-isolated, never force-flipped (rtl-rules §2).
- **Numerals: Western in system chrome** (ledger row; finance/data surface per design-language-map §7): counts, dates, list figures, job counters all 0-9 in both locales; Arabic-Indic glyphs (٠-٩) appear ONLY as document-render specimens — the numeral toggle's own labels and the document preview/export output when the render config selects Arabic-Indic. Storage is canonical Western-digit money; display is formatting (07a).
- One pairing for reading AND chrome (T3 discipline; no third family; no microcopy exception — both families are legible at money-table density).

## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Density B (standard, 6–10px)**: line-item rows 6px, cards and dialogs 8px, document preview pane 10px (the paper reads as a sheet, slightly rounder than the controls that shape it). Chips fully-rounded exception ONLY for removable filter chips (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here).
- **Elevation: flat-line**: hairline slate-tinted borders carry all structure; app chrome separates by border weight and canvas→raised step (0.97→0.99); the document paper separates by the warm-vs-cool surface step — the paper is "elevated" by being different stuff, not by a shadow. Zero shadows; no glass, no blur (rejected below).
- **Icons: line-2** (standard-weight outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary converging with the ledger): file-text (invoice document), list (line items), calculator (totals rail), receipt (payment marks), send (delivery — directional glyph, flips in RTL), printer/download (export/manual path), users (clients), sliders (schemes), hash (number series — constructed as the line-2 "#" on the grid), calendar (due date), check-circle (settled), alert-circle (overdue), slash-circle (voided), and a **constructed numeral-system glyph**: two stacked half-height digit rows ("0123" over "٠١٢٣"-shaped strokes on the line-2 grid — the skill's CSVs ship no numeral-system row, so the glyph is constructed and recorded here). Directional glyphs (send arrow, drill chevrons) flip in RTL; semantically anchored glyphs (document, calculator, receipt, hash, the numeral glyph) never flip (rtl-rules §2). No sparkle, wand, or "smart" glyphs anywhere — declared non-AI product (ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: minimal** (motion-language §1): opacity-only transitions ≤120ms; position changes instant with crossfade; route changes hard-cut or ≤120ms crossfade; modals fade+scale 0.98→1.0 ≤120ms with focus trap and return. Money never moves while being read. **Signature micro-interaction (the one, T3 allowance)**: **the Numeral Flip** — toggling Western ↔ Arabic-Indic re-renders every figure on the document paper in a single ≤120ms opacity crossfade (two render layers swap; no digit-by-digit animation, no counting, no spin), accompanied by a polite announcement ("Arabic-Indic numerals on" / "الأرقام العربية الهندية مفعّلة"). The motion does real work: it dramatizes the product's core guarantee — the same value, different clothes — and the crossfade boundary is exactly the render/storage boundary. Reduced-motion: instant swap + identical announcement (perceivability by construction, motion-language §5). Success moments (issued, delivered, settled) are state chips + polite announcements — no animation-carried meaning (a11y-baseline §4).
