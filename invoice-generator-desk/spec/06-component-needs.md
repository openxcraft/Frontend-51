## Layer-3 token additions

`line.item.row.*` (validation + focus states), `totals.rail.figure.*` (tnum figure voice, breakdown disclosure), `totals.rail.words.*` (amount-in-words block), `numeral.toggle.*` (segmented states + specimen labels), `document.paper.*` (warm paper surface + hairline rules + block directions), `document.block.{seller|client|lines|totals|notes}.*`, `jurisdiction.card.*` (rate/label/flag fields), `series.badge.*` (preview vs seal states), `payment.mark.*` + `payment.state.*`, `due.chip.*` (state variants incl. derived-overdue), `delivery.panel.*` — all Layer 2 re-binds of the 05 palette; no raw values (token-architecture §1).

## Reused-with-twist

- **ConfirmDialog**: P04 level-2 pattern carrying the integrity disclosures — issue names the freeze consequence; void names the consumed-number consequence and requires a reason (never a content-free confirmation; P04's banned generic-confirm rule).
- **QuotaMeter**: exports + channel sends only; the meter is calm by default and the 80% warning is a notice, never a red panic surface (P14).
- **DiffView**: concurrent draft-edit conflicts render as two stacked document-block diffs (per-line amounts + header fields), not character diffs — money conflicts are read in figures.

## Deviations recorded

- Fully-rounded filter chips inside density-B system: permitted exception for removable-chip semantics (P08), declared in 05; all other radii in the B family (document pane 10px upper bound).
- Icon flip decisions (rtl-rules §2): send arrow and drill chevrons flip in RTL; document, calculator, receipt, hash, and the constructed numeral-system glyph never flip (semantic anchors). The numeral glyph's two specimen rows are content, not direction.
- The DocumentPreview's per-block direction is data-driven (field script + render config), not chrome-driven — the one surface in the product whose mirroring follows the artifact, not the app.
