## Motion personality: crisp (motion-language §1)

Durations 100–180ms; standard ease-out; **no overshoot, ever**. Micro-interaction mapping (motion-language §2): hover = affordance discovery (border.interactive emphasis + hairline edge brightening, no scale); press = ≤80ms tactile confirm (scale 0.97→1.0 on chips and controls); focus = visible ring at accent.bright (motion never replaces focus); success = the Anneal Pinch (below); error = arrive-don't-bounce (panels settle; no shake); streaming = narration streams into its reserved region per ai-native-patterns §2 (paragraph chunks under reduced motion); empty→filled = strata settle in place (never spin-in).

### The motion signature — the Anneal Pinch (SME-I original)

Every act of **refinement** in this product lands as a pinch: selecting a seam pinches the cascade's population band to that transition; each admitted predicate pinches the Anneal Spine's population band narrower; applying a filter pinches the whole canvas's basis. The cadence is three phases: **narrow (≤80ms inline-scale of the band width toward its new value, ease-out) → flash (60ms 1px border.interactive edge flash at the band's new boundary) → settle (the count re-renders in Space Mono tabular numerals; nothing loops)**. 140ms total, zero overshoot. The pinch is the product's emotional register made motion: focus tightening on the leak — each question the analyst asks makes the population smaller and the evidence sharper, and the motion says exactly that, once, then rests. It is also the flagship's recognizability contract: an operator who feels one pinch knows every refinement in the product feels identical (motion-language §2 taxonomy honored — one grammar, zero surprises). The counter-motion is the **Stratum Settle**: when the cascade renders or re-queries, each stratum lands with a single 140ms vertical decelerate into its final height (staggered ≤60ms, capped at 5 strata, then simultaneous) — sediment, not confetti.

### Tier-1 motion obligations (motion-language §4) — original proposals delivered

1. **The Cascade** (13 §B1): strata settle in sequence; the lost flow drapes from the seam once per render; seam selection pinches the population band. The motion teaches the funnel's shape by arrival order.
2. **The Anneal Spine** (13 §B2): each admitted predicate pinches the population band; rejected candidates fade to their reason rows; the excess-headcount figure settles last. The motion teaches the greedy search's order by admission order.
3. **The Displaced-Flow Ribbon** (13 §B3): on seam selection, the lost users' next-event strands draw once from the seam toward their ranked paths (≤180ms per strand, staggered ≤60ms, capped at 6 strands, then simultaneous); ranking re-orders with a single settle. The motion teaches "where they went" by direction.

Each proposal states the work its motion does; none is ornamental. Route transitions: crossfade ≤180ms; funnel row → canvas is a shared-element intent on the stratum stack; sheets scale 0.96→1.0 + fade 140ms with focus trap and return (a11y-baseline §3). Stagger ≤60ms capped at 5 elements everywhere.

## Brand-distance check (LOOP-11 — mandatory for this product)

**Blocklist trigger**: ledger §6 names "chartreuse-on-observability-dark" as BLOCKLISTED for funnel-drop-off-explorer — the composite must not read as an existing observability brand. **Nearest famous neighbors**: (a) **dark-canvas observability platforms** — Grafana-class dashboards (dark chrome, orange/heat-map accents, panel grids), Datadog-class (dark + purple/violet brand marks), New Relic-class (dark + teal-green), Honeycomb-class (dark + honey/amber), Sentry-class (dark + violet): the entire family shares our theme polarity and the "bright accent on near-black" strategy; (b) **terminal/hacker culture** — the #7FFF00-class neon chartreuse of movie terminals and prompt themes: the loudest version of our hue; (c) **developer-tool dark apps** (editors, notebooks: dark + single bright accent). **Distance strategy, axis by axis**: (1) **hue system** — our chartreuse is L 0.82 C 0.16 at hue 105: a restrained olive-chartreuse, NOT neon (#7FFF00-class runs far higher perceptual saturation and yellow-shifted hue), and it is paired with a CHROMA-CARRYING blue-tinged abyss (oklch 0.22 0.05 212) — no observability neighbor pairs chartreuse with a blue-tinged chroma canvas (they sit on near-neutral blacks or blue-blacks with warm accents: Grafana orange, Datadog purple, New Relic teal — none is chartreuse); the accent is further discipline-limited to ≤5% of screen + the hero data series, where observability brands splash their accent across chart elements, nav, and marketing. (2) **shape** — A-radius cut everywhere vs the standard-rounded panel vocabulary of every neighbor. (3) **elevation** — ink-press lightness steps + edge highlights, no glow — vs the neighbors' glow-on-dark hover and neon data strokes. (4) **typography** — P14 Rubik + Space Mono: absent from the neighbor set (they run neutral grotesks or custom sans; none pairs a rounded-quirk sans with a mono for data tokens). (5) **iconography** — line-2 single-tone vs the neighbors' duotone/colored icon systems. (6) **motion** — crisp pinch cadence with zero loops vs observability's pulsing/status-blinking animation grammar (our sources never pulse — freshness is a text+icon chip). (7) **posture** — an exploration canvas with a bilingual AR/EN taxonomy and governed dictionary semantics has no observability-brand equivalent at all: the composite reads as a precision instrument for growth teams, not a monitoring console. Verified: no flagged similarity risk remains; the nearest single-axis overlaps (dark theme, single bright accent, mono-for-data) are each resolved by the other six axes.
