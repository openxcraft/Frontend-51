## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Next/previous-theme chevrons (board traversal): directional — flip. External-link arrow (artifact links): flips. Send (n/a — no send surface; recorded as not-applicable). Git-branch merge glyph: semantically anchored (branch topology is direction-free) — never flips. Tag, flag, message-circle, thumbs-up/down, bookmark (pin), bar-chart, eye, clipboard, search, filter, users, globe: semantically anchored — never flip. Quote-spine tint bar: it is a background edge tied to `inline-start` (logical property), so it *relocates* with direction rather than flipping — a token-level `inset-inline-start` binding, recorded here. Pagination/step indicators mirror per rtl-rules §2; respondent refs and counts keep physical order stable (rtl-rules §4).
