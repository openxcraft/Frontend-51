## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Ink Ledger.** A mining instrument drawn on cool lavender-tinted paper — canvas the color of #F5F4F8-class calm, one muted identity hue (Mashour Lavender, L0.70 C0.06) working only as tint: selection washes, chip fills, the pinned-quote wash — never text, never a button. The theme board is a ledger of claims: ThemeCards lift one diffuse shadow step off the paper (soft-lift, radius C at 14px), each card headed by its bilingual pair — the Arabic label flowing right-to-left, its Latin counterpart leftward, each in its own bidi-isolated slot, announced together — with the member count and share set in Western tabular figures beneath, the provenance chip in icon + text (`keyword-grouping` / `model-proposed` + confidence band / `human-confirmed` + actor), and a preview quote below the fold. The provisional themes wear the dashed lavender-line border; the confirmed wear solid deep — the border IS the state, and the state is never color alone. The quote wall stands beside the board as the evidence layer on raised paper: every verbatim at its own detected script direction, a quote-mark teal spine down its inline-start edge (teal so quotes read as from the data, not from the UI), computed quotes carrying their printed rationale, pinned quotes carrying Dana's name. The coverage strip runs beneath in text first — "239 assigned · 41 unassigned · 17 in multiple themes" — with the two honesty lines in writing, because shares that sum past 100% are the truth and the ledger says so. The response reader is the tail-work workhorse: virtualized rows of excerpt + detection chips on the bed band, the reading pane rendering the full verbatim at its own direction with the marker panel naming its evidence; the intake preview runs detection row-by-row before anything commits. The crosstab console crosses themes × metadata with count + share text and tint as redundancy; the crosstab and the reader meet at the drill — evidence one click from every cell. The confirm gate is a bordered preview that names its exclusions before the analyst spends her name on it, and the one moment the product allows itself is **The Ink Sets**: the dashed border crossfades to solid deep while the seal fades in beneath the labels — 240ms, one settle, zero overshoot — ink setting on paper. Everything else is stillness: 200ms fades, opacity steps, no scale on hover, nothing bounces, and the EngineDisclosurePanel waits one click away — the methods section, in-product, always.
