## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: cards and panes 8px; queue rows and ledger rows 6px; chips fully-rounded ONLY as the removable filter-chip exception (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here).
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: diffuse ambient shadows — raised surfaces carry `0 1px 2px` ambient + `0 4px 12px` low-opacity diffuse (token-bound, never raw values); hover lifts one step; the modal lifts two. No layered occlusion, no ink-press chrome — the console breathes; borders stay hairline rosewood-tinted for structure, shadows for depth.
- **Icons: duotone** (two-tone constructed set; glyph vocabulary from the Lucide-class outline catalog — clock, moon, send, mail, message-circle, bell, ban, rotate-ccw, receipt, alert-triangle, filter, layers, languages, calendar-clock): the two-tone rendering is the ledger's binding style — solid primary layer + rosewood-wash secondary layer; the catalog supplies semantics, the duotone construction supplies the identity (full record in 06; the skill's outline-only rows are a recorded partial adoption, §integration record).
- **Motion: energetic** (motion-language §1): 150–250ms, standard easing with a **single controlled overshoot ≤8% reserved for success moments** — and in this product exactly one moment may use it (below). Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; queue rows settle in place (nothing spins in); deferred badges arrive with a 160ms amber settle (arrival always accompanied by the ledger's live-region text — motion never carries information alone); errors arrive flat — no shake, no bounce, ever.
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Cart Comes Back")**: when a checkout-completed event lands on a cart, the queue row's state chip flips to the recovered seal — a duotone stamp glyph with the cart total — with the personality's one controlled overshoot (≤8%, 220ms, ease-out), and the queue header's recovered-revenue counter counts up by the cart total in tabular figures over ≤400ms. Reduced-motion: the seal renders instantly, the counter jumps, and the announcement carries the full summary ("Cart 005 recovered — SAR 349.00. 3 of 14 carts recovered this window"). Nothing else in the product expresses the overshoot; nothing else counts up.
