## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Clock, moon, bell, ban, filter, layers, languages, calendar-clock, alert-triangle, check, receipt: semantically anchored — never flip. Send / mail glyphs on dispatch actions and next-send indicators: directional — flip. Next/previous chevrons in queue and ledger pagination: directional — flip. External-link arrow (policy source labels): flips. DiffView insertion/deletion arrows (template revisions): directional — flip, with the diff content itself at its own script direction (P16). The recovered seal stamp: symmetric composite glyph — never flips. Timeline node connectors: vertical — direction-free.
