## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Rosewood Outbox.** A scheduling instrument in one working rosewood line on cool paper — soft-lift shadows, hairline rosewood-tinted borders, radius B, duotone icons whose second tone is always the wash, never a second hue. The queue is a ranked worklist, not a dashboard: rows like ledger entries, each carrying its number in tabular figures (rank, total, the clock), a band chip, a locale glyph, a state chip — and when a send defers, an amber moon settles beside the time with its reason one focus away; the row never repaints, it annotates. The signature object is the **QuietClockTimeline**: one horizontal 24h band per sequence step, the sendable window washed rosewood, the quiet span paper, the due-time marker a solid tick, and when the clock says no, an arrow from the due time to the window's next opening with the reason typeset beside it in amber-deep — the jurisdiction row and its source label ride under the band so the operator always knows WHICH rule moved their message and whether it was statute or their own policy. The template editor is the desk's second instrument: two columns facing each other across a hairline seam — English flowing leftward, Arabic rightward, alignment links keystone'd between corresponding blocks — with length rulers etched under each column and variable chips that insert at the caret of whichever side holds focus. The send ledger is the compliance outbox: every message the workspace ever intended to send, its state in icon + text + color, its deferrals with their pinned policy versions, its failures with their retry paths — an outbox a merchant could hand an auditor without a word of explanation. And exactly one moment moves more than once: **the cart comes back** — the state chip flips to the recovered seal with a single controlled overshoot, the header's recovered-revenue counter counts up in tabular figures, and then the console goes still again, because the next cart in the rank is already waiting for a human.
