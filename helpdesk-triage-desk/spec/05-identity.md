## Typography (P02 — Noto Kufi Arabic + Space Grotesk)

- **Arabic: Noto Kufi Arabic** (weights bound: 400/500/700 — named instances only, no synthetic bold per rtl-rules §3; the family's wider variable axis exists but the spec binds these three): 400 body in Arabic ticket bodies and ar-locale chrome; 500 queue/cluster labels and row metadata; 700 the single H1 per screen and cluster labels on cards. Kufi's geometric, low-join-density letterforms are the point: on dense triage rows, kufi reads as constructed and grid-made (the pairing's temperament, design-language-map §3) and survives 15px row heights without the ascender collisions of taller naskh — a deliberate fit for a data console, recorded here as the pairing rationale rather than an accident of the ledger. Body ≥16px in ticket detail; 15px floor in queue rows; line-height 1.6 (tashkeel-bearing customer text gets the ≥1.8 reservation when diacritics appear — rtl-rules §3); tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level obligation).
- **Latin: Space Grotesk** (400/500/700): 400 body; 500 queue headers, severity badges, SLA countdown figures; 700 H1 and the ticket-number mark. Space Grotesk's constructed grotesque voice is the Latin half of P02's geometric-tech temperament — a console, not a brochure.
- **Tabular-figures rule**: every count, minute, hour, percentage, and ticket number renders tabular figures (`tnum`) — SLA countdowns and queue counts are scanned vertically; proportional digits misalign the scan (the skill's mono-for-data instinct adopted as a rule, not a third family).
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; data surface per design-language-map §7): severity tiers, SLA minutes/hours, counts, ticket numbers, dates all 0-9 in both locales — including inside Arabic chrome strings (never mixed systems mid-sentence, copy-voice §1.7). Arabic-Indic digits appear ONLY inside verbatim ticket text (customer content preserved as evidence, 07a) — never in system chrome.
- Ticket references ("TK-0142") are Latin-digit tokens inside possibly-Arabic chrome — bidi-isolated containers, never force-flipped (rtl-rules §2).

## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: queue rows and cluster cards 8px; chips and badges 6px; removable filter chips fully-rounded ONLY as the P08 declared exception; dialogs 10px.
- **Elevation: flat-line**: hairline cobalt-tinted borders carry all structure; ticket detail is the "deepest" surface by border weight + the raised paper step alone; zero shadows anywhere (including focus — focus is the ring, not a glow). The tint step (canvas → raised → cobalt tint) is the only surface hierarchy and it prints legibly in grayscale.
- **Icons: line-2** (standard 2px-stroke outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary per the skill's icons.csv): inbox (intake), languages (language chip), layers/git-branch (clusters), gauge (severity), route/split (routing), clock (SLA), flame (breached), bell (escalation), check-circle (met/closed), merge (duplicate merge), archive (spam/disposition), users (queues/members), table (policy matrix), plug (channel binding). Directional glyphs (queue chevrons, next/previous arrows, external-link) flip in RTL; semantically anchored glyphs (inbox, clock, gauge, merge, languages) never flip (rtl-rules §2; full decisions in 06). **No sparkle/wand glyphs on the clustering assist** — proposals are labeled with the violet ink and a plain "proposed" word, not magic affordances (conditionally-AI discipline, ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, **zero overshoot ever**. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; queue rows settle in place (nothing spins in); cluster proposals arrive 120ms fade+settle; the confirm-gate flip (violet → cobalt-confirmed) is a 140ms state change always accompanied by the live-region text (a11y-baseline §4 — motion never carries information alone); SLA countdown digits never animate (they are data, not spectacle — the anti-count-up rule). Press feedback ≤80ms tactile. Reduced-motion: hard cuts + announcements, identical perceivability (motion-language §5).
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Handoff Settle")**: on route commit (CP-3.2 — the product's single most important action: a ticket leaving the triage desk for its queue), the ticket row's queue chip stamps in (scale 0.96→1.0, 140ms) and the row slides one slot toward its queue grouping and settles (160ms ease-out, no overshoot), while the destination queue's count ticks once in tabular figures and the polite announcement lands ("Routed to Payments by rule R-04 — first response due 12:40"). Reduced-motion: the chip and row render instantly in place, the count updates, and the announcement carries the full decision. Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the crisp standard set.
