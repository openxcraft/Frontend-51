## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius A (cut, 0–4px)**: panels and drawers 2px; table rows 0px (ruled lines only); inputs, buttons, chips 2px. Cut shapes read as measurement instruments — the console equivalent of caption-lab's edit-bay squares, on the opposite canvas. No fully-rounded exception is declared: even removable filter chips (P08) render cut at 2px in this family, recorded here as the A-family chip posture.
- **Elevation: flat-line**: hairline cyan-tinted borders carry all structure; the canvas→raised step (0.97→0.99) separates the row table from the summary header; the summary header is the "deepest" surface by border weight alone; zero shadows anywhere; focus is the ring, never a glow.
- **Icons: line-2** (standard 2px stroke outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary): sigma (rules), sliders (scope), percent (margin), banknote (price), calendar (validity window), **arrow-down-to-line (the cost floor — semantically anchored: it depicts the value axis's bottom, NOT reading direction, so it never flips in RTL — the decision recorded here per rtl-rules §2)**, trending-up/trending-down (price direction — value-semantics glyphs, never flip), triangle-alert (clamp), check-circle (approval), clipboard (paste imports), file-down (export), history (cost timeline), play (simulation run — media convention, never flips). Chevrons in table pagination and drawer disclosure flip in RTL (navigation-direction class). No sparkle, wand, or "smart" glyphs anywhere — declared non-AI product (ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, **zero overshoot ever** (price rules are timing-critical work — the personality the catalog assigns this exact use case). Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; row focus highlight <80ms; clamp chips arrive 120ms fade+settle; formula drawers open 140ms with focus trap and return (a11y-baseline §3); nothing bounces, nothing celebrates a save.
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Clamp Settles")**: when a floor-clamped simulation row first renders (arrival in the table, or entering the floor-clamped filter), the row's raw rule price is struck through and the **final price settles into place over 140ms ease-out beside the floor stamp**, with the amber clamp chip fading in (120ms) and the polite live-region line "Row clamped at floor 96.14 — raw rule price 84.50" carrying the same information as text (a11y-baseline §4 — motion never carries information alone). Reduced-motion: instant strike-through + chip + announcement, identical perceivability. This is the product's single most important moment — the visible proof that the floor is a clamp, not a warning (00) — and nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the crisp standard set.
