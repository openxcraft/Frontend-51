## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Mizan Bench.** A scale-maker's workbench drawn in one Sarwa Cyan line — flat hairline borders on cool paper, cut 2px corners, zero shadows, zero glass. The rules console reads as a bench of set squares: the rule list a ruled index of instruments, each row carrying its priority, its validity window, and its scope sentence in ink — the Ramadan markdown sits beside the White Friday rehearsal, inert-by-time badged honestly when its window has passed. The rule editor is the bench's marking gauge: condition and action pickers on one side, and the FormulaPanel beside them like a stamped brass tag of arithmetic — "net = landed cost ÷ (1 − 0.38); round to the whole riyal; clamp at the floor" in tabular Space Grotesk, re-stamping itself within 120ms as every field changes, a worked example from a live seeded item underneath. The simulation workspace is the balance itself: the summary header as the beam — one hero figure per market, "KSA 31.4% → 33.9%", set 700 in primary-ink with the catalog-weighted label permanently riveted to it, counts hanging off the beam as icon+text+color chips (3 clamped, amber; directions in ink); below, the weighing plate — a virtualized field of ruled rows where every figure is tabular, price direction renders as signed ink numerals (a markdown is often a good decision; the scale does not editorialize), margin judgment as green and red points, and the VAT-inclusive display pair sits beside every net like the counterweight — "display 113.85 SAR incl. VAT 15%" — the number the shopper sees, previewed beside the number the lead sets. The clamp is the balance's hard stop: when a rule would price below the floor, the raw price is struck through and the final settles into place beside the amber floor stamp over 140ms — **"The Clamp Settles"** — with the same sentence spoken politely to the screen reader, because a stop you cannot hear is a stop that did not happen. The proposal is a manifest: rows with include and exclude marks, every exclusion carrying its reason in the record's own voice; the gate refuses while any row is unreviewed — naming the row, never the count alone — and when it clears, the approval seal lands as a bordered block, primary-ink on raised: actor, timestamp, counts, frozen. Then the package exports, and the bench goes quiet. Everything is crisp: drawers open in 140ms with focus held and returned, chips arrive in 120ms, rows highlight in under 80ms, nothing bounces, nothing counts up, nothing celebrates — the scale's whole personality is that it does not move when it is right.
