## Layer-3 token additions

`appointment.block.state.*` (six lifecycle chip states per 05 contracts), `appointment.block.flagged.*`, `slot.picker.free|selected|conflict.*`, `waitlist.offer.pending|expiring|accepted|declined.*`, `waitlist.row.penalty.*` (the disclosed penalty input), `ladder.step.*` (seven step states), `flag.chip.pattern.*`, `summary.count.display.*` (the large-text primary exception), `policy.rule.value.*` (value+explanation pairs) — all Layer 2 re-binds of the 05 palette; no raw values anywhere (token-architecture §1).

## Reused-with-twist

- **EmptyState**: S1 first-run loads the interactive sample day (07c seed) so the cancellation→fill moment is experienceable pre-patients (P22; 03 non-happy path).
- **FilterChips**: state + provider + locale filters on board/waitlist/outbox; fully-rounded chip exception recorded in 05 (P08 semantics).
- **JobRow**: consolidated T3 jobs console (message batches, fill runs, exports) with link-to-context (async-contract §7).

## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2 — recorded per component)

Flip in RTL: week-stepper arrows (DaySummaryBar), send glyph (WaitlistPanel, LadderTimeline), "next provider" chevron in the 360px collapse, external-link arrow. Never flip (semantic anchors): calendar, clock, bell (reminder), flag (no-show pattern), phone, printer, checkmark-seal, list-with-plus. The phone glyph's handset orientation follows the constructed-grid default and is recorded as direction-free.
