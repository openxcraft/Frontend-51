## Typography (P15 — Mada + Instrument Sans)

- **Arabic: Mada** (real weights 400/500/700) — contemporary-grotesk Arabic with a low-kashida temperament: reads as an operational console face, not a poster. Body 400 at ≥15px equivalent in dense surfaces (patient names, outbox rows), 16px+ in policy explanations; 500 for field labels and chips; 700 reserved for the day-ruler times and appointment-block patient names (the board's load-bearing strings).
- **Latin: Instrument Sans** (400/500/600/700) — 500 for labels/chips, 400 body, 600 for section headers; the single H1 per screen at 700. Patient names render at their own direction inside bidi-isolated containers (rtl-rules §2) — a Latin name in an RTL column is never force-flipped.
- **Weight strategy (the P15 co-user differentiator, LOOP-2 — the pairing's 4th and final slot)**: console-steady — 400/500 working text with 700 display ONLY on the time ruler and block names; no 600 in Arabic (Mada lacks it — re-bind to 500 or 700 per rtl-rules §3, no synthetic bold). Distinct from seo-brief-builder's 500-headers/600-H1 analyst posture, field-service-dispatch's heavier 700-forward display strategy, and okr-cascade-board's energetic D-radius composites.
- Line-height: Arabic 1.6 body / 1.4 display; Latin 1.5 / 1.2; tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level, rtl-rules §3 — a build rejection, not a preference). Tashkeel headroom: patient-facing outbound templates are unvocalized MSA (copy-voice §3); the board reserves line-box room where seeded names carry diacritics.
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; data surface per design-language-map §7): times, durations, counts, phone numbers, offer-expiry countdowns all 0-9 everywhere, including inside Arabic prose (rtl-rules §3 mixed rule — storage canonical, display formatted). Tabular-figure settings where the implementation supports them (the mono-for-data instinct from the skill record, adopted as a rule, not a font).

## Shape, elevation, iconography

- **Radius C (soft, 12–16px)**: appointment blocks 12px, cards and drawer 14px, day-summary chips 12px; the waitlist offer card (the product's one object-quality surface) 16px. Declared deviation: removable filter chips render fully-rounded (P08 chip semantics — the same exception the portfolio's density-B siblings record); all other radii stay in the C family.
- **Elevation: soft-lift** — diffuse ambient shadows only. The day board is flat canvas (a working surface, not a stack); the appointment drawer lifts one step; the waitlist offer card carries the deepest soft-lift (the moment the product exists for — 05 motion). No hard drop shadows, no borders-as-structure (that is the flat-line family's grammar), no glass.
- **Icons: filled-rounded** (friendly solid set): calendar glyph (appointment), clock (time ruler), phone (call-script path), chat-bubble (outbound message), checkmark-seal (confirmed), flag (no-show pattern — the semantic anchor), list-with-plus (waitlist), bell (reminder), printer (day sheet). Directional glyphs (next/prev week arrows, send glyph) flip in RTL; semantically anchored glyphs (calendar, bell, flag, phone, printer) never flip (rtl-rules §2 — per-component decisions recorded in 06). No sparkle glyphs anywhere — the product is declared non-AI and leaks no magic affordances (ai-native-patterns §1).

## Motion (calm — motion-language §1)

200–320ms, symmetric ease-in-out, no scale change on hover (opacity/border only), zero overshoot ever. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; the appointment drawer settles in place; blocks never bounce; flags arrive, they never animate-in aggressively. Hover band adopted at 200–300ms (the skill checklist's 150–300ms overlap with calm's floor).

**The ONE signature micro-interaction (T3 discipline: exactly one) — the Slot Refill**: when a waitlist offer is accepted (`respondWaitlistOffer` → booked), the open slot's dashed outline fills as the appointment block settles into place — a 280ms ease-in-out opacity-and-border-weight change, no translation, no scale — while the day-summary count updates in the same frame and a polite announcement fires ("تمت تعبئة الموعد من قائمة الانتظار — تبقى 4 طلبات"). The offer card itself lifts one soft step on accept. Reduced-motion: the refill renders as a ≤2-step opacity change with the same announcement and the same count update — identical perceivability by construction (a11y-baseline §4; 09). Everything else is standard-set: check-in is a state chip; no-show declaration is a confirm then a chip; ladder updates are text deltas.
