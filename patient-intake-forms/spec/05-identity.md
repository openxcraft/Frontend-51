## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius C (soft, 12–16px)**: cards and panes 16px; inputs 12px (the sunken "paper tray" wells); chips fully-rounded ONLY as the removable filter-chip exception (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here). The fill surface's question cards run 16px — softness is the care register, and the radius is where it lives.
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: one diffuse ambient shadow step above raised for cards and the action bar; the fill surface stacks depth by surface lightness (canvas → sunken wells → raised cards), never by shadow stacks. Focus is the ring, never a glow.
- **Icons: line-2** (standard 2px stroke outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary per the skill's icons.csv — clipboard for intake, user/users for patient/staff, globe for locale pair, activity for symptoms, flag for urgent marks, check-circle for confirmations, link for fill sessions, download for exports, search, filter, arrow-left/right for flow navigation — the directional pair flips in RTL, recorded in 06). No sparkle glyphs anywhere except the single assist-suggestion mark, which is a bordered "suggested" chip with TEXT, not a sparkle icon (the conditionally-AI surface stays visually sober — ai-native-patterns §1 discipline: no magic affordances).
- **Motion: calm** (motion-language §1): 200–320ms, symmetric ease-in-out, no scale change on hover (opacity/border only), nothing bounces, nothing spins in. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; question-to-question advance is a 240ms slide along the reading axis (inline-start reveal in RTL); suggestion chips arrive at 220ms fade+settle with the live-region equivalent (09). Errors arrive — they never shake (motion-language §2). Reduced-motion: hard cuts + announcements, identical perceivability (09).
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Calm Hand-off")**: on the patient's attested submit (03 CP-2.9 — the product's single most important action), the one-question-per-screen flow settles into the confirmation card: the progress spine fills to its end, the answered question cards fold gently into a single summary card (280ms ease-in-out, stagger ≤60ms capped at 5 cards — calm's band, zero overshoot), and the clinic's bilingual name typesets at rest with the next-step line. The motion does one job: it closes the anxious loop — the patient watches her work become a kept record. Reduced-motion: the card renders instantly and the polite announcement carries the section counts (counts only — no PHI in announcements, 09). Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the calm standard set; the urgent flag ARRIVES without motion emphasis beyond the amber/red ink (motion never carries urgency — text and icons do).
