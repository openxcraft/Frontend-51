## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Sage Clipboard.** A clipboard drawn in sage, softened at every corner. The patient surface is one warm card at a time on paper-toned canvas — the question typeset large in Harmattan (18px, line-height 1.7, the register of a form read aloud to a parent), the input resting in a sunken well like the tray a receptionist slides across a counter, the progress spine a quiet column of sage dots filling one by one toward the consent line. When she types her symptoms the way she says them, the suggestion chips rise beneath the well — each a small paper tag carrying its canonical pair (صداع — headache) and its receipt (matched by normalization) — and the tag she takes leaves her own words visible underneath, the ink never overwritten, the match doing the bending. The staff console is the same paper, ruled: submissions queue as a ledger of states and counts, the normalization panel a two-column proof sheet — her text on one side, the canonical term and its mode on the other, indigo where the matcher proposes and waits, red ink only where the clinic's own vocabulary said urgent — and the review gate a simple bar that refuses, in plain Arabic and plain English, until every proposal has met a human yes. The one moment the product allows itself: at submit, the question cards fold gently into a single confirmation card — the Calm Hand-off — the clinic's bilingual name typeset at rest, the counts she can retell at the desk, nothing bouncing, nothing sparkling, everything kept exactly as she wrote it. Then the desk moves to the next intake.
