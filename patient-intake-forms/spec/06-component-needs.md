## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Flow back/next chevrons and arrow-left/right (question navigation): directional — flip. Link-copy glyph (external-link arrow on fill cards): flips. Progress-spine direction: mirrors by layout, not glyph. Clipboard, user, users, globe, activity, heart, flag, check-circle, search, filter, download, log-in: semantically anchored or symmetric — never flip. The printed-QR glyph: symmetric — never flips (a QR is direction-free by construction). Send (escalation note submit): directional — flips.
