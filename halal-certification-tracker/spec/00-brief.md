## What it is

A certificate-workflow workspace for halal certification bodies: the operating desk a certification body (CB) runs its halal program on — applicant intake, application cases that walk a typed stage ladder, ingredient and formulation review, supplier-certificate evidence, audit-outcome records, committee decisions, issued certificates, and the renewal engine that keeps a register of live certificates from ever lapsing silently. The buyer is the certification body itself (not the food manufacturer — that applicant-side market is served by different tools, 01), and the product's grammar is the CB's own: an application is a case with stages; a certificate is an issued record with a scheme, a scope of products, and an expiry; a decision is a committee act with a named member and a date; and every consequential mutation lands in an append-only activity log because the body answers to its accreditation (ISO/IEC 17065-class obligations, research digest §C) for exactly this record-keeping.

The unique feature is **ingredient-to-certificate traceability with renewal ladders**, two deterministic mechanisms that interlock. Traceability: every certified product carries a versioned formulation, and every formulation line points at a master ingredient record (status: حلال halal / حرام haram / مشبوه mashbooh / قيد المراجعة under-review — the trichotomy-plus the category actually works in, research digest §B) and, where required, at the supplier certificate that evidences it. Because the links are data, impact is a graph walk, not a research project: a gelatin supplier's certificate that expires in 21 days resolves — deterministically, same state in, same result out — to the three products, two issued certificates, and two applicants it puts at risk, ranked by days to expiry. A reclassified ingredient (mashbooh → haram) walks the same graph and names every certificate now carrying a non-compliant formulation. Renewal ladders: a certificate's renewal is not one deadline but a ladder of staged rungs computed from scheme policy — policy the body configures per scheme, with defaults modeled on the sourced conventions of the trade (renewal applications six months before expiry on Gulf-scheme certificates, 45 days on general schemes, three months on four-year certificates — 01), each rung a date derived from the certificate's own expiry, each rung climbing states (upcoming → reached → cleared, or missed) as the body does the work. The register reads in urgency zones derived from the ladder, so "which certificates need action this month" is a computed answer, never a memory test.

The product is **declared non-AI** (ai-native-patterns.md §1, explicit declaration): no operation generates, scores, clusters, or transforms content by model. The traceability graph is deterministic set-arithmetic over typed links; the ladder engine is date arithmetic over versioned policy; the issuance gate is a typed refusal naming unresolved formulation lines. There are no smart anything, no suggestion affordances, no quota ladders for capabilities that do not exist — and the specs must not leak AI affordances anywhere (07a/07b honor the declaration by construction; 08's degradation map is correspondingly trivial and says so). What the body buys is exactly this determinism: an assessor's formulation review and a director's register answer must be reproducible, auditable, and boring in the specific way accreditation assessors and import inspectors expect paperwork to be.

## Buyer

Halal certification bodies operating in and around the MENA region — the Gulf-zone bodies certifying against Gulf food standards, national-scheme bodies, and smaller independent Islamic certification organizations issuing export-accepted certificates: typically 3–25 staff (assessors, technical reviewers, a shariah committee that meets periodically, a program manager or director). The economic buyer is the director or program manager who answers when a certificate lapses unnoticed, when an accreditation audit finds the register in spreadsheets, or when an importer calls to verify a certificate the body cannot quickly lay hands on. Daily users are the assessors who review formulations and evidence, and the program manager who runs the renewal pipeline. Committee members are occasional decision users, not daily drivers; verification queries from trade parties are served by exports the staff generate, not by a public surface (LOOP-10). Per-seat pricing for staff seats with free viewer seats; register-size tiers (12 Q-3). Full personas in 02-user-research.md.

## Surface

Web application, desktop-first (≥1280px primary: register grid, application workspace, formulation editor, certificate detail with ladder timeline), responsive to 360px in review and committee-decision modes. Zones: register home (certificate grid + urgency digest), applicants directory, application workspace (stage ladder, formulation tab, evidence vault, audit records, decision), ingredient directory, supplier-certificate vault, certificate detail (renewal ladder + traceability graph), renewal console (cross-certificate rung digest), ladder-policy editor, activity log, jobs console, settings. ⌘K command palette (P05 — the register, applicants, ingredients, and certificates exceed three list surfaces). No mobile app; no public web portal of any kind at v1 (consumer-facing registry is excluded, LOOP-10); no public API at v1.

## Unique feature

Ingredient-to-certificate traceability with renewal ladders, defensible on three grounds (01 evidence): (1) **no verified competitor joins the ingredient graph to the certificate register as one data spine** — certification-body platforms (Certifable-class, Intact-class) manage applications, audits, and certificates as workflow objects with documents attached, but formulations and supplier certificates are attachments to read, not linked data to walk; the impact question ("what does this supplier's expiring certificate touch?") is answered by a person opening files (digest §D). (2) **renewal ladders as computed policy data do not exist in the verified landscape** — deadline columns in spreadsheets and expiry reminders in email are the status quo (the spreadsheet-template ecosystem is itself a competitor class, 01); the sourced conventions prove the trade thinks in staged windows (six months, three months, 45 days) that no product productizes as per-scheme ladders with rung states. (3) **the bilingual obligation is structural, not cosmetic** — Gulf food labels require Arabic with English alongside (digest §E), the body's certificates and register must read in both scripts, and applicant legal identities exist in both; every identity-bearing field is authored bilingual, rendered per-side direction, and searchable through Arabic-aware normalization (rtl-rules.md §4).

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)

- **No lab testing management** — laboratory results (composition, DNA, alcohol-residue tests) arrive as evidence documents attached to applications and ingredients; the product manages no lab orders, sample chains, test workflows, or results interpretation. Testing belongs to laboratories.
- **No audit scheduling** — the product records audit *outcomes* (report, findings, non-conformity closure) as case events, but owns no auditor calendars, no booking, no route planning, no auditor assignment boards. The boundary: scheduling is when the audit happens; recording is what it found. Scheduling tools are a different buy.
- **No consumer-facing registry** — no public lookup site, no QR-to-public-page feature, no logo-verification portal for shoppers. The consumer verification market (scanner apps, 01) is excluded; the body's verification answer at v1 is an export artifact a staff member generates.
- No halal status verdicting of products for consumers (that is the scanner-app class); ingredient status classification here is the CB's internal master data for its own certification decisions.
- No Shariah fatwa authoring or research tooling — the committee records decisions and justifications; the product does not draft rulings.
- No financials: no invoicing the applicant, no fee tracking beyond optional cost fields on cases, no accounting. The body's fee schedule is its own business.
- No multi-workspace federation at v1 (one workspace per certification body; accreditation-body views are export artifacts, 12 Q-13).

## Success signals (Mock-Adapter Definition-of-Done)

A certification-body user can, in one first session on the Mock Adapter with zero provider-specific knowledge anywhere in the UI: register an applicant with bilingual legal names; open an application case and watch the stage ladder; paste an ingredient list into the directory (import job with row-count progress) and see Arabic normalization in search; build a product formulation with per-line status resolution visible; attach a supplier certificate and watch its expiry derive; run the impact analysis from an expiring gelatin supplier certificate and read the exact affected products, certificates, and applicants; resolve a mashbooh line with a committee justification; record an audit outcome; hit the issuance gate once with the named unresolved line (typed refusal), resolve it, and issue the certificate; open the certificate's renewal ladder and read the computed rungs with states; open a renewal case before the submission rung's date; suspend a certificate with a reason and read the activity log entry for every mutation above; and export the bilingual register before artifact expiry. The full loop must complete with every abstract capability unbound (document-storage optional, notification channel optional, delivery channel optional — 08): metadata-mode documents, in-product digests, downloaded artifacts (LOOP-5 bar).
