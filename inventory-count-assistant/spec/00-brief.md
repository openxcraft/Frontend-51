# 00 — Brief: inventory-count-assistant

Family: business_ops · Tier: 3 · Roster status: researched proposal, pending owner confirmation · Seed: none supplied.

## What it is

A guided cycle-count assistant for MENA store managers. The product runs **count sessions**: a manager opens a session over a defined scope (a cycle-count slice of overdue items, a zone, or the full store), staff walk the floor and the backroom recording counted quantities — scan or type, on phone or tablet — and the session ends in a **variance reconciliation** that shows exactly where the physical store and the book record disagree, by how much, and whether that disagreement is inside the tolerance the store pre-agreed. The core arithmetic is deterministic: variance is a subtraction, tolerance is a declared band, the recount trigger is a rule — the same count state always yields the same variance result, which is what an operational QA artifact requires. The product is declared **non-AI** (ai-native-patterns.md §1): no operation generates, scores, or transforms content by model; there are no smart anything, and the spec must not leak AI affordances.

Cycle counting is the method the product productizes (research digest A: NetSuite/DCL — continuous counts of small preselected sections instead of one or two store-closing physical counts a year). An **ABC cadence engine** keeps the counting honest over time: items carry an A/B/C class computed from their share of stock value, each class carries a count-interval policy (A items counted monthly-class frequency, B quarterly-class, C half-yearly-class — editable, never statutory), and the engine proposes the next slice as the items whose interval is most overdue. **Blind counting** is a first-class mode: in a blind session the counter sees the item and its location but never the book quantity, and the adapter itself withholds expected quantities until the manager ends counting and reveals variances — the discipline professional count crews run (digest A: Champion Business Solutions, FCB — quantity and value withheld from counters to remove anchoring). Directed mode shows the book quantity beside each entry for controlled recounts and zone checks. **Variance tolerance** is policy data, not a magic threshold: per policy the store declares an absolute-unit tolerance and/or a percentage tolerance, combined by a declared band strategy, and any variance outside the band triggers a recount request automatically; a recount that still lands outside hands the item to the manager for an explicit resolution — accept the count (post an adjustment), keep the book, or open an investigation note.

The buyer's reality (01 evidence): the status quo is a paper sheet and a spreadsheet — count the stock, type the numbers, cross-reference the sales, repeat — and counting frequency collapses precisely because manual counting costs a closing night. The tools that exist are Latin-first, suite-shaped (order management, purchasing, demand forecasting bundled around the count module), and per-seat priced in ways that tax the seasonal counter who only needs to count. Nothing in the verified landscape treats the **bilingual count sheet** as a first-class object: Gulf retail labeling is Arabic/English by default (digest E), counters are Arabic-first or English-first floor staff, and a count sheet that renders كل صنف in both scripts — with Arabic-normalized search (alef variants, taa marbuta, yaa forms per rtl-rules §4) — is not served by any verified competitor. This product ends at the counted truth and its handoff: book quantities import from the store's system of record through an abstract stock-level channel (or paste — always works), adjustments post to the product's own book register and export as an adjustment sheet for the external system. No POS integration at v1, no purchasing, no demand forecasting (LOOP-10, roster row).

## Buyer

Store managers and small-chain owners at MENA retail — home goods, electronics accessories, groceries, pharmacies, gift and bakhoor shops — running 200–5,000 SKUs across one to three stores. The economic buyer is the manager/owner who answers when stock numbers are wrong: for the count itself, for shrink conversations, and for the variance evidence an auditor or franchisor asks to see. Daily users are the manager (sessions, reconciliation, policies) and counter staff on the guided mobile count surface — counters are a typed roster on the session, not seats; counting attribution needs a name, not a license. Two to four seats for managers; unlimited typed counters. Full personas in 02-user-research.md.

## Surface

Web application, desktop-first for the management surfaces (sessions home, setup, reconciliation, catalog, policies, reports) and a dedicated **guided count view** that is designed one-thumb for a counter walking an aisle (≤767px full participant; scan field always focused, big quantity pad, bilingual item labels, zone-by-zone progression). No native app at v1 (the count view is web); no kiosk mode; no public API at v1.

## Unique feature

Cycle counts with variance tolerance and bilingual labels — defensible on three grounds (01 evidence): (1) **tolerance-triggered recounting as product grammar** — the tolerance band is pre-agreed policy, breaches auto-request recounts, and second breaches force explicit manager resolution, the convention professional count services sell as a crew discipline (digest B: PICS — "the pre-agreed amount a counted zone may differ from the book record before a recount is automatically triggered"); (2) **ABC cadence as honest scheduling** — the slice proposal names why each item is in the scope (class, interval, days overdue), so counting stays continuous without becoming an unexplainable robot; (3) **bilingual count sheets end to end** — items carry Arabic and English names, search normalizes Arabic variants, count sheets and exported reports render both scripts, and nothing in the verified category does.

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)

- No POS integration at v1 — the store's system of record connects through an abstract stock-level channel only (book-quantity import, adjustment-sheet export; LOOP-5 manual paste/download path is primary).
- No purchasing, reorder-point automation, or supplier workflows — counting produces truth, not orders.
- No demand forecasting, no AI-assisted counting, no computer-vision shelf audit — declared non-AI (07a/07b/07c honor it by construction).
- No order management, invoicing, CRM, or e-commerce listing sync (marketplace-listing-sync owns channel listings; boundary in 12).
- No asset custody chains (asset-register-tracker owns discrete-asset عهدة hand-offs; this product counts stock quantities, not custody — boundary in 12).
- No payroll/labor features for counters; the typed roster records attribution only.
- No multi-currency valuation: unit costs are single-currency workspace data (SAR-context fixtures); accounting valuation belongs to accounting systems.

## Success signals (Mock-Adapter Definition-of-Done)

A store manager can: import a bilingual catalog by paste (28 seeded items land with Arabic normalization visible in search); paste book quantities for a store (the stock import job completes with row-count progress); open the ABC cadence proposal and read why each item is proposed; create a blind cycle-count session over the slice with a typed counter roster; run the guided count on the mobile view (scan/type entries, progress 12 of 14, blind — no book quantities shown); end counting and watch the variance reveal compute; see an out-of-tolerance item auto-request a recount; submit the recount (still out — review-required); resolve it by accepting the count; clear the two uncounted items with late entries; pass the close gate and post adjustments; read the session IRA (27/28-class arithmetic); export the variance report and retrieve the artifact before expiry — all in the first session, on the Mock Adapter, with zero provider-specific knowledge anywhere in the UI and the full loop completing with every abstract capability unbound.
