- **Latin: Inter** (400/500/600/700): 500 table headers and amount-column labels, 400 body, 600 H1, 700 reserved for the paid seal's amount. The invisible hand doing pipeline work.
- **Tabular-figures rule**: every amount, rate, day-count, threshold, and claim reference renders tabular figures (`tnum`) — amount columns are scanned vertically and summed by eye; proportional digits misalign vertical scanning (the skill's mono-for-data instinct adopted as a rule, not a third family).
- **Currency display**: amounts render currency-formatted in the workspace currency with consistent 2-decimal precision (`Intl.NumberFormat`-class formatting discipline, styles.csv Financial Dashboard checklist); storage is canonical minor units — display is formatting and never changes parsing semantics (rtl-rules §3).
- Line-height: Arabic 1.6 / Latin 1.5; tracking 0 for Arabic everywhere (token-level obligation, rtl-rules §3). Claim references ("CLM-0142") are Latin-digit tokens inside possibly-Arabic chrome — bidi-isolated containers, never force-flipped (rtl-rules §2).
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; money surface per design-language-map §7): amounts, rates, days, thresholds, dates, claim references all 0-9 in both locales — including inside Arabic chrome strings (never mixed systems mid-sentence, copy-voice §1.7). ## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius C (soft, 12–16px)**: item cards and dialogs 14px; queue rows and chips 12px; removable filter chips fully-rounded ONLY as the P08 declared exception; the paid seal is a circle (a stamp is round — physical metaphor, recorded deviation).
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: one diffuse ambient shadow on raised surfaces; the composer's decision bar and the findings rail sit a step above canvas; tint carries the warmest grouping (blocking rows). Focus is the ring, never a glow (a11y-baseline §3).
- **Icons: duotone** (two-tone glyphs — vermilion primary tone + ink secondary tone, constructed on the standard outline grid): receipt, camera (capture), car (mileage), calendar-days (per-diem), route/map-pin (tiers), stamp (payout), link (citations), check-circle-2 (approved), octagon-alert (blocking), flag (advisory), message-circle (query), users (members), table (rate table), layers (policy pack). Directional glyphs (citation chevrons, next/previous claim arrows) flip in RTL; semantically anchored glyphs (receipt, camera, stamp, car, table) never flip (rtl-rules §2). No sparkle/wand glyphs on the OCR assist — the assist is a proposal surface, not a magic trick (conditionally-AI discipline, ai-native-patterns §1).
- **Motion: energetic** (motion-language §1): 150–250ms, standard easing; **ONE controlled overshoot (≤8% travel) reserved for the payout success moment and nothing else** — never errors, never hover. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms; queue rows settle in place (never spin in); findings chips arrive 160ms fade+settle and dissolve on resolution 180ms; the receipt draft fields populate with a single settle per field (no per-token animation — the draft announces start and completion, ai-native-patterns §2). Press feedback ≤80ms tactile. Reduced-motion: hard cuts + announcements, identical perceivability (motion-language §5).
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Reimbursement Stamp")**: on `recordPayout` success (CP-5.2 — the product's money-moves moment), the claim summary card lifts once (soft-lift shadow deepens and releases, 200ms) and the paid seal stamps in — scale 1.06→1.0 (≤8% travel, 220ms) with the amount and method announced politely ("Paid SAR 2,187.50 — bank transfer, ref TRF-8841"). Reduced-motion: the seal renders instantly and the announcement carries the full summary. Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the energetic standard set.
