## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Citation chevrons and next/previous-claim arrows: directional — flip. The approval-chain's level progression arrows: directional — flip. External-link arrow on exported artifacts: flips. Receipt, camera, car (mileage), calendar-days (per-diem), stamp, table (rate table), layers (policy pack), octagon-alert, flag, message-circle, users, check-circle-2: semantically anchored — never flip. The duotone receipt glyph's paper-spine metaphor: paper receipts read top-to-bottom in both scripts — never flips (metaphor decision recorded per rtl-rules §2).
