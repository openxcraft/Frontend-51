## Motion — stately (motion-language §1)

- Durations 280–480ms, emphasized decelerate; staggered reveals ≤60ms capped at 5 elements. Route changes crossfade ≤180ms (stately's single content settle allowed at 280ms); list→detail slides along the reading axis (inline-start reveal in RTL).
- **The one signature micro-interaction (T3 discipline — exactly one, on the product's most important action): the Verdict Settle.** When `runCalculation` completes, the NisabGauge settles — the threshold arc and the basket marker arrive with a single emphasized decelerate (360ms), the verdict chip fades in with the number stated in text (فوق النصاب / Above nisab, or تحت النصاب / Below nisab), and on a below-nisab result the signed distance slides from the marker to the threshold line and stops. What the motion does: it stages the two numbers the whole product exists to relate (net base vs threshold) into one readable instant — the figure is never animated into existence (no count-up, ever), only their RELATION is placed. A11y: the verdict + figures are announced politely as soon as the result lands (a11y-baseline §4); reduced-motion renders gauge, chip, and distance simultaneously with the same announcement — identical perceivability (motion-language §5).
- Everything else is the standard stately set: trace lines cascade in ≤5-line groups with ≤60ms stagger (motion carries no information — every line's text is complete without it); success moments are a state chip + polite announcement; errors arrive, they never bounce; no motion during typing/caret; no transition >480ms on interactive paths.
