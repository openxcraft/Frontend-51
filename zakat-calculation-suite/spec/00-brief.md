## What it is

A bilingual (ar/en) zakat calculation workflow for Islamic finance officers and nonprofit finance desks: one workspace in which a zakat case — an individual or an entity, one hawl or fiscal period, one reporting currency — is built item by item from evidence (cash and bank balances, gold and silver holdings, trade inventory, receivables, investments, livestock counts, crop weights, deductible liabilities), then computed under a **methodology profile**: an inspectable, sourced rule set covering the nisab basis and grams convention, per-category rates, jewelry treatment, receivables treatment, liability deduction scope, livestock band schedules, and agriculture rates. The output is not just a number. Every calculation is an immutable snapshot carrying the full **derivation trace** — classify, value, aggregate, deduct, threshold, rate, round — one auditable line per step, each line naming the rule that fired and the source note behind it, to the minor unit. The calculation engine is deterministic arithmetic over typed data (07a/07b): same items at the same case version, same profile version, same pinned nisab references → the identical result, every time. The AI stance is **declared non-AI** (ai-native-patterns §1): no generation, no inspection, no clustering, no transformation by model exists anywhere in the contract — the engine's promise is reproducibility, and reproducibility is exactly what a trust-facing number requires.

The second face is the one no retrieved competitor ships at all (01): **madhhab comparison and audit export**. The same case can be computed under two to five approved methodology profiles side by side, with divergence markers naming the rule pair behind every category-level difference — jewelry zakatable (Hanafi) versus exempt-within-uruf (Shafi'i), all-due versus current-only liability deduction, silver versus gold nisab basis, 85/595 versus 87.48/612.36 grams conventions, livestock band variants — because the research digest documents that these divergences are real, sourced, and consequential (01 evidence; 07c seeds every rule with a citation or a verify flag, never a silent default). Review is a first-class state machine: an officer submits a case, a reviewer approves or returns it, and a locked case is frozen evidence — items, profile, period, and pinned reference values all immutable — with corrections traveling through a successor case linked on a chain. From a locked case the product generates the **audit bundle**: a bilingual export carrying the certified result, the derivation trace, the pinned references, and the methodology profile and version — the artifact an external auditor, a shariah board, or a state zakat authority's filing desk can actually read. Nisab reference values are entered by officers with source notes and pinned per case (the manual path is the primary path, LOOP-5); no feed credential is ever required.

The buyer's reality (01 evidence): the zakat calculation tooling that exists is donor-facing calculators — a four-step "own minus owe, check nisab, take 2.5%" flow embedded in a donate button — or state collection portals serving the payer, not the professional who computes for many files. An Islamic finance officer at a nonprofit or advisory computing zakat for dozens of cases a season works in spreadsheets where the methodology is an invisible formula bar, the nisab value is a stale pasted number, the madhhab position is whatever the last colleague assumed, and the annual audit question "show me how this figure was derived" is answered by rebuilding the spreadsheet from memory. The category's scholarship (AAOIFI's institutional standards posture, ZATCA's corporate filing regime, the named-mufti guidance corpus) is bilingual and source-disciplined; the tooling is not. This product is the officer's instrument: the case register as the working surface, the methodology profile as reviewable data, the trace as the receipt, the lock as the certification, the export as the audit answer.

## Buyer

Islamic finance officers and nonprofit finance desks in MENA and zakat-administering communities — two shapes. (1) The **finance officer at a nonprofit or zakat-administering foundation** (2–6 seats): computing zakat cases for the institution's own treasury and for the families and small businesses it serves, answering to a shariah authority who owns methodology decisions and to external auditors who ask for derivations. (2) The **independent Islamic finance professional** (advisory or accounting practice): running client zakat files alongside tax and bookkeeping work, needing each file's methodology choice documented and each figure reproducible years later. The economic buyer is the person who answers when the board, the auditor, or the authority asks how a zakat figure was derived; daily users are officers (create cases, enter items, run calculations and comparisons), the shariah authority in the owner seat (approves and retires methodology profiles), reviewers (approve, lock, spawn successors), and external auditors in viewer seats (read locked cases, retrieve audit artifacts — the deliberately open export path). Per-seat for working seats with viewer seats free (12 Q-3); calculations themselves are never metered — determinism is not rationed (07b rule 9).

## Unique feature

**Nisab-aware calculations with multiple madhhab methodologies and audit export**, defensible on four grounds (evidence in 01): (1) the methodology is **data, not code** — every rule the engine applies is an inspectable profile row with a source note or a verify flag, so the shariah authority reviews the fiqh basis instead of trusting a black box, and the same discipline extends the category's own named-mufti conventions into software; (2) the **comparison** is a governance artifact no calculator can produce — the same evidence computed under named rule sets with divergence markers citing the rule pair, which is precisely how methodology decisions are actually made in institutions; (3) the **trace** makes every figure walkable to its items, rules, and pinned reference values — the audit answer is the product's native output, not a reconstruction; (4) the **lock and successor chain** give the register certified-state semantics (frozen evidence, corrections as linked successors, never edits) that spreadsheet practice structurally cannot offer. No retrieved competitor binds sourced methodology profiles, deterministic traces, madhhab comparison, and locked-case audit export into one product (01 positioning gaps).

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)

- **No payment collection** — the product never moves money and touches no payment rails. A computation produces a certified figure and an audit artifact; the payment of zakat belongs to banks, state portals, and the payer's own hands. Distribution of zakat to recipients (the eight categories) is likewise out of scope: the product computes obligations, it does not disburse them.
- **No general accounting** — no double-entry books, no payroll, no inter-account reconciliation, no financial statements. The case's item list is evidence intake for a computation, not a ledger of record (the ledger territory belongs to the waqf-endowment-registry sibling — LOOP-3 mirror in 12).
- **No donor CRM** — no donor profiles, no campaigns, no fundraising funnels, no giving-history tracking. The donate-button posture of the consumer calculators (01) is the opposite of this product's buyer; zakat collection and donor relationships belong to collection institutions and donor platforms.
- No zakat al-fitr computation (a per-head staple-food obligation with its own rules — 01 source [12]; wealth-zakat arithmetic only at v1).
- No shariah adjudication: the product applies recorded rules and shows their sources; ruling on contested questions is the authority's act performed inside the profile-approval gate, never the engine's (07a honesty note).
- No automatic market-price feeds at v1 (entered references with source notes are the primary path — LOOP-5); no OCR of statements; no AI anywhere (declared non-AI — 07b rule 10).
- No tax filing or e-invoicing integration: corporate-filing regimes (01 source [14]) are context for the audit export's shape, not a submission channel.
