## Direct competitors (zakat calculation tooling, as it exists)

The retrieved direct category is **donor-facing calculators embedded in donation flows** plus **state collection portals**. None of them is an officer's calculation workflow; the differentiation note on each names what it does not do.

1. **National Zakat Foundation UK — Zakat Calculator** — https://www.nzf.org.uk/zakat-calculator/ [5]. The category's best-shaped tool: a four-step flow ("work out what you own… take off what you owe… check the balance against the Nisab value… work out 2.5%"), a maintained "Today's Nisab" value [4], a knowledge bank answering asset/liability/jewelry/business questions with named-mufti review [6][7][8][9], and free 1-to-1 scholar consultations. Single methodology posture (silver nisab for mixed assets [4]), single payer, no case register, no methodology comparison, no trace, no export beyond the browser. Differentiation: this product keeps the calculator's honesty (their nisab guidance becomes one reviewable profile) and adds the register, the comparison, the trace, and the audit artifact.
2. **Muslim Hands UK — Zakat Calculator** — https://muslimhands.org.uk/zakat-calculator [10]. Live nisab on the calculator, hawl explained, a worked example (£6,500 → £162.50), a zakatable-assets list spanning pensions to agricultural produce, debt subtraction including HMRC tax set-aside. Same single-payer, single-method, donate-at-the-end shape. Differentiation: the worked-example transparency Muslims Hands shows in prose is exactly what this product's derivation trace does per line for a professional file.
3. **Zakat Foundation of America** — https://www.zakat.org/ [3→q3]. US posture: "Zakat Calculator" as the donation funnel's front door ("Calculate & Donate Zakat"), transparency badges as marketing, 25-years/50-countries claims (source-asserted). The calculator is acquisition, not practice tooling.
4. **IslamicFinder — Zakat Calculator** — https://www.islamicfinder.org/zakat-calculator/ (fetch returned HTTP 403; landscape fact only, no content retrieved [research log]). A high-traffic global Islamic utility with a calculator among prayer-time tools; existence documented, content not usable — recorded honestly rather than characterized.
5. **Islamic Relief UK — Zakat Calculator** — https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/zakat-calculator/ (fetch blocked by a bot challenge, inherited corpus f12; landscape fact only). Same donate-funnel shape as [3].
6. **ZATCA — Zakaty e-portal (Saudi Arabia)** — https://zatca.gov.sa/en/Pages/default.aspx [14]. The state's surface for optional individual zakat payment, alongside establishment zakat returns, registration, installment plans, and "Zakat Payment for Livestock, Crops, and Fruits." This is the filing/payment regime itself — the regulator's desk, not the professional's. Differentiation: this product computes and documents; the authority's portal receives. The audit export is shaped so its output can stand in front of that desk (bilingual, trace-bearing, methodology-named), but no submission integration exists at v1 (LOOP-10).
7. **BAZNAS (Indonesia)** — https://www.baznas.go.id/ [15]. Trilingual national zakat institution running collection and distribution at state scale — programs, mustahik services, payment surfaces. No officer-facing calculation workflow on the fetched surface.
8. **Lembaga Zakat Selangor (Malaysia)** — https://www.zakatselangor.com.my/ [16]. State institution: pay-zakat/apply-for-help flows, five assistance pillars, Arabic verse with Malay translation in the marketing chrome. Same collection-side shape.

**Category read**: eight direct surfaces retrieved, all either payer-side calculators or state collection bodies. The professional workflow surface — many cases, chosen methodology, reviewable rules, derivation evidence, certification, audit export — is unoccupied. The closest structural cousin in the whole corpus is the waqf-administration vertical (awqaf.online [20], and the enterprise suites documented in the sibling waqf-endowment-registry's research), which administers endowments, not zakat obligations — a different artifact with a different buyer, mirrored as an adjacency in 12 Q-4.

## Indirect competitors (where the work actually happens today)

1. **Spreadsheets** — the true incumbent (paraphrase-pattern evidence: the guidance corpus's own question topology [9] and corporate-workflow evidence [14]; no spreadsheet vendor page is needed to name the status quo). An officer's zakat file lives in a sheet where the methodology is an invisible formula, the nisab value is a pasted stale number, the madhhab position is whoever last edited, and the audit question is answered by rebuilding from memory. Differentiation: this product makes each of those invisible things a typed, sourced, inspectable object — the formula becomes rules with source notes, the pasted number becomes a pinned reference with provenance, the assumed position becomes a named profile, and the audit answer becomes the trace.
2. **Donorbox** — https://donorbox.org/pricing [18]. Nonprofit fundraising/donor-CRM platform: free Standard with platform+processing fees, Pro $150/month, Premium contact-tier. Indirect because it is the donor-side money tool nonprofits already buy — the opposite of this product's exclusions (no donor CRM, no payment collection, LOOP-10). Cited for pricing posture only.
3. **Zoho Books** — https://www.zoho.com/books/pricing/ [19]. Generic accounting incumbent (Free / Standard $12 / Professional $24 / Premium $36 per org/month, billed annually): the bookkeeping the officer's practice already runs. Its zakat gap is structural — no nisab concept, no hawl, no madhhab rules, no in-kind dues, no religious-obligation artifact. Cited as the pricing band anchor for professional-finance seats.
4. **Awqaf.online** — https://awqaf.online/ [20]. Islamic-institution vertical SaaS (endowment administration: assets, beneficiaries, income and distributions, role-based workspaces; freemium posture). Indirect as the family-adjacent vertical: it proves Islamic-institution vertical SaaS exists and prices freemium, while administering a different obligation — the waqf sibling's territory (12 Q-4), never zakat computation.

## Demand evidence

- **The calculation canon is real and stable where it matters**: 2.5% of net zakatable wealth above nisab after one lunar year is the customarily cited rule across the corpus [1][5][10]; the four-step own-minus-owe flow is the category's shared convention [5]. Demand for calculation help is proven by the existence of maintained calculators and live nisab values at every major zakat institution [4][5][10].
- **The divergence axes are documented, not anecdotal**: grams conventions differ between authorities (85/595 [2] vs 87.48/612.36 [4][10]); the mixed-asset nisab basis is contested in the field's own words ("some scholars still maintain that gold should be used regardless, using silver is safer… for the recipients" [4]); jewelry, debts, business assets, agricultural scope, and nisab prerequisites are all named divergence axes in the scholarship's own enumeration [1]; livestock is computed by in-kind band schedules [3]. A professional tool that cannot hold multiple positions side by side cannot serve this field at all — the comparison surface is demand-derivative, not a feature wish.
- **Institutional demand is a filing reality**: KSA's authority runs establishment zakat returns, business registration, installment plans, and publishes debt-treatment guidance for regular accounts and implementing-regulation procedures (1445H edition) [14] — corporate zakat is recurring paperwork with methodology questions attached. AAOIFI-standard jurisdictions and AAOIFI-guideline jurisdictions (KSA, Malaysia among them) anchor institutional methodology presets [13].
- **The audit answer is the unserved moment**: the guidance corpus itself carries named-mufti review and review dates on every answer [6][7] — accountability is the category's native grammar, but the calculators produce a number with no derivation a professional could hand to that grammar. The buyer's board, shariah board, or auditor asking "show me how this figure was derived" has no retrieved tool that answers.
- **Seasonality is a demand shape, not a demand proof**: Ramadan as the calculation season is documented custom [1][10]; the product's case-period model supports year-round hawl dates, which is where professional files actually sit (a fiscal-year entity case is not Ramadan-shaped).
- **Market size**: no auditable figure for the buyer population exists in the corpus; the $1-trillion-class sector claims live in waqf-sector marketing (sibling's research), not in anything usable here. Refused to invent a TAM.

No usage-based pricing anywhere near the certified number: a product whose arithmetic integrity is its identity cannot ration honesty.

## Positioning statement

For Islamic finance officers and nonprofit finance desks who must produce zakat figures that survive review, zakat-calculation-suite is the calculation workflow that makes methodology a choice and evidence a habit: cases built item by item, computed under sourced, authority-approved methodology profiles, compared across madhhab positions with every divergence named, locked as certified evidence, and exported as a bilingual audit bundle. Unlike donor-facing calculators that end at a donate button and unlike spreadsheets that hide the fiqh in a formula bar, it treats the derivation itself — every classify/value/aggregate/deduct/threshold/rate/round step to the minor unit — as the product.
