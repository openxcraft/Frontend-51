## 1. arabic-copy-studio — content_tools

**What it is**: AI-assisted Arabic/English copywriting studio with register control (فصحى بيضاء/وسطى), tashkeel-aware proofing, dialect detection, and inline bilingual co-writing.

**Why flagship**:
- **Buyer reach**: every bilingual marketing/content team in MENA — agencies, brand teams, publishers, e-commerce content desks — is a buyer. The global AI-writing category (Jasper, Copy.ai, Writesonic — see product's 01-market-research.md) prices at $20–60/seat and is English-first; Arabic is an afterthought everywhere in the category.
- **Market timing**: Arabic LLM capability matured recently (Jais, ALLaM, Fanar, Falcon, Mistral Saba — cited in product 01), making register-aware Arabic generation credible for the first time; a design-led Arabic-first studio is a category creation, not a follower product.
- **Design opportunity**: the category is visually uniform (generic sans, sparkle buttons). A manuscript-serif, ink-and-saffron writing environment (P08 Amiri + Source Serif 4, layered-depth, stately motion) with ORIGINAL interactions — Register Dial, Parity Weave transform view, Ink transitions — is Awwwards-caliber territory (curated references + 3 original proposals in the product's 13-reference-mockups.md).

## 2. shift-swap-marketplace — business_ops

**What it is**: fairness-ranked shift-swap marketplace for frontline teams: workers post swaps, qualification-matched colleagues respond, supervisors approve with full fairness ledger.

**Why flagship**:
- **Buyer reach**: frontline-heavy retail/F&B/logistics/healthcare operations dominate MENA private employment; every schedule generates swaps, currently handled on paper and chat groups with no fairness record.
- **Market size**: workforce management is a mature global category, but swap-specific workflows are a wedge the incumbents bury in modules (see product 01).
- **Design opportunity**: a social marketplace mechanic inside an ops tool — fairness-ranked queues, negotiation choreography, notification ladders, and a copper/calm identity (P04, C-radius, energetic) — is an original interaction space with no direct visual precedent. SME-K's three original interactions are specified in the product folder.

## 3. cod-order-confirmation — commerce_integrations

**What it is**: confirmation console for cash-on-delivery orders: multi-attempt confirmation ladder, delivery-window agreement, RTO-risk scoring, courier handoff manifests.

**Why flagship**:
- **Buyer reach**: COD dominates MENA e-commerce — around 62% of MENA online shoppers preferred COD per Think with Google research, with regional reports placing it up to 70–80% in some markets (citations in product 01); every COD merchant bleeds revenue on unconfirmed/failed deliveries, making this the single highest-ROI purchase in the commerce family.
- **Market size**: rides the MEA e-commerce growth curve directly (product 01 citations).
- **Design opportunity**: high-stakes stateful conversation design — attempt ladders, agreement moments, risk surfacing — on a distinctive courier-carbon ink-press identity (P04, A-radius, crisp) with brand-distance from every express-courier identity (LOOP-11 check documented).

## 4. kyc-onboarding-flow — specialized

**What it is**: applicant-facing multi-session onboarding flow + review console for identity verification: document-capture guidance, resumable applications, reviewer queues with audit trails.

**Why flagship**:
- **Buyer reach**: fintech onboarding is the gateway product of the region's most heavily funded sector; every wallet, lender, and trading platform buys onboarding conversion.
- **Market size**: KYC vendor spend scales with fintech formation rates (product 01).
- **Design opportunity**: trust design at the highest stakes — resumable multi-session applications, camera guidance with screen-reader alternatives, review consoles with audit-grade accountability (a11y overlay obligations per a11y-baseline §7). The reference bar (best-in-class onboarding craft) is exactly the tier the flagship program exists to compete with. Deep-navy stately identity (P07, B, stately).

## 5. funnel-drop-off-explorer — analytics

**What it is**: exploration canvas for conversion funnels: segment-annealed drop-off exploration, bilingual event taxonomy, cohort-linked drill paths.

**Why flagship**:
- **Buyer reach**: every growth/product team; the analytics family's widest buyer pool.
- **Market size**: product analytics is a large, growing category whose incumbents bury drop-off exploration behind query builders (product 01).
- **Design opportunity**: original data-viz interaction design — an exploration canvas rather than a dashboard, on an abyss-canvas + chartreuse ink-press identity (P14, A, crisp) — the analytics family's only shot at award-tier craft, with accessibility-done-right data density as the differentiator.
