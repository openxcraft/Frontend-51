## Original-direction sketch (prose)

The desk console as a **ruled proofing table**: a paper-white queue drawn like a newspaper ledger — hairline rules, ink metadata, one red annotation ink reserved for findings and integrity moments. Reading a submission is deliberately print-like: the blind workspace sets the anonymized text in generous Naskh/Newsreader columns with lint findings pinned to the margin like proofreader's ticks, severity expressed by mark shape (tick, underline, double-underline) before color. The decision composer is the one "raised" object: a bordered letter sheet assembling itself from the round digest and findings digest, the override gate rendered as a required marginal note in red when a round splits. Nothing animates except a state chip; the room stays as still as paper — the drama is the queue moving, not the interface. Contributors get the simplest possible surface: one form, one reference code, one honest status sentence.

## Mood anchors

A proofing table under morning light · red-pencil discipline · the queue as a ledger, not a feed · blind copies stacked neatly · a letter sheet that leaves the desk and is logged the moment it does.
