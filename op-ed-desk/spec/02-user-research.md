# 02 — User Research: op-ed-desk

SME-B pass · Paraphrased sentiment only (marked); grounded in 01 evidence. Zero invented quotes.

## Persona 1 — Amina, Opinion Editor (primary buyer)

**Role**: heads a pan-Arabic daily's opinion desk; two reviewers, one writer; triages every arrival beside commissioning and editing.
**JTBD**: "When submissions pile up, I want them pre-checked against desk policy and sorted by promise, so my morning goes to judgment, not filtering."
**Triggers**: a 200-item unread week; a strong piece found a month late; an ombudsman complaint that a known name jumped the queue.
**Objections + responses**: "Blind review slows us" → 48h rounds, visible SLA; "reviewers need context" → COI matching is editor-side at assignment; "another tool" → one queue surface + ⌘K (P05).
**Pains**: slush volume vs triage hours; unprovable name-bias suspicion; no audit trail when accused of favoritism.
**Verbatim (paraphrase of public desk sentiment)**: "Everything lands in one inbox; the strong piece and the PR pitch look identical at a glance."

## Persona 2 — Yousef, Staff Reviewer

**Role**: editorial writer reviewing 8–12 submissions weekly; bilingual; prefers blind reads when offered.
**JTBD**: "When I open a submission, I want clean text and honest style findings, so my recommendation is about the argument."
**Triggers**: rounds where he recognized the author by paragraph two; Arabic submissions with mixed numerals and dialect drift; identity leaks in "anonymous" email material.
**Objections + responses**: "The linter dumbs down judgment" → findings are advisory span-anchored flags with dismiss/acknowledge, never auto-edits (P15); "flagging a leak feels like snitching" → the flag is anonymous in the round and pauses it for regeneration.
**Pains**: re-reading for the same five desk violations; ambiguity over judging person vs piece.

## Persona 3 — Dr. Farida, External Contributor

**Role**: university economist; 3–4 Arabic op-eds yearly; submits by email per each paper's guidelines, rarely hears back.
**JTBD**: "When I submit, I want a reference and a status, so I know whether to offer it elsewhere after the exclusivity window."
**Triggers**: three weeks of silence on an exclusive submission; a reason-free rejection after hand-cutting to the word band.
**Objections + responses**: "No account for free copy" → form + reference code only (00); "automated linting rejects my style" → the linter informs the letter, humans decide; dialect in quoted speech is flagged, not corrected.
**Pains**: unknown status inside exclusivity windows; word-band guesswork; feedback lottery.

## Persona 4 — Tariq, Managing Editor (operations)

**Role**: owns newsroom tools and accountability to the EIC; not a daily user.
**JTBD**: "When the ombudsman asks how the desk decides, I want the activity log and round records to answer, so the desk's integrity is auditable."
**Triggers**: favoritism complaints; assignments that later looked conflicted; tool sprawl.
**Objections + responses**: "Contributor data safe?" → processing disclosed at intake, code-scoped access, identity revealed only to roles that need it (07a/08); "another export need" → audit export has a manual alternative (LOOP-5).
