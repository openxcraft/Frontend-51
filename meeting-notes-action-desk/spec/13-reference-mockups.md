## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Maqam Desk: a lilac-ruled commitment ledger on a soft-lift morning surface. The desk home reads as one calm instrument — zone counts as plain arithmetic in text ("2 بانتظار التأكيد، 1 متأخرة، 1 قرب الاستحقاق"), every number a link, nothing decorative competing with the morning's triage. The meeting detail is a two-register reading: the capture stream settles at the top in its own mixed directions, bilingual and unashamed, while the actions below it sit as commitment cards — lilac-outlined while proposed, quietly green-filled once confirmed, each carrying its owner's name, its due date in Western numerals, and a thin quotation-frame link back to the exact words that birthed it. The signature surface is the **loop desk**: a queue of handshakes waiting to happen. Each card carries its respond-by countdown as text ("الرد بحلول 22 أكتوبر — 8 ساعات متبقية"), and its three responses — تأكيد، استفسار، رفض — sit below the action's bilingual text so the commitment is read before it is made. When an owner confirms, the card does exactly one thing: the Ta'ahhud Settle — a ≤120ms opacity crossfade from outlined-slate to filled-green with the commitment seal, the owner's name and timestamp settling into the text row beneath, and one polite announcement. No confetti, no bounce, no second movement — a promise becoming a record, at the speed of a nod. Escalation is typographic, not theatrical: seven-day-old unconfirmed actions simply tell the truth in red text with named dates. Motion everywhere else is nearly still — opacity steps, instant position, hard cuts under reduced motion — because the desk's job is to hold commitments still long enough to be kept.
