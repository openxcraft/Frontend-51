## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T2-appropriate)

**The Wijdan Lattice.** A planning surface drawn like a well-ruled instrument: one mid-indigo structural line, cool paper, a single soft shadow step — no glass, no gradient, no dashboard chrome. The screen is the week itself. Down the inline-start edge, the roster rail reads like a ledger column — bilingual names each at its own script direction, Western codes, slate qualification chips, the contract-cap mark that says the engine enforces the stricter of the two. Across the top, the week navigator carries both calendars (Gregorian primary, Hijri alongside, each isolated so the pair never re-orders) and the ruleset badge — jurisdiction and version, clickable into the rule values themselves, because on this surface *the values are the marketing*. The lattice center: worker rows × day columns, alternating paper/raised columns so the grid reads in grayscale, shift blocks as small ruled cards — role, tabular times, break, headcount — that lift exactly one soft step when dragged and settle in place, never spin in. The inline-end rail is the product's conscience: the violation inspector, blocking first, every row a sentence — the rule's name, the measured value against the required value, the worker, the day, the version it was computed against — each row one jump away from the cell that caused it, and both cells named when a rule spans a pair (the clopen's Sunday close and Monday open, caught across the week edge the spreadsheet never saw). Guardrail color does semantic work but never works alone: every chip carries its icon and its words. Ramadan mode arrives as a brass-edged banner — dual dates, the reduced caps in text — and the whole lattice quietly re-baselines to 6h days. And the product leans toward one moment: **the publication gate**, a bordered frame that lists its blockers like a manifest and refuses, naming each, until the manifest is empty — then the blocker rows fold away in a crisp stagger and leave the seal: actor, timestamp, the waiver ledger behind it. That record is the artifact Amal hands an inspector. Everything else is stillness: chips arrive and dissolve in ≤140ms, nothing bounces, nothing sparkles, and the only voice on the floor is the inspector's — calm, numeric, always naming the rule.

## Mood anchors

A ruled lattice in one indigo line on cool paper · a ledger of names down one edge, a week of blocks across the field · an inspector rail that speaks in measured-versus-required numbers · a brass-edged Ramadan banner that re-baselines the whole week · a gate that refuses in named sentences and seals in one — then Sunday is done, and the manager sleeps.
