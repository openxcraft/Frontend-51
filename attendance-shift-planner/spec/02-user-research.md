Personas use regional names; no real individuals are represented.

## Persona 1 — Fahad, Multi-Branch Retail Operations Manager (primary buyer)

**Role**: runs operations for a 3-branch home-goods retail group in Riyadh — 46 workers at the flagship, 28 at a mall branch, 22 at a logistics hub desk; he owns the weekly roster across all three, delegates branch-level tweaks to supervisors, and is the person HR calls when a labor-rule question lands. Plans on Sunday evenings in a spreadsheet grid he built himself four years ago; the grid has drifted into three variants because each branch edits its own copy.
**JTBD**: "When I build next week's roster, I want every worker inside the labor rules before the week starts — not discovered in an HR audit in March — so I can publish on Sunday night and sleep, instead of re-checking 96 rows by eye for rest gaps and Ramadan hours."
**Triggers**: an HR query about overtime hours during a Ramadan trading surge (the week that breaks every cap at once — reduced statutory hours against the year's biggest demand); a supervisor who closed Sunday and opened Monday for three weeks running because the spreadsheet didn't compute gaps (the clopening failure class, 01); three versions of "the roster" arguing during a branch call.
**Objections + responses**: "My spreadsheet is free and it works" → it cannot compute a rest gap across midnight or a Ramadan cap change, and it keeps no publication record — the first inspection asks for exactly that (01 fair-workweek posture). "Will this become another suite I have to roll out to 90 workers?" → v1 is the manager's tool; workers get review views, not accounts to manage; swap negotiation stays in the marketplace product the group is evaluating (12 Q-4). "I don't trust software to know Saudi labor law" → rulesets are inspectable data with capture dates and sources, marked "verify against current law"; he reads the rules before trusting them (05 ruleset library).
**Pains**: Ramadan as an annual cliff (statutory 6h/36h for fasting Muslim workers colliding with the seasonal peak — 01); no shared referent for "which version is live" between branches; overtime discovered after the month closes; Saudization-aware role coverage tracked on a whiteboard (paraphrase of multi-constraint rostering sentiment from manager forums — recorded as a v2 question, 12).

## Persona 2 — Mariam, F&B Floor Manager (primary daily user)

**Role**: floor manager of a 32-worker casual-dining branch in a Dubai mall; builds the week grid herself on a laptop, lives in it daily — coverage holes appear at 8am and she fills them by 8:15. Runs brunch-heavy weekends, late mall closes, and a night window (21:00+) that touches the UAE night-overtime class; two supervisors hold the food-safety qualification the law and the brand require on every hot shift.
**JTBD**: "When I place shifts in the grid, I want the tool to tell me immediately that this creates a 7-hour rest gap or breaches the night-window threshold — so I catch it now, while moving one block still fixes it, not at publish or, worse, in the payroll report."
**Triggers**: a Friday clopen she caught only because the worker herself flagged it (paraphrase of the clopening discovery pattern — 01 HR Dive/Indeed class); mall Ramadan hours changing branch operating times while statutory hours shrink simultaneously; a qualification check she does by memory across 32 workers (paraphrase of qualification-tracking pain from category forums — 01).
**Objections + responses**: "I don't have time to learn a compliance system" → the compliance is ambient: she keeps placing blocks; the inspector speaks only when a rule breaks, and always names the rule (00 unique feature). "Auto-schedulers got it wrong at my last job" → this product never auto-schedules — declared non-AI, the manager authors and the rules check (ai-native-patterns §1 posture; the anti-Deputy/Quinyx position, 01). "My team speaks a mix of Arabic and English" → bilingual by construction — worker names, roles, and copy in both, grid direction per workspace locale (05).
**Pains**: discovery-late violations (rest gaps found by workers, not tools); no overtime *visibility* — hours accrue silently until finance reacts; the grid she wants is a grid, not a calendar-list toggle buried in a suite (paraphrase of scheduling-UX fatigue — the Connecteam complaint thread, 01).

## Persona 3 — Salem, Shift Supervisor (swap recorder + published-plan reader)

**Role**: shift supervisor at the flagship branch; his team agrees swaps verbally on the floor ("take my Thursday, I take your Saturday"), he approves them, and until now they lived in his head and a chat thread. Reads the published plan on his phone during the week; answers "who's on tonight?".
**JTBD**: "When a swap is agreed on the floor, I want to record it in one minute and know immediately whether it breaks something — rest gap, weekly cap, qualification — so Thursday's fix doesn't create Friday's violation."
**Triggers**: a verbally-agreed swap that silently put a worker over the weekly cap (paraphrase of post-hoc violation discovery); being the last to know that the published plan changed; needing the week's plan on a phone without a desktop login ceremony.
**Objections + responses**: "Swaps belong to the swap app, no?" → exactly: negotiation belongs to shift-swap-marketplace (12 Q-4 boundary — the group is adopting it); this tool records the *agreed* swap against the authoritative plan and re-checks the rules for both workers — the piece the marketplace doesn't own. "I won't type on a grid from my phone" → he doesn't: mobile is review + single swap recording; authoring stays desktop (00 surface). "Another password?" → supervisor seat inherits the workspace session model; published-plan review is link-shareable within the workspace (08 N3 manual alternative).
**Pains**: swap provenance disappearing into chat threads; the "was that swap even legal?" question nobody could answer; printed plans going stale the moment one swap lands.

## Persona 4 — Amal, HR & Compliance Officer (auditor)

**Role**: HR/compliance officer at a facilities-services company running mixed KSA/UAE contracts across 60 cleaners and technicians on client sites; she doesn't build rosters but owns the answer when a labor inspector, an internal audit, or a wage dispute asks: were the working-time rules followed, and if not, who decided that and why.
**JTBD**: "When an inspection or dispute names a month, I want the published plans, the rule breaches, and the waiver reasons for that month in one export — so the answer is an artifact, not an archaeology project across three branch spreadsheets."
**Triggers**: a wage-dispute claim where the roster and the reality disagreed (paraphrase of dispute-evidence pain); the annual scramble to show Ramadan-hour compliance across two jurisdictions; waivers that existed only in a manager's email replies.
**Objections + responses**: "Is a scheduling tool really my system?" → she doesn't schedule; she consumes the publication records, violation ledgers, and waiver audit trail the tool produces as its normal grammar (07a PublicationRecord). "How do I know the rules match the law?" → every ruleset carries source notes and capture dates; she is the natural owner of ruleset review before activation (05 ruleset library; 12 Q-9). "Saudization and leave policies?" → out of v1 scope, honestly recorded (12).
**Pains**: evidence scattered across branches; two jurisdictions in one company (KSA 150% overtime class vs UAE 125%/night-150% classes — 01) handled as one blurry "Gulf rules" guess; no record of *why* an exception was allowed.

## Voice derivation (SME-J)

Ops-floor voice: **calm-command, evidence-first, zero ceremony** — states the rule, the measured value, and the next action in one breath ("Rest period 9h required — 7h 30m provided. Move the Monday open or waive with a reason."). Arabic register فصحى بيضاء (utility precision surface — a rule engine must read as an instrument, not an oracle); English professional-neutral, contractions off (governance-adjacent family, copy-voice §4). Warmth is allowed in exactly two places: publish success and first-run — never in violations (copy-voice §1.6). Fixed terminology (locked across 07a/11; one concept, one term everywhere — copy-voice §3): فريق=team · عامل=worker · مؤهل/تأهيل=qualification · مناوبة=shift · إسناد المناوبة=shift assignment · خطة الأسبوع=week plan · مجموعة قواعد الولاية القضائية=jurisdiction ruleset · قاعدة=rule · مخالفة القاعدة=rule violation · تنازل مسجّل=waiver · نشر الخطة=publication · سجل النشر=publication record · تبادل=swap · حاجز النشر=publication gate · جدول الشبكة=planning grid · مفتش المخالفات=violation inspector · وضع رمضان=Ramadan mode. Numbers Western per ledger row (times, minutes, counts — a data surface, design-language-map §7; never mixed systems mid-sentence, copy-voice §1.7). Arabic strings may run ~125% of English length — layout budgets for it (10) and the 11 matrix lists both strings verbatim (copy-voice §5).
