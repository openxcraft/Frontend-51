## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius B (standard, 6–10px)**: shift blocks and inspector rows 8px; roster rows 6px; chips fully-rounded ONLY as the removable filter-chip exception (P08 — declared deviation, recorded here).
- **Elevation: soft-lift**: one diffuse ambient step from paper to raised surfaces; the grid is the ground plane (flattest), shift blocks lift one step when dragging (the drag affordance is elevation + hairline, never a heavy drop shadow); violation rows stay flat (they are findings, not objects); zero glow, zero glass (brand-distance below).
- **Icons: line-2** (standard 2px stroke outline set, Lucide-class vocabulary per the skill's icons.csv — calendar for the week navigator, clock for time entries, users for the roster, grid for layout, alert-triangle for warnings, alert-circle for blockers, check-circle for the seal, arrow-left-right for swaps, award for qualifications, bell for notifications, settings). No sparkle glyphs anywhere — non-AI product, no magic affordances (ai-native-patterns §1); no "AI-schedule" button exists to iconify.
- **Motion: crisp** (motion-language §1): 100–180ms, standard ease-out, **zero overshoot ever** (timing-critical work — the buyer moves blocks against a clock, 02 Mariam). Shift-block placement settles in place (120ms, nothing spins in); violation chips arrive (120ms fade+settle) and dissolve on resolution (140ms) — arrival/departure always accompanied by the inspector's live-region text (a11y-baseline §4 — motion never carries information alone); route changes crossfade ≤180ms. Reduced-motion: hard cuts + announcements, identical perceivability.
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Week Seals")**: on publish success, the gate's blocker rows collapse row-by-row — stagger ≤60ms capped at 5 rows, 120ms each, ease-out — into the published seal with actor + timestamp (the product's single most important action, CP-3.3). Reduced-motion: rows vanish instantly, the seal renders, and the announcement carries the full summary. Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the crisp standard set.
