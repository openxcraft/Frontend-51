## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

The Rosewater Counter: a warm, low-arousal console that behaves like the good version of the shop it serves — unhurried at rest, instant when spoken to. The surface is rosewater paper (#F9F3F7-class canvas) with soft-lift cards, pill geometry everywhere, and exactly one object of desire on screen at a time: the loyalty card itself, rendered like the wallet object it replaces — stamp dots as friendly filled-rounded seals in Wafa Rose, tier rows as two-script menu lines ("كوكيز مجاني · free cookie · 4") set in Baloo Bhaijaan 2's rounded terminals. The stamper is one search field with generous type and a results list that reads like a regulars' roll-call; resolving a member settles the card sheet into place in calm 240s-of-a-second transitions, and the one moment of ceremony in the whole product is the Stamp Landing — a single dot filling with rose ink while the count ticks "4 من 8" and the approach to the next tier is spoken aloud in one quiet line. The studio's signature surface is the **dual-face card preview**: the Arabic face and the English face side by side as tabs of the same object, each a true render (never a mirrored hack), tier rows aligned across faces by the P23 link grammar so the two languages are audited against each other, not translated after the fact. The audit ledger is the honest basement of the house: a plain ruled table in ink where every row names its actor ("أمينة ختمت بطاقة نورة العتيبي — 08:12"), flags arrive as written arithmetic ("3 تعديلات خلال 24 ساعة"), and nothing ever deletes. Motion is calm throughout — 200–320ms, symmetric ease-in-out, no scale on hover, no overshoot, no confetti when a coffee is earned; the reward moment is a receipt line and a state chip, the shopkeeper's kind of celebration. Accessibility is the quiet spine: rose-deep focus rings at 7.61:1, counts always in text, the pass legible at 400% zoom with worst-case Arabic tier names wrapping whole-word.
