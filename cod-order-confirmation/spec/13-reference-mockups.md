## B. Original proposals (3 — none exists as a composition in current COD tooling; SME-K; ids pre-referenced from 03/04/05/06)

### B1 — The Ladder Trail (the attempt state machine as a stamped rung rail; original interaction #1, referenced by 03 CP-2.3, 04 IA decision, 05 tier-1 obligations, 06 LadderTrail/AttemptStamp)

**Layout proposal (S3 ORDER DRAWER, console ≥1280px; re-flowed as the on-call bottom sheet's mid section at ≥360px; RTL-first).** A horizontal rung rail that renders the ladder's state machine in reading order — rungs flow inline-start → inline-end for attempt 1 (rich message), 2 (SMS-class text), 3 (voice/IVR), 4 (agent call); in RTL the rail mirrors (progress toward inline-end, rtl-rules §2) while timestamps and numerals stay readable. Each rung is an **AttemptStamp** (06): outline-geometric kind glyph, ordinal text ("المحاولة 2 من 4" / "attempt 2 of 4"), state as text+icon (queued → sending → sent → delivered → read → responded, or failed / skipped — never color alone), and the sent timestamp in text.secondary tabular numerals. The ACTIVE rung carries the **cooldown arc** — an SVG stroke-dashoffset ring on a composited layer — with the remaining time rendered as TEXT beside it ("المحاولة التالية بعد 2:10" / "next attempt in 2:10" — the arc is decoration; the engine computed that time against quiet hours and expiry, and the trail visualizes, never guesses). The buyer's response tag hangs beneath the rung that produced it, carrying the reply verbatim (bidi-isolated, ≤280 chars — "أكد الطلب — الخميس مساءً" class fixtures, 07c §1). Two rail-scope instruments complete the object: the **expiry countdown** at the rail's inline-end cap ("تُغلق مهلة التأكيد بعد {duration}" / "Confirmation window closes in {duration}") — the deadline is visible from minute one because auto-cancel is policy, never surprise (CP-4.1) — and the **quiet-hours hold band**, a hatched no-go band over the timeline ahead of the active rung with the release time in words ("بانتظار انتهاء ساعات الهدوء — يستؤنف الإرسال عند 08:00"). A wash header strip above the rail names the ladder policy version in force (getLadderPolicy context) — the trail is evidence, so it always says which rules produced it.

**Motion choreography.** On drawer open the rail renders from the row's embedded data immediately (P02 honesty — no skeleton dance); rungs then stamp in chronological order using the dispatch-stamp cadence (press ≤80ms scale 1.0→0.97 → land 60ms with the 1px border.interactive flash → settle; 140ms total, ease-out, zero overshoot), staggered ≤60ms and capped at 5 elements (4 rungs + the response tag, then simultaneous — 05's stagger cap). Thereafter the trail is event-driven ONLY: a new attempt.state or response.captured event stamps exactly the rung it belongs to (subscribeOrderEvents) and nothing else re-animates — a trail that re-ran its history on every re-render would destroy the very trust it exists to create (10: stamps fire on events, never on re-renders). The cooldown arc depletes at 1Hz coalesced per frame with zero layout shift; failover patches the failed rung once and names the takeover ("فشلت الرسالة عند القناة؛ تتولى درجة الرسالة النصية بعد {duration}" / "The message failed at the channel; the SMS rung takes over in {duration}" — 11 §D3); resolution (confirm, cancel, auto-cancel) settles the whole rail to its terminal state in a single 140ms arrive-don't-bounce patch.

**A11y implications.** The trail is an ordered list of rung groups — attempt ordinal IS list position, announced on rung focus ("المحاولة 2 من 4 — رسالة نصية" / "attempt 2 of 4 — SMS"); states are text+icon (feedback.critical carries failed rungs at 5.68:1 on canvas with the reason and failover named — 11 §D3); response rawText is bidi-isolated inline content; the cooldown arc is decorative with the remaining time ALSO text; rung stamps announce politely once per state change, never per tick (09 schedule); reschedule defers are visible facts on the rung ("أُجِّلت بواسطة {actor} — أُعيد جدولتها إلى {time}" / "Deferred by {actor} — rescheduled to {time}"); 400% reflow stacks the rail vertically with identical labels and order (09 verification matrix). **Reduced motion:** all rungs render at their final state simultaneously; stamps collapse to state chips; the cooldown arc renders as its text; the cascade is skipped entirely (06 LadderTrail contract; motion-language §5).

**Mood anchor.** A dispatcher's waybill strip — the conversation's paper trail, stamped in order, never rewritten.

### B2 — The Agreement Stamp (the signature ceremony; original interaction #2, referenced by 03 CP-3.5, 04 IA decision, 05 motion signature, 06 AgreementStamp/WindowPicker, 00 unique feature, 11 §D5/§F)

**Layout + choreography (S4 AGREEMENT SHEET → the order's agreement slot; console and on-call identical; fires ONCE per agreement write, never on re-renders — 10).** The sheet's **WindowPicker** is a day-grouped radio grid (fieldset/legend per day; chips carry day-part label + range + remaining capacity as text: "صباحاً 9–12 · متبقٍ 12" / "Morning 9–12 · 12 left" — 11 §D7) and commits on Enter — deliberately NO confirm dialog: the stamp IS the commit, and reversal is the 7-second undo affordance (P03), the sheet's first tab stop with focus preserved ("خُتم الاتفاق — تراجع (7 ثوانٍ)" / "Agreement stamped — Undo (7s)" — 11 §D5). At commit, the selected window chip travels the short inline distance to the order's agreement slot (the drawer row's agreement column) and **presses in**: scale 1.0→0.97 over ≤80ms (press), scale-back with the 1px border.interactive edge flash over 60ms (land), settle — ≤140ms total, ease-out, NO overshoot, the crisp personality's one and only ceremony (05 cadence verbatim). What remains is the **stamped chip**: window + evidence glyph (the square seal with check notch — semantically RESERVED for confirmed-with-agreement, enforced) + the evidence link ("الدليل: رد على المحاولة {n} ({kind})، {time}" / "Evidence: reply on attempt {n} ({kind}), {time}"). In the same event the window's remaining count decrements everywhere (sheet, queue column, catalog — CP-3.6), the risk score recomputes and the RiskRail wipes to its new band with the delta named (B3), and the toast pair lands ("اعتُمدت النافذة — الخميس 17:00–20:00" + "ينتظر {buyer} المندوب مساء الخميس" / "Window agreed — Thu 17:00–20:00" + "{buyer} is expecting the courier Thursday evening" — 11 §F). Nothing else on screen moves — no confetti, ever (motion-language §2; 02: the Stamp is the one ceremony). The counter-image for this artifact is the missed-delivery door tag (q17: "If you missed your delivery, we'll leave a door tag for you." — https://www.fedex.com/en-us/customer-support/faqs/receiving/tracking-questions/missed-delivery.html): the door tag is the record of a FAILED window; the stamped chip is the record of a kept one — evidence printed at the moment of promise, which is why it must be the calmest 140ms in the product.

**A11y implications.** Assertive-once live-region announcement AT the press moment — 11 §D5 verbatim: "اعتُمدت النافذة — الخميس من 17:00 إلى 20:00، مسجَّلة مع رد الرسالة" / "Window agreed — Thursday 17:00 to 20:00, recorded with the message reply" + order facts — then a polite summary (09 schedule); motion is never the only carrier: the persistent stamped chip and its ledger entry carry the identical fact statically for later review (06 AgreementStamp; a11y-baseline §4); the evidence link opens the dispute chain in place (S11, CP-4.5 — Salem's two-click test); the undo affordance is focus-reachable for its full 7s window; superseded agreements (buyer reschedules) render the chain with both entries visible ("أُعيدت الجدولة — نافذة الجمعة 09:00–12:00 حلّت محل مساء الخميس؛ وكلاهما باقٍ في السجل" — 11 §D5). **Reduced motion:** the chip crossfades into the agreement slot in place; the press collapses to the state chip at identical information; the rail wipe becomes an instant band change with the delta announced politely (05 reduced-motion contract; motion-language §5).

**Mood anchor.** A date-stamp biting into a waybill — the moment intent becomes evidence.

### B3 — The Risk Rail (the scorecard as a readable meter; original interaction #3, referenced by 03 CP-2.3/CP-3.5, 05 tier-1 obligations, 06 RiskRail, 07b computeRiskScore, 11 §D1/§D2)

**Layout proposal (S3 drawer's risk region + the OrderRow's band chip; both shells).** A horizontal three-band meter: low (scores 70–100) | medium (40–69) | high (0–39), reading inline-start → inline-end in BOTH directions — the RISK direction is semantic, not geometric (09 verification matrix): the marker's position maps the score onto the risk axis with 100 points at the inline-start origin and 0 at the inline-end, so band position and the band-label text can never disagree. Band regions are delimited by border.subtle hairlines (decorative only) — band identity is carried by the marker's position plus the label text, never hue (06). The marker is a focusable figure (accent.primary, ≥24px target) whose described-by states score and band in words ("الخطورة منخفضة: 82" / "Risk low: 82" — 11 §D2). The **drill** (Enter on the figure — a disclosure with aria-expanded) decomposes the score into its six component rows — buyer history, value band, confirmation latency, channel engagement, address quality, buyer tenure — each rendering raw value → normalized → weight → points as visible text (the 07c §2.1 math as rows, composed from the 11 §D1 chip templates: "سجل المشتري: 12 طلباً مكتمل، 0 مرفوض خلال 180 يوماً" / "Buyer history: 12 delivered, 0 refused in 180 days"), and closing with the engine-provenance sentence so the floor is always named ("التقييم بجدول القواعد — كل نقطة من المكونات الستة أعلاه؛ السياسة الإصدار {version}." / "Scored by the rule scorecard — every point is the six components above; policy v{version}." — 11 §D2). Provisional scores (no response yet) carry the notice-toned "مؤقتة" / "provisional" tag (07c §2.1.3); the over-cap hold card renders the cap math as its own line ("القيمة 640 ريال، السقف 500" / "Value 640, cap 500" — 11 §D7).

**Motion choreography.** On score render or patch: the marker wipes to its position in a single 120ms composited transform (ease-out, arrive-don't-bounce) while the band chip settles simultaneously; when the score CHANGED, the delta is named in text and announced politely once ("ارتفعت 18 نقطة بعد الاتفاق: رد سريع على درجة الرسالة" family — 11 §D2 — engine-signed: an agreement RAISES points, which LOWERS risk, 07c §2.1; the sentence direction is a determinism guarantee, not a style choice). The drill expands as a disclosure cascade: component rows stagger ≤60ms capped at 6 rows, then simultaneous; rows settle in place with no per-row counters (the numbers are facts, not fireworks). Recompute stays inside the 250ms p95 budget (10) so the loading line ("إعادة حساب درجة الخطورة — الحساب سريع؛ وسيعرض كل جزء سببه" / "Recomputing the risk score — the math is quick; every point will show its reason." — 11 §B) almost never renders — and when it does, it tells the truth about why.

**A11y implications.** Band is position + text + icon, never hue alone (05; the three feedback tokens carry states, not band identity); the marker figure's described-by states score and band in words; the drill is a disclosure with aria-expanded and full keyboard traversal; component rows meet 4.5:1 on raised surfaces (text.primary 11.46:1 — 05 ladder); the delta sentence is polite-once, never per keystroke or per wipe; the queue row's band chip (the rail's collapsed form) announces band + total on row focus without opening the drawer (P19 discipline); 400% reflow stacks the rail vertically with the band order and labels identical. **Reduced motion:** the wipe collapses to an instant marker/band change plus the delta text; drill rows render simultaneously; no counters anywhere (06 RiskRail contract; motion-language §5).

**Mood anchor.** A weighbridge ticket — the number, its parts, and the scale's calibration sheet in one glance.
