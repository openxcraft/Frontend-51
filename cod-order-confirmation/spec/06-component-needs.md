## Icon metaphor ledger (rtl-rules §2 record)

Flip in RTL: send/attempt arrow, next/previous chevrons, external-link arrow, reschedule arrows (directional time-travel metaphor). Never flip: clock (countdowns), phone (call leg), stamp (seal/agreement — physical object metaphor), window-grid (slots), courier-box (manifest), magnifier (search), scale (risk policy), bell (notifications). Justification: flipped glyphs are directional metaphors; unflipped are physical-object or semantically anchored metaphors — recorded per rtl-rules §2 requirement. Icon construction: outline-geometric, 24px grid, 1.75px stroke, currentColor, single tone (05).
