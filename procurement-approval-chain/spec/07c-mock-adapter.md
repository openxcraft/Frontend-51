## Deterministic computation rules (EXACT — formulas, not vibes)

1. **Money & arithmetic**: all amounts are integer minor units; line total = quantity × unitPriceMinor (quantities are positive integers); header total = Σ line totals; deltas, projections, and thresholds are integer subtraction/addition; deltaPercent = round-half-up(deltaMinor × 10,000 ÷ lowestTotalMinor) ÷ 100. No floating-point anywhere; display formatting (separators, currency naming per locale) is render-only.
2. **Matrix authoring validation + versioning**: first band fromMinor = 0; bands ascending and contiguous (band[n+1].from = band[n].to + 1); terminal band toMinor = null. A gap (including a finite highest band with no unbounded terminal) → VALIDATION naming the gap's range; an overlap → VALIDATION naming the shared range; duplicate exact scope → VALIDATION naming both rules. Every save appends a matrix version; in-flight chains complete under their pinned version; new submissions route under the latest (03 CP-4 edge).
3. **Routing resolution**: scope precedence category > cost-center > workspace-default (exactly one rule fires — duplicate scopes are refused at authoring); the band containing the header total selects the ordered chain; the decision records ruleId, band bounds, matrixVersion, computedAt. Submission requires ≥ 1 line (VALIDATION otherwise); a zero-line draft previews band 1.
4. **Decider eligibility & self-approval**: per step, candidates = position assignees ∪ members with a delegation window on the position active at submission; eligible = candidates minus the requester; the eligible set is FROZEN into the chain at submission (audit-clean: the record shows who could sign). Any step with empty eligible at submission → VALIDATION naming the position — "has no assignee" when the position carries none, "self-approval blocked" when the requester is the only candidate (03 CP-1 failure, CP-2 edge). Delegate decisions validate the window still covers the decision instant (else PERMISSION naming the expired window) and record both identities + the window (CP-2.5).
5. **Budget projection**: envelope match = same (costCenterId, categoryId) AND period containing neededBy; if several match, the latest-starting wins (declared tie-break). committed = Σ totals of requests in in-review|approved resolving to the envelope, excluding the projected request; projected = committed + total. States: over iff projected > allocated; approaching iff projected ≥ floor(allocated × nearLimitPercent ÷ 100) and not over; within otherwise; no match → the advisory finding "no envelope mapped for {cc} × {cat} in FY{year of neededBy}", recorded, never blocking. Envelope reads compute the same states on committed alone. Blocking + over at submit → VALIDATION naming the envelope and breach = projected − allocated. Snapshots pin per chain version; envelope edits never rewrite them.
6. **Quote comparison**: minimum = min(quote totals); every quote at the minimum is lowest (ties allowed); display order = totalMinor ascending, ties by earliest receivedAt, ties by vendorId lexical. Non-lowest rows carry deltaMinor = total − minimum and deltaPercent per rule 1; the selected quote is "not the lowest" iff its total > minimum (a tie selection is lowest — no justification gate). Seed assertions: PR-1037's two 6,900.00 quotes both render lowest, delta 0.00, manara ordered before bunya; the PR-1041 demo quotes compute deltas 250.00 (2.12%) and 1,100.00 (9.32%).
7. **Quote-count policy**: the gate applies iff total ≥ quotesRequiredThresholdMinor AND (no soleSourceJustification OR soleSourceExceptionEnabled = false). Attached < minimumQuoteCount → VALIDATION: "needs at least 3 quotes above the 25,000.00 threshold — 2 are attached; add a quote, or record a sole-source justification". Sole-source is recorded on the request, in the snapshot, and in the activity log. Below the threshold any count ≥ 1 proceeds (single quotes allowed and recorded — PR-1035).
8. **Selection & justification**: exactly one selected quote with ≥ 1 attached quote is required at submit (the quote evidence is the approval package's spine); a selected total above the minimum requires a non-empty justification at selectQuote AND at submit (VALIDATION names the rule and the delta); selection may change while drafting; ties need no justification (rule 6).
9. **Chain stepping & decisions**: submit → status in-review, step 1 awaiting (frozen eligible set), rest queued; approve → step approved, next awaiting; final approve → status approved and the commitment derives (The Chain Closes, 05); return → active step returned, later steps stay queued (not reached), status returned, commitment released; reject → status rejected, same release; withdraw → requester-only, in-review, zero decisions on the current chain version (else VALIDATION naming the status), status withdrawn; cancelApprovedRequest → requester or admin/owner on approved only, reason required, status cancelled-after-approval, activity detail logs released amount and remaining committed (03 CP-3.5); double-cancel → CONFLICT. Notes are required on return and reject decisions.
10. **Chain versioning & commitment recompute**: resubmit requires status returned|rejected (the same loop either way, 03 CP-3 edge) and a changeNote; edit operations (updateRequest, addQuote, updateQuote, removeQuote, selectQuote) are legal while draft|returned|rejected; resubmission computes chain v(n+1) under the CURRENT matrix (band may move — the 8,200 → 54,000 edge yields more steps); prior versions immutable; every envelope read recomputes commitments from live statuses — nothing is stored stale.
11. **Idempotency & concurrency**: submit/resubmit/decide/withdraw/cancel/exports replay their idempotency keys to the original result; stale baseRevision / baseMatrixVersion / baseVersion / pack baseVersion → CONFLICT carrying both payloads; decide on a superseded chain version → CONFLICT naming the newer version; a decide-vs-withdraw race → first write wins, the loser sees CONFLICT with the newer state.
12. **Clock, codes, artifacts, activity**: the fixed clock is the only "now"; request codes are "PR-" + the counter (seeded 1041, incremented per createDraftRequest, never reused); quote receivedAt defaults to the clock; artifact expiry = 7 days from job success with renewal surfaced inline (06 JobRow); every consequential mutation appends exactly one activity entry in chronological order.
