## Typography (P16 — Scheherazade New + Libre Caslon Text)

- **Arabic: Scheherazade New** (real weights 400/500/600/700) — the textbook Naskh of classical Arabic print: formal weight, generous ascenders/descenders, the script of registers and letters of authority. Body at 16px+ equivalents; **line-height 1.8** (family-specific raise above the shared 1.6 floor — Scheherazade's deep vertical metrics clip stacked marks at 1.6; the raise satisfies the ≥1.6 token obligation and is recorded here as the binding value); tracking 0 everywhere for Arabic (token-level, rtl-rules §3); 500 for field labels and band labels, 700 for the H1-class request code and seal text.
- **Latin: Libre Caslon Text** (real weights 400 + italic 400, 700) — bookish Caslon authority for the money and the metadata: 400 body and amounts, 700 the single H1 per screen and status seal text. No 500/600 exist — no synthetic weights (rtl-rules §3); emphasis re-binds to 700, hierarchy to size and the honey-deep accent.
- **Numerals: Western** (ledger row; data/finance surface per design-language-map §7): amounts, thresholds, percentages, dates, counts all 0-9; storage canonical, display formatted. Libre Caslon Text does not ship a reliable tabular-figures feature — arithmetic legibility is carried by the **alignment rule** instead: amount columns align inline-end with fixed decimal presentation (2 decimals, thousands separators per locale format), declared here as the binding compensation (no third family at T3, no mono substitution — the CLM/seo precedent of adopting the instinct as a rule, not a font).
- Worst-case joined-word truncation at word boundaries only (rtl-rules §4); bilingual pairs (request titles, line descriptions, vendor names, position titles) render per-side direction in paired fields (P23 column logic applied to metadata fields, not authoring columns — this is a bilingual-en product whose *data* is bilingual, its chrome directional).
