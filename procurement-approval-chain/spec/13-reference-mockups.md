## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

**The Wax Ledger.** Tafwid Honey over warm butter paper: the workspace reads as a bound register of authorities, not a dashboard. The request is a *document* — a paper-shaped sheet on the canvas stratum with a hairline honey border — that grows a chain down its inline edge: the approval-chain rail is not a widget beside the document, it is the document's own margin, links filling with seal-wax honey as authority descends. The routing preview is the same margin rendered in outline while the ink is still wet — the requester reads exactly the rail the approver will later act on, which is the product's whole argument made physical. Threshold bands render as ruled table rows out of the Manchester grammar: a label, an interval in Western numerals, an ordered list of positions — never a graph, never a funnel. The quote comparison is a ledger table with the lowest row carrying a small wax-dot marker and the word "الأدنى / Lowest", deltas in plain arithmetic to its right, every figure inline-end aligned at fixed two decimals. The envelope bar is the one instrument-gauge surface: honey filling toward honey-deep at the near-limit line and red past the allocation — always with the numbers beside it in text, because a ledger does not whisper. The seal is the single circular moment (a stamp, not a card): it appears once, on final approval, weighted and slow, and the commitment figure settles beneath it in a three-step stagger. Two scripts are inscribed as paired fields throughout — Arabic and English side by side like a bilingual deed, each at its own direction, never one translated into the other. Nothing sparkles, nothing pulses, nothing counts up: money settles, authority descends, and the register keeps both.
