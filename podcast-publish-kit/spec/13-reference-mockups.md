## Curated references

1. **Descript — transcript-as-document workbench** — https://www.descript.com · show-notes tool: https://www.descript.com/tools/podcast-show-notes-generator
   Take: the transcript-centric mental model buyers already carry (text as the primary editing surface, audio as reference); the calm density of a doc-like pane beside structure surfaces. Not taken: the editing-suite scope (we exclude audio editing — LOOP-10); black-box generation posture for notes; their per-seat+media-hours pricing shape.

2. **Podcast Chapters (macOS) + MP3 Chapters (mp3chapters.github.io)** — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/podcast-chapters/id1070963477 · https://mp3chapters.github.io
   Take: the narrow-tool clarity — a chapter marker's UI can be ONE timeline and a list, and local-only processing (never uploading the audio) is a privacy posture worth keeping verbatim. Not taken: timecode-typing and waveform-scrubbing as the primary marking act (our segments replace the scrub); monolingual title fields; file-tag-tool ceiling (no notes, no package).

3. **Castmagic — publishing-layer generation funnel** — https://www.castmagic.io · pricing/transcription page: https://www.castmagic.io/tools/podcast-transcription
   Take: the category's proven promise shape ("upload finished audio → publishing layer"), which validates demand for exactly our slice; their artifact checklist (notes, summaries, highlights, timestamps) as a notes-structure sanity list. Not taken: generation as the product (suggestions here are drafts with rationale, never auto-applied — P15); monolingual output; unanchored timestamps that can drift from content.

## Original direction (prose sketch — identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

A producer's bench at golden hour: warm paper surfaces, olive chapter blocks laid along a mono timecode ruler like marks on a tape log, and one clay playhead crawling the line while the transcript scrolls in step. The three zones read as one continuous act — read the transcript, bracket the chapters, write the notes — with the checklist drawer as the bench's edge where tools hang ready. Bilingual columns sit side by side like a carbon-copy pad, alignment links drawn between corresponding blocks; parity warnings arrive as small honest stamps, never alarms. When a suggestion set arrives, it settles into a reserved shelf below the ruler — each card carrying its rationale in plain language, accepted only by an explicit hand. And when the package seals, the single pop of the card — one controlled overshoot, once — says what an hour of retyping used to say. Then back to work.

## Mood anchors

Olive ink on a warm bench · mono timecodes as the only strict geometry · the playhead is the one hot color · transcripts are ground truth, work objects lift · one pop when the package seals.
