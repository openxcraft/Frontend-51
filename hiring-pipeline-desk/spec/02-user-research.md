# 02 — User Research: hiring-pipeline-desk

SME-B pass · Paraphrased sentiment only, marked (paraphrase) — zero verbatim quotes; personas synthesized from the research digest's practitioner sources (research/batch5/hpd-q1…q10) and portfolio-adjacent buyer patterns. Personas use regional names; no real individuals are represented.

## Persona 1 — Hessa, In-House Recruiter (primary daily user + buyer)

**Role**: sole recruiter at a 220-person Riyadh trading-and-logistics group; runs 9–12 open roles at a time (Arabic-fluent customer-facing roles, warehouse supervisors, accountants, junior analysts); screens CVs by hand, schedules interviews by message threads, and personally answers the ops director when a hire goes wrong. Works in English-first tools but reads and conducts half her interviews in Arabic.
**JTBD**: "When a hiring manager says a candidate bombed the interview, I want to show what was asked, what was rated, and by whom — so the argument is about evidence, not memory."
**Triggers**: a department head overturning a recruiter decision with "I just felt he was stronger" (the unstructured-interview failure class, digest q3); three interviewers asking the same candidate three different question sets and disagreeing about the result; a rejected candidate's discrimination complaint that her spreadsheet cannot answer (the audit gap — activity-log as governance artifact, 00); an Arabic-only CV whose name her tracker mangles into two records (digest q10 — name handling).
**Objections + responses**: "We might buy a real ATS eventually — why a desk?" → the real ATS sells distribution, CRM, and analytics she is forbidden to buy here; the interview layer is what she lacks today and the sidecar survives whatever platform arrives (01 positioning). "Bias nudges sound like a compliance lecture" → nudges are three deterministic rules that fire on her own team's scorecards, never on candidates, and never block a decision (07c — she can dismiss one with a reason). "My hiring managers will never fill scorecards" → included seats, a phone-sized card with 4–6 ratings and one recommendation, and the debrief that refuses to open until cards are in — the social contract the product enforces for her.
**Pains**: interview notes living in personal notebooks and chat threads (paraphrase of tool-hopping sentiment, digest q1/q9); no record of *which language* an interview ran in or whether the Arabic questions even existed; decision meetings that re-litigate ratings from memory.

## Persona 2 — Tarek, Hiring Manager (department head)

**Role**: head of customer operations, 30 seats under him, bilingual team; requests hires, sits in final interviews, and owns the decision on paper. English-first in tools, Arabic-native in the room — he interviews in whichever language the candidate is stronger in.
**JTBD**: "When I decide on a candidate, I want my interviewers' judgments already collected and compared — so my decision meeting is 15 minutes, not a scheduling war."
**Triggers**: final-round debriefs where two interviewers contradict each other and nobody can cite evidence; a strong CV candidate who interviewed poorly in English but was never asked in Arabic (the bilingual-gap failure his team hits monthly); being shown a "score" by a previous tool he could not decompose (the opaque-scoring distrust class, digest q5 — Upturn posture).
**Objections + responses**: "Another tool to check daily?" → his surface is the board's stage view, the debrief console, and the decision record — three screens, all read-mostly (04). "Why can't I just trust my gut on finals?" → he can: the product records his rationale, it never overrides it — but his interviewers' sealed cards land beside it, so the record shows both (02 voice: evidence-first, never judgment-replacing). "What do these numbers mean?" → every rating is an anchored 1–4 with named levels and the interviewer's own words attached — means and divergence are arithmetic on cards he can open (07c rules).
**Pains**: recruiting queues that stall silently between stages; rejecting a candidate and being unable to say why six weeks later; interviewers who ask their favorite questions instead of the kit's (structured-drift, digest q3).

## Persona 3 — Dalal, Interviewer (senior specialist, the scorecard citizen)

**Role**: senior customer-success specialist, Arabic-dominant, strong English; conducts 3–5 interviews a month as a duty, not a job. She wants to do the work well between meetings — on her phone — and to be treated fairly when her read of a candidate differs from a peer's.
**JTBD**: "When I finish an interview, I want to record my ratings in ten minutes with the questions in front of me in the language we spoke — so my judgment is captured before anyone else's can color it."
**Triggers**: being handed a candidate CV and no questions (unstructured drift); being asked to fill a form after she has already heard what a peer said about the candidate (the independence violation, digest q3 — Bohnet); rating forms with "excellent/good/fair" labels that mean nothing (digest q4 — anchored-scale finding); being asked for "a quick number" with no place to write why.
**Objections + responses**: "I'm not filling a ten-tab form" → one card: 4–6 anchored ratings, one evidence line each, one recommendation — phone-complete in ~10 minutes (04 S5). "What if I disagree with the nudge?" → the nudge is a mark on her card, dismissible with a reason, and the debrief shows the disagreement by name rather than averaging it away (07c rule N3). "My Arabic is stronger than my English — the form fights me" → the card renders in her locale with the kit's questions in the interview's language, and quoted candidate answers in the other script are bidi-isolated, never mangled (05/09).
**Pains**: typing interview notes in mixed scripts that break in LTR-only forms (digest q6/q10 — RTL corruption class); not knowing whether her card even reached the decision; peer pressure arriving before she has submitted (the seal exists for her).

## Persona 4 — Yasmin, Talent & HR Lead (group-scale buyer, audit-facing)

**Role**: HR director at a 400-person group (Riyadh HQ + Dubai branch), two recruiters under her; owns hiring governance, answers internal audit and ministry-adjacent queries, and is watching the 2026 Nitaqat phase ratchet up both hiring volume and scrutiny (digest q7).
**JTBD**: "When audit or leadership asks how a hiring decision was made, I want to export the opening's full trail — moves, interviews, sealed scorecards, nudges, decision rationale — so the answer is an artifact, not an afternoon of reconstruction."
**Triggers**: an audit request about a disputed termination-on-probation that traces back to the hire decision; recruiters leaving and their spreadsheets leaving with them (institutional memory); the 2026 quota tightening meaning more roles, more Saudi-national-first pipelines, and more decisions that must show their work (digest q7 — consulting-sourced, honesty-flagged).
**Objections + responses**: "Is this an ATS? We're evaluating platforms" → it is the interview-decision desk; it coexists with whatever funnel platform arrives, and its export artifact is portable either way (01 pricing read). "Bias nudges — do they make us liable?" → nudges are deterministic rules shown in-product with their formulas disclosed (07c); they document that the process looked for bias, which is the defensible posture, and they never auto-reject or score anyone (00 exclusions). "Will it track Saudization compliance?" → no — nationality-quota compliance is workforce-systems territory with its own data; recorded as a deliberate non-goal with a text-level handoff only (12 Q-13).
**Pains**: no audit trail around hiring decisions at group scale; inconsistent interview practice between her two recruiters and 30+ interviewers; evidence requests that take days to reconstruct from chat threads.

## Voice derivation (SME-J)

Recruiter-desk voice: **warm, brisk, evidence-first** — states what was asked, what was rated, and what the rules marked, never editorializes about candidates ("Rated 2 — evidence attached", not "unfortunately weak"). The warmth lives in success and empty-state copy (an energetic product for a human process); errors and refusals are plain and factual. Arabic register فصحى بيضاء (utility precision); English professional-neutral, contractions off (governance-adjacent family). Fixed terminology (locked across 07a/11; one concept, one term, everywhere — copy-voice §3): وظيفة شاغرة=job opening · مرشح=candidate · طلب التقديم=application · مرحلة=stage · لوح المسار=pipeline board · دليل المقابلة=interview kit · جدارة=competency · سؤال=question · مقابلة=interview · بطاقة التقييم=scorecard · توصية=recommendation · قرار التوظيف=hiring decision · تنبيه الانحياز=bias nudge · مناقشة التقييمات=debrief · ختم الاستقلالية=independence seal · سبب الرفض=rejection reason · سجل النشاط=activity log · تصدير=export. Numbers Western per ledger row (ratings, counts, dates — a data surface, design-language-map §7); Arabic strings may run ~125% of English length — layout budgets for it (10) and the 11 matrix lists both strings verbatim (copy-voice §5).
