# 09 — Accessibility: hiring-pipeline-desk

SME-F pass · WCAG 2.2 AA floor (a11y-baseline), both directions, 1280px and 360px viewports, 200%/400% zoom. The product's structural a11y problems are its identity: **a drag-and-drop pipeline board whose every move needs a full keyboard path** (2.5.7), **bilingual candidate names that must render at their own direction inside either chrome**, and **verdict/nudge states that must never ride on color or motion alone** — solved below as the board focus contract and the name-pair discipline. The design-center user is Dalal (02): an interviewer who fills scorecards on a phone between meetings, in Arabic, with mixed-script evidence — the scorecard path is the a11y path.

## Focus order & the board focus contract

Reading order in the desk chrome is the workspace's active direction (LTR default, fully mirrored on locale flip — 05 direction model). Focus follows the chrome's reading order while *content* renders at its own direction: a candidate card in the LTR board whose display name is Arabic renders the name RTL, bidi-isolated, inside the card's LTR flow — focus does not reorder the name pair, and the card's single focus target announces `displayName + stage + tenure + next-interview (language, time) + recommendation tally when present` so a screen-reader user hears the card's meaning before interacting. Anonymized-screening rows announce exactly what sighted users see ("Candidate #3 — in Applied 2 days") — the masking is a display discipline announced as such, never a silent substitution. The board's column order mirrors in RTL with the physical arrow mapping held stable (rtl-rules §4); drag-and-drop is never the only path to any move (2.5.7 — Alt+M is the contract).

## Per-surface WCAG 2.2 AA checklist

- **S2 pipeline board (the dense one)**: cards ≥68px tall with ≥24×24 targets on every control (2.5.8); verdict/state chips are icon + text + color, never color alone (05 palette contracts — coral is never a verdict); stage tenure and tallies in tabular figures as text; name pairs bidi-isolated per side (rtl-rules §2); truncation at word boundaries with worst-case Arabic budgeted ("مستشارو التدقيق والحوكمة" — rtl-rules §4); >200 cards per column virtualizes with focus preservation (P19); drag has the Alt+M complete equivalent (2.5.7); sticky column headers clear focused cards (2.4.11); the bulk action bar names its count and scopes every action (P07).
- **S3 application detail**: timeline as a focusable list with actor + timestamp per entry; rejection reason is on the record and announced as text (never a red-only mark); notes render bidi-isolated (user content).
- **S4 kit studio**: bilingual inputs each at their own direction with persistent labels beside the fields — never ghost text inside them (a11y-baseline §5); weight-sum drift announced on blur; coverage meters text+bar; proposal rows carry origin badges as text ("from the template bank — not generated"); the assist panel's reserved region is sized before arrival (no CLS on stream, 10).
- **S5 interview + scorecard**: rating controls are radio groups inside fieldsets (competency = legend, anchors = labels — real radios, never div-roles; the skill's semantic-HTML row adopted per 05); evidence textareas `dir="auto"` with described association to their competency; recommendation control is a radio group; mobile-first single column at ≤767px (the between-meetings scenario); nudge chips text-first; submit button keeps its label while showing state (never a spinner-only button).
- **S6 debrief console**: sealed rows are status text ("Sealed — Dalal has not submitted") with the teal accent + eye-off glyph, never hue alone; means and tallies in tabular figures; the ratings grid is arrow-navigable (P18) with cell evidence on Enter; divergence marks carry the spread in words ("4 vs 2 — Dalal / Tarek") plus jump links; the decision gate refusal names the missing card in text.
- **S7 candidates directory**: name-pair display per side; duplicate warnings name the field that matched; virtualized >200 with focus preservation; autocomplete tokens on name/email fields (a11y-baseline §5) and prior-answer recall across the intake form (3.3.7).
- **S8 interviews list**: language chips are text tokens ("العربية" / "English" / "bilingual") — direction-free by construction (06); "mine" vs "all" scoping is a labeled control; upcoming times in text with dual-calendar display on hover/focus (P24).
- **S9 jobs center**: determinate progress in written copy; cancel/retry as persistent row buttons; artifact expiry in text.
- **S10 activity + settings**: the log is keyboard-navigable and filterable (P17); the anonymized-screening toggle carries its disclosure text adjacent (evidence + limits, 08); consents render in capability language (credential-contract §2); usage figures tabular.
- **All surfaces**: 400% reflow without one-dimensional horizontal scroll — the board collapses to stacked per-stage lists at 320px (mobile S2 read mode, 04), the ratings grid collapses to per-interviewer cards, the kit studio's bilingual question lists stack vertically; 200% zoom on 1280px keeps all targets ≥24px; every image-free icon has a text label or is decorative-marked.

## Reduced-motion contract (motion-language §5)

Route crossfades → hard cuts; **The Cards Unseal** (the T2 signature, 05) → sealed rows render simultaneously in their revealed state, means and tally appear instantly, and the announcement carries the identical full summary ("All scorecards in — 2 interviewers, 4 competencies, recommendation split 1 yes / 1 strong-no"); the unseal's soft-lift step becomes an instant state change with the same announcement; board card moves settle instantly in place (the 180ms settle collapses to a position change + the move announcement); the tally-numeral overshoot collapses to a state chip (motion-language §5); nudge chips arrive and dissolve instantly with their text equivalents announced; the assist's streaming text renders in paragraph chunks on completion rather than token flow (ai-native-patterns §2, announced per chunk completion politely); press feedback → a single opacity step ≤2. Every state change remains perceivable by construction — the announcement schedule is the reduced-motion equivalent of every animated transition, and no information ever travels by motion alone (a11y-baseline §4).
