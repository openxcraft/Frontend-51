## Icon-flip decisions (rtl-rules §2, recorded per rule)

Stage-move chevrons and board navigation arrows: directional — flip. Nudge "address" arrow (re-open evidence): directional — flips. External-link arrow on candidate links: flips. Send (interview cancel-notify actions): directional — flips. Eye-off (anonymized screening / sealed cards): semantically anchored — never flips. Users/user-plus/calendar/list-checks/layers/flag/trending-up/x-circle/languages (interview-language glyph): symmetric or semantically anchored — never flip. The 'ar'/'en' language chips are text tokens ("العربية" / "English") rendered at their own script direction inside a symmetric pill — direction-free by construction.
