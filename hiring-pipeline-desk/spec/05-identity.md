## Shape, elevation, iconography, motion

- **Radius D (pill, 20px+ / fully-round controls)**: stage cards 20px; candidate cards 24px; buttons and segmented controls fully-round; competency wells 20px; nudge chips fully-round (pill chips are the family norm here, not the P08 exception they are in radius-B products — declared deviation from the sibling convention, recorded). Asymmetric radii use logical `radius.inline-start/end` tokens (token-architecture §2.4).
- **Elevation: soft-lift** — the diffuse ambient step paper→raised plus one soft shadow on raised; the board columns sit in the well (one step down); the unseal moment lifts the revealed cards one step (the only elevation change tied to motion in the product, and it carries a non-motion equivalent in the announcement, 09).
- **Icons: filled-rounded** (solid friendly glyphs on a 2px-optical grid, Lucide-vocabulary shapes re-drawn filled with rounded terminals — the skill's outline rows are the shape reference, the fill is the ledger): user/users/user-plus (candidate/interviewer actions), calendar (interviews), list-checks (scorecard), layers (kit), flag (nudge), trending-up (advance), x-circle (reject — slate, never coral), eye-off (anonymized screening), languages (interview language). No sparkle glyphs anywhere — the assist panel uses a draft-document glyph, not a magic wand (ai-native-patterns §1 discipline: the one AI surface is fenced by copy and iconography, not decorated).
- **Motion: energetic** (motion-language §1): 150–250ms, standard easing with a single controlled overshoot (≤8% travel) reserved for ONE success moment. Board card moves settle in place (180ms settle, no bounce on rejection paths — errors arrive, they never bounce, motion-language §2); nudge chips arrive with a 160ms pop-fade and dissolve on resolution (140ms) with live-region text equivalents; route changes crossfade ≤180ms; the assist panel's proposals render into a reserved region (P13) — no layout shift. Reduced-motion: hard cuts, instant state chips, full announcements (09 §reduced-motion).
- **T2 signature micro-interaction (exactly one — "The Cards Unseal")**: when the last outstanding scorecard of a round submits, the debrief's sealed stack flips open — sealed rows rotate/slide into their revealed state in a ≤60ms stagger capped at 5 rows, the weighted means settle beneath (single 4% overshoot on the tally numerals, the product's one energetic flourish), and the live region announces the full summary ("All scorecards in — 2 interviewers, 4 competencies, recommendation split 1 yes / 1 strong-no"). It is the product's single most important moment: the independence seal opening IS the structured-interview method completing (03 CP-4.2). Reduced-motion: rows render simultaneously, means appear instantly, the announcement carries the identical summary — the information is never motion-only (a11y-baseline §4; motion-language §5). Nothing else in the product expresses personality beyond the energetic standard set.
