# 08 — Integration Map: hiring-pipeline-desk

AI declaration (ai-native-patterns §1): **conditionally-AI** — exactly one capability may touch an AI: the **interview-question drafting assist** (N1), whose every proposal lands behind a per-question review-and-confirm gate (P15) and whose absence leaves the deterministic **template bank** as the panel's native content. Every core workflow — openings, bilingual kit authoring by hand, candidate intake by paste, stage moves, scheduling with the coverage gate, scorecards, nudges, the debrief, decisions, the activity log, exports — completes with every capability unbound; the 00 success signals test exactly that state against the Mock Adapter's all-off configuration (07c: `capabilityBound: false` is the tested default). Architecture speaks only in abstract capabilities; UI copy says kind, not vendor (credential-contract §2). Need numbering is fixed by the earlier files: **N1 assist · N2 export delivery channel · N3 notification channel · N4 candidate-intake parsing capability** (00, 03 CP-3.4/CP-5.2, 07b rule 6).

## Abstract needs (4 — N1 optional-AI, N2–N4 optional; manual alternatives per LOOP-5)

| # | Abstract need | Why it exists | Consent semantics (credential-contract §4) | Manual alternative (always first-class) |
|---|---------------|---------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| N1 | **Interview-question drafting assist** — "proposes kit questions in the requested language from the competency and seniority you name" | Drafting 8–12 anchored behavioral questions per competency, in two languages, is the kit's authoring cost; an assist shortens it without ever owning it | Grant displayed as: "Reads the competency name, seniority, and language you choose in this workspace and proposes interview questions — every proposal waits for your accept, edit, or dismiss" — read/draft class only; per ai-native-patterns §4, consumption surfaces before and after runs (estimated + actual assist runs per window, P14); the quota meter lives in Settings and in the panel itself | **The template bank + typing**: a deterministic competency-class × seniority × language matrix of vetted bilingual questions (07c rule 2 — 3 questions per cell, fixed order, zero quota) plus hand-typed questions — the panel's default state, fully functional, honestly labeled "from the template bank — not generated" on every row |
| N2 | **Export delivery channel** — "files opening-package artifacts to a destination" | Yasmin-class buyers file the audit artifact into a document vault the auditors already read, rather than downloading | Grant: "Writes export package files on behalf of the workspace" — write class scoped to export artifacts only | **Download the artifact** — the default handoff (03 CP-5.2); regenerable for 7 days, deterministic forever (07c: identical state yields the identical package) |
| N3 | **Notification channel** — "notifies members outside the app" | Interview reminders, cancel notices, and debrief-readiness must reach interviewers who open the product twice a month, not twice a day (02 Dalal) | Grant: "Sends notifications about your interviews, cancellations, and debrief readiness to workspace members" — message class only, per P21 tier control (instant for cancel/reschedule, digest for reminders and readiness) | **In-app notification center + the S8 "mine" list + the weekly interviews digest on entry** (async-contract §6.3 return state) — nothing is ever only in email; the scorecard deadline a Dalal-class seat actually feels is the one on her own interviews list |
| N4 | **Candidate-intake parsing capability** — "reads candidate documents you supply and proposes intake rows" | A recruiter with 40 CVs in a mailbox types slower than the backlog grows | Grant: "Reads candidate documents you choose to import in this workspace and proposes candidate rows — proposed rows wait for your confirmation" — read/propose class only; per P15, no parsed row ever writes without a confirm step | **Manual entry + paste-import** — the v1 primary path (00): one candidate per form, or `name-latin | name-arabic | email | phone | source` rows pasted as an async Job with named invalid rows and named duplicates (07c rule 11); Arabic parsing quality is unbenchmarked (01 honest gap #5), which is exactly why the manual path stays first |

## Capability binding gates (LOOP-7 posture — evaluated at binding time, not now)

- **N1 bilingual question quality**: a fixed bilingual evaluation set must pass before the assist ships active — the set pairs every competency-class × seniority cell with (a) the register the kit expects (فصحى بيضاء-class MSA for AR questions, professional-neutral EN), (b) behavioral-past-tense phrasing ("describe a time…", not "how would you…"), and (c) anchors compatible with the 1–4 scale. A proposal that ships generic trait-shaped questions ("Are you a team player?") would poison the very structure the kit exists to enforce (01: the same questions, asked of every candidate, are the mechanism); every proposal remains confirm-gated regardless, so the worst failure mode is recruiter time, never corrupted state. Cost transparency (ai-native-patterns §4) and the quota ladder (§5) are non-negotiable at activation. Assist-mode refusal or exhaustion MUST return the template bank in the same response with an explicit degraded notice — the ladder bottoms out at full authoring function, never a wall (07c `proposeKitQuestions`).
- **N2 delivery fidelity**: artifact integrity at destination — the opening package is an audit artifact; corruption surfaces as typed errors, not silent truncation, and the delivery event appends to the activity log (`export-run`).
- **N3 payload hygiene**: consent-scoped recipients only; notification payloads carry opening title, interview time, language, and the interview's id — never scorecard content, never evidence text, never candidate contact details (data minimization per credential-contract §7; the seal follows the data wherever it goes — a notification must not leak what a sealed card protects).
- **N4 proposal fidelity**: parsed rows land as PROPOSALS with per-field confidence marks, confirm-gated per row; the duplicate-detection contract (normalized email / normalized Arabic name, 07c rule 11) runs on accepted rows exactly as on pasted ones — a parser that re-delivers the same document must not mint phantom candidates. Arabic name-pair fidelity is the specific evaluation: the Latin and Arabic fields must land in their own fields, never one mangled string (01 source 44 — the name-handling failure class).

## Degradation map (ai-native-patterns §8)

| Surface | All capabilities unbound (default v1 state) | Capability failing mid-session |
|---|---|---|
| Openings, board, moves, rejects, filters | **Full function** — deterministic state operations (07b/07c); no capability dependency | Full function |
| Kit authoring (competencies, weights, questions by hand) | **Full function** — typing is the floor; validation and coverage meters are local rules | Full function |
| Question assist panel | **Template bank**: deterministic rows per competency-class × seniority × language, origin `template`, labeled "from the template bank — not generated"; the consent surface renders as optional, never a wall | Assist failure mid-run → proposals-so-far retained and visibly marked `partial` (async-contract §4); stop/regenerate honored; template bank one click away in the same panel |
| Candidate intake | **Manual entry + paste-import** — the tested primary | N4 failure → the document route disappears from the import dialog; paste and manual entry unchanged |
| Interview scheduling, coverage gate, scorecards, nudges, debrief, decisions | **Full function** — every one of these is deterministic arithmetic over declared state (07c rules 1–12); none is ever metered | Full function |
| Notifications | In-app center + S8 "mine" list + digest-on-entry | N3 failure → in-app surfaces unchanged; out-of-app delivery pauses with the failure surfaced where the capability is used (credential-contract §3), never silently dropped |
| Exports & artifacts | Download/copy, 7-day expiry, deterministic regeneration | N2 failure → per-format retry + manual download of what delivered |

Quota ladder (ai-native-patterns §5) applies to the metered assist runs only (P14, 07b `getUsage`): rung 2 reduces the proposal count with an explicit notice; rung 3 falls to template-bank-only — which is the default state anyway, so the ladder bottoms out at full authoring function. The template bank itself is never metered and never degrades. Exports are softly metered (03 CP-5.2) with the same never-blocking posture: a refused export still regenerates after reset from unchanged state. Scorecards, nudges, moves, and decisions are never metered (07b rule 9).

## Risk flags for the owner

- **N1 is the product's only AI surface and its most dangerous dependency**: the honest posture (template floor + per-question confirm gate + visible origin badges) is already structural, but binding-time evaluation must specifically cover Arabic question register — an assist that drafts English-shaped questions transliterated into Arabic would quietly undo the bilingual kit's value (01 honest gap #2: no MENA-specific structured-interview research exists; the evaluation set is our substitute for evidence we cannot cite).
- **N3 is the interviewer-compliance lever**: without it, scorecard submission depends on interviewers checking the app unprompted; the in-app "mine" list and digest keep the product honest but slower. The notification payloads' seal-discipline (above) is the binding gate that matters most.
- **N4 is deliberately last**: the regional incumbents' engine (CV parsing + distribution, 01 sources 33–37) is the two-function posture this product counter-positions against; a parsing capability is offered for backlog scale only, never as the intake story, and its confirm gate means a parsed row has the same human provenance as a typed one.
- **Anonymized screening is a capability-free feature with a declared evidence position**: the toggle (workspace-level, Applied/Screening display discipline, 07c rule A5) is supported by the blind-recruitment literature's direction (Vivek et al.: "Blind recruitment is an effective method for reducing bias in the recruitment and selection process", research digest source 45) and bounded by its named limit (anonymous material "leaks identity through language", source 46 — especially sharp for Arabic evidence prose); the in-product disclosure beside the toggle states both, and the export's include-names toggle states that the audit artifact defaults to names included (03 CP-5 edge).
- The full-funnel ATS the company may already run (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby class, 01) is deliberately NOT an integration need at v1: this desk holds the interview layer; handoff to any surrounding platform is the export artifact and the bilingual posting block copied out by hand (00 LOOP-10). A future two-way bridge (application-state sync with an external ATS) would be a new LOOP-4 proposal and a new capability class, never a silent addition.
