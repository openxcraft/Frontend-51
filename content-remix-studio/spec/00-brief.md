# 00 — Brief: content-remix-studio

Family: content_tools · Tier: 3 · Roster status: researched proposal, pending owner confirmation · Seed: none supplied.

## What it is
A recipe-driven content remix tool: take ONE source asset (a blog post, newsletter issue, transcript, or long social post) and turn it into derivative formats — thread, carousel outline, email teaser, short-video script, community announcement — via recipes that specify structure, length, and tone per format. Bilingual by construction: recipes produce ar and/or en derivatives from either-language sources, with dialect-preservation guardrails that flag (never silently normalize) colloquial source text.

## Buyer
Solo creators and small marketing teams (1–3 seats) in MENA producing bilingual content across platforms: newsletter authors, podcast hosts, course creators, and boutique agency owners. The volume buyer Nour-type (studio 02) is served by the studio family; this is the self-serve tier below it.

## Surface
Web application: source library, recipe gallery, remix workbench (source left, derivative right), derivative library with export. Desktop-first, tablet-friendly workbench; mobile read-only library.

## Unique feature
Recipe-driven remix with dialect guardrails: recipes are inspectable, editable transformation definitions (structure map, length policy, tone notes) — not black-box AI — and every derivative carries a "fidelity receipt" (what was drawn from the source, what was condensed, what the recipe added). Dialect in sources is detected and preserved-or-flagged per the creator's choice; never silently flattened to MSA.

## Exclusions (LOOP-10)
- No scheduling/publishing (calendar family).
- No design/template rendering (carousels are OUTLINES, not designed images; design tools are out of family).
- No video editing (clip family).
- No team collaboration features (solo/small-team scope; sharing is export).
- No AI generation beyond recipe execution (recipes may execute on deterministic or AI-bound runners — declared abstract; manual alternative = recipe checklist the creator follows by hand).

## Success signals
A creator can: add a source; run 3 recipes; review derivatives with fidelity receipts; tweak a recipe; export — in the first session, on the Mock Adapter, zero provider knowledge in UI.
