## Curated references

1. **Repurpose.io** — https://repurpose.io and https://repurpose.io/pricing
   Take: the workflow shape buyers already understand (source → derivative → distribution handoff); the automation promise we counter with inspectability. Not taken: distribution-connection-centric IA (we hand off, not deliver); automation opacity (rigidity complaints — 01).

2. **Lately.ai** — https://www.lately.ai
   Take: AI-repurposing category conventions (long→many posts) that prove the buying behavior. Not taken: black-box generation posture — our recipes/receipts are the explicit counter-position (01 gaps #1–2).

3. **Notion's template/gallery patterns** — https://www.notion.com
   Take: gallery-card conventions for recipe browsing; the clarity bar for structure previews. Not taken: docs-tool chrome (we are a workbench, not a workspace).

## Original proposals (identity-driven, T3-appropriate)

- **Recipe preview as anatomy diagram**: each RecipeCard renders its structureMap as a small anatomy diagram (section blocks with role labels and length ranges) — the creator sees the skeleton the recipe builds before running anything (CP-1.3). Text-first implementation keeps screen-reader parity.
- **Receipt ribbon**: the fidelity receipt rendered as a linked ribbon between source and derivative columns — teal for drawn, amber for recipe-added, plum for condensed, with the ratio printed on each link (CP-2). The ribbon is the product's trust visualization and its most distinctive surface.
